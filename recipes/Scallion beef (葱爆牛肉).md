# Scallion beef (葱爆牛肉)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The beef marinates in a bowl. The stir-fry is in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Marinate the beef first. Cut the scallions while it rests. Stir-fry when the beef looks tacky.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Beef**

- 10 oz flank steak
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the beef
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the beef
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 tsp potato starch
- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the beef

**Scallions**

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry
- 2 tsp ginger, julienned
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 8 oz Chinese scallions (大葱), cut into 2 in lengths
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the stir-fry
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the stir-fry
- 1/4 tsp sugar

## Beef

Use flank steak so the slices soften in this time.

- 10 oz flank steak
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the beef
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the beef
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 tsp potato starch
- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the beef

1. Slice the **flank steak** against the grain, 1/8 in thick and about 2 in long. If the steak bends under the knife, freeze it 15 minutes, then slice.
2. Add the **light soy sauce for the beef**, the **Shaoxing wine for the beef**, the **ground white pepper**, the **potato starch**, and the **peanut oil for the beef**. Stir in one direction until the beef is tacky and no liquid pools in the bowl, about 1 minute.
3. Rest 15 minutes. Go on to the scallions.

## Scallions

Use thick Chinese scallions so the pieces stay crisp.

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry
- 2 tsp ginger, julienned
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 8 oz Chinese scallions (大葱), cut into 2 in lengths
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the stir-fry
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the stir-fry
- 1/4 tsp sugar

1. Julienne the **ginger**. Slice the **garlic**.
2. Trim the **Chinese scallions** and cut them into 2 in lengths. Put the white pieces and the green pieces in two piles.
3. Put the **peanut oil for the stir-fry**, the **light soy sauce for the stir-fry**, the **Shaoxing wine for the stir-fry**, and the **sugar** into their own cups.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Beef**

- 10 oz flank steak, marinated

**Scallions**

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry
- 2 tsp ginger, julienned
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 8 oz Chinese scallions (大葱), cut into 2 in lengths
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the stir-fry
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the stir-fry
- 1/4 tsp sugar

1. If the beef is not tacky yet, wait. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to high. Heat it until a drop of water flicked into the center vanishes, about 5 minutes.
2. Add 1 tbsp of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry**. Swirl it over the bottom.
3. Add half of the **marinated flank steak** in one layer. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 30 seconds. Toss until the red is gone, about 30 seconds more. Scoop it onto a plate. If the oil smokes, turn the coil off and stir until the smoke stops, then turn the coil back to high. When the oil shimmers, keep going.
4. Add the rest of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** and the rest of the **marinated flank steak**. Cook the beef the same way, about 1 minute. Scoop it onto the plate.
5. Turn the coil off. When it stops glowing, turn it to medium. Add the **ginger**, the **garlic**, and the white pieces of the **Chinese scallions** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 30 seconds.
6. Add the green pieces of the **Chinese scallions**. Stir until they turn bright green and still have bite, about 30 seconds.
7. Add the **marinated flank steak**, the **light soy sauce for the stir-fry**, the **Shaoxing wine for the stir-fry**, and the **sugar** together. Toss until the beef is glossy and the scallions still have bite, about 30 seconds.
8. Eat it hot.
