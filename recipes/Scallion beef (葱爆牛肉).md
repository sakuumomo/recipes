# Scallion beef (葱爆牛肉)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The beef marinates in a bowl. Stir-fry on the Mosaic at 500°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Marinate the beef first. While it rests, cut the scallions and set the cups. Stir-fry when the beef looks tacky.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Beef

Use flank steak so the slices soften in this time, and salted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz flank steak
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the beef
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒), for the beef
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 tsp potato starch
- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the beef

1. Slice the **flank steak** against the grain with the 8 in cleaver, 1/8 in thick and about 2 in long. If the steak bends under the knife, freeze it 15 minutes, then slice.
2. Add the **light soy sauce for the beef**, the **Shaoxing wine for the beef**, the **ground white pepper**, the **potato starch**, and the **peanut oil for the beef**. Stir in one direction until the beef is tacky and no liquid pools in the bowl, about 1 minute.
3. Rest 15 minutes. Go on to the scallions.

## Scallions

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry

- 2 cloves garlic, sliced

- 2 Chinese scallion greens (大葱), cut into 2 in lengths

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the stir-fry
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒), for the stir-fry
- 1/4 tsp sugar

1. With the 8 in cleaver, julienne the **ginger** and slice the **garlic**. Cut the **Chinese scallion whites** and the **Chinese scallion greens** from the same stalks into 2 in lengths. Keep them in two piles.
2. Put the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** in one cup. Put the **peanut oil for the scallions** in a second cup. Put the **ginger** and the **garlic** in a third. Put the **Chinese scallion whites** in a fourth. Put the **Chinese scallion greens** in a pile. Put the **light soy sauce for the stir-fry**, the **Shaoxing wine for the stir-fry**, and the **sugar** in another cup.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Scallions**

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the scallions

<!-- cup -->

- 1 in ginger, julienned
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 2 Chinese scallion whites (大葱), cut into 2 in lengths

<!-- cup -->

- 2 Chinese scallion greens (大葱), cut into 2 in lengths

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the stir-fry
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒), for the stir-fry
- 1/4 tsp sugar

**Beef**

- 10 oz flank steak, marinated

1. If the beef is not tacky yet, wait. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 500°F. Heat the empty wok until a drop of water flicked into the center vanishes, about 30 seconds.
2. Add 1 tbsp of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** and swirl it over the bottom. Add half of the **marinated flank steak** in one layer. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 30 seconds. Toss until the red is gone, about 30 seconds more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. Scoop it onto a plate.
3. Add the rest of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** and swirl it over the bottom. Add the rest of the **marinated flank steak** in one layer. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 30 seconds. Toss until the red is gone, about 30 seconds more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. Scoop it onto the plate.
4. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **peanut oil for the scallions**. When it shimmers and has stopped smoking, about 30 seconds, go on.
5. Add the **ginger**, the **garlic**, and the **Chinese scallion whites** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 30 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
6. Add the **Chinese scallion greens**. Stir until they turn bright green and still have bite, about 30 seconds.
7. Add the **marinated flank steak**, the **light soy sauce for the stir-fry**, the **Shaoxing wine for the stir-fry**, and the **sugar** together. Toss until the beef is glossy and the scallions still have bite, about 30 seconds. Press on/off. Taste. If it is flat, add a few drops of the light soy sauce for the stir-fry. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精). Eat it hot.

---

Replace the 1 tsp light soy sauce for the stir-fry with 1 tsp oyster sauce (蚝油). Add it with the Shaoxing wine for the stir-fry and the sugar.
