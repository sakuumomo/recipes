# Malatang (麻辣烫)

Serves 2. About 40 minutes.

The noodles soak in a bowl. The broth and the pot cook in a heavy saucepan.

Soak the noodles. Make the broth and the bowl sauce while they soak. Cook the pot when a strand bends.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Noodles**

- 2 oz sweet potato noodles (红薯粉条)
- 3 cups water, for the noodles

**Broth**

- 2 tbsp peanut oil
- 6 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1 tbsp chili oil (红油), for the broth
- 4 cups water, for the broth
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp sugar, for the broth
- 3/4 cup whole milk

**Bowls**

- 2 tbsp sesame paste (芝麻酱)
- 2 tbsp cold water, for the paste
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tsp Zhenjiang vinegar (镇江香醋)
- 1/4 tsp sugar, for the bowls
- 1 tsp chili oil (红油), for the bowls
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)
- 1 scallion, chopped
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

**Pot**

- 8 oz firm tofu (北豆腐)
- 3 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇)
- 6 oz hot-pot beef slices (肥牛)
- 4 oz bok choy (小白菜), cut into 2 in pieces

---

## Noodles

Use dried sweet potato cords about as thick as a chopstick.

- 2 oz sweet potato noodles (红薯粉条)
- 3 cups water, for the noodles

1. Put the **sweet potato noodles** in a bowl.
2. Put the **water for the noodles** in a heavy saucepan on high until it steams and a finger can stay in it, about 5 minutes.
3. Pour the **water for the noodles** over them. Leave them until a strand bends without snapping, about 30 minutes. Go on to the broth. If a strand still snaps when the broth is ready, wait.

## Broth

Use the coarse Pixian paste, medium 二荆条 flakes marked 中辣, and plain red chili oil.

- 2 tbsp peanut oil
- 6 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1 tbsp chili oil (红油), for the broth
- 4 cups water, for the broth
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp sugar, for the broth
- 3/4 cup whole milk

1. Wipe the saucepan dry. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Slice the **ginger** and the **garlic**. Chop the **Pixian doubanjiang**.
2. Set the saucepan on a coil turned to medium. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, about 2 minutes, add the **dried facing-heaven chilies** and the **Sichuan peppercorns** together. Stir until the peppercorns smell citrusy and the chilies turn one shade darker, still red, about 20 seconds. If they blacken, pull the saucepan off the coil and stir until they cool, then set it back.
3. Add the **ginger** and the **garlic** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 20 seconds.
4. Add the **Pixian doubanjiang**. Stir until the oil turns red and the paste smells sweet, about 45 seconds. If it smells sharp, pull the saucepan off the coil and stir until the smell sweetens, then set it back.
5. Add the **Sichuan chili flakes** and the **chili oil for the broth**. Stir until the oil is red, about 15 seconds.
6. Pull the saucepan off the coil. Add the **water for the broth**, the **light soy sauce**, and the **sugar for the broth** together. Set the saucepan back on high.
7. When the broth boils, pull the saucepan so it sits half on the coil and the surface bubbles gently. Simmer until the broth smells numbing, about 8 minutes.
8. Lift out the **dried facing-heaven chilies**, the **Sichuan peppercorns**, the **ginger**, and the **garlic** with a slotted spoon. Leave the saucepan half off the coil.
9. Add the **whole milk**. Stir until the broth turns opaque and the surface shivers, about 1 minute. If the surface boils hard, pull the saucepan farther off the coil. If the noodles are not ready, leave the saucepan half off the coil.

## Bowls

Use toasted Chinese sesame paste.

- 2 tbsp sesame paste (芝麻酱)
- 2 tbsp cold water, for the paste
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 2 tsp Zhenjiang vinegar (镇江香醋)
- 1/4 tsp sugar, for the bowls
- 1 tsp chili oil (红油), for the bowls
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)
- 1 scallion, chopped
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

1. Mince the **garlic**. Chop the **scallion**. Chop the **cilantro**.
2. Stir the **sesame paste** and the **cold water for the paste** together until the paste is smooth and falls slowly from the spoon.
3. Add the **garlic**, the **Zhenjiang vinegar**, the **sugar for the bowls**, the **chili oil for the bowls**, and the **toasted sesame oil** together. Stir until the sauce looks even. Spoon it into two bowls, half in each.

## Pot

Cut these before they go into the broth. Use firm tofu, the block marked 北豆腐, small bok choy, and the thin marbled slices sold for hot pot.

- 2 oz sweet potato noodles (红薯粉条), drained
- 8 oz firm tofu (北豆腐)
- 3 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇)
- 6 oz hot-pot beef slices (肥牛)
- 4 oz bok choy (小白菜), cut into 2 in pieces
- 1 scallion, chopped
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

1. Cut the **firm tofu** into 3/4 in cubes and pat the cubes dry. Trim the base off the **enoki mushrooms**, tear them into small bunches, and rinse them. If the **hot-pot beef slices** are frozen together, separate them while they are still cold. Pat them dry. Cut the **bok choy** into 2 in pieces and rinse it.
2. If a strand of the **sweet potato noodles** still snaps, wait. Drain them.
3. Add the **drained sweet potato noodles** to the broth and press them under the surface. Cook until they turn translucent at the edges, about 6 minutes. If the surface boils hard, pull the saucepan so it sits half on the coil.
4. Lay the **firm tofu** on the noodles. Cook until the noodles are translucent and chewy and the tofu is hot, about 2 minutes. If a noodle still has a white core, cook 2 minutes more.
5. Add the **enoki mushrooms**, the **hot-pot beef slices**, and the **bok choy** together. Stir once. Cook until the pink is gone and the slices are still tender, the mushrooms have wilted, and the bok choy leaves have wilted with the stems still crisp, about 45 seconds. If the beef stiffens, pull the saucepan off the coil.
6. Ladle the pot and the broth into the two bowls. Scatter the **scallion** and the **cilantro** over the top.
