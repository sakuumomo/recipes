# Red-braised pork (红烧肉)

Serves 2. About 90 minutes.

Blanch the pork in a heavy saucepan. Braise on the Mosaic at 375°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Pork

Leave the skin on.

- 12 oz pork belly

<!-- cup -->

- 6 cups water, for the blanch

1. Cut the **pork belly** into 1 1/2 in chunks with the cleaver. Leave the skin on each chunk.
2. Put the **pork belly** and the **water for the blanch** in a heavy saucepan together. Set the saucepan fully on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. Skim the gray foam as it rises. When the water boils, cook 2 minutes. Pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off.
3. Lift the pork out. Rinse it under hot water until the scum is gone, and drain it. Pour off the blanch water and wipe the saucepan dry.

## Braise

Do this before the wok is hot. Use salted Shaoxing.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 12 oz pork belly, blanched

<!-- cup -->

- 1 in ginger, sliced
- 2 scallions, cut into 2 in lengths
- 1 star anise (八角)
- 1 piece cassia bark (桂皮), 2 in

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 2 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

<!-- cup -->

- 1 1/2 cups water, for the braise

1. Slice the **ginger** and cut the **scallions** into 2 in lengths with the cleaver. Put each group into one cup or pile, prepared the way the line says.
2. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Turn the right dial to 90 minutes. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, add the **blanched pork belly** and keep it in the bottom. Stir until the water boils off and the edges turn golden, about 6 minutes. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start.
3. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **ginger**, the **scallions**, the **star anise**, and the **cassia bark** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 1 minute. If they smell sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start.
4. Add the **Shaoxing wine**. Stir until the raw wine smell is gone, about 30 seconds.
5. Add the **light soy sauce**, the **dark soy sauce**, the **sugar**, and the **kosher salt** together. Stir until the pork looks reddish-brown, about 30 seconds.
6. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Pour in the **water for the braise**. Stay until it boils, and skim any foam.
7. Turn the left dial to 250°F. Cover the wok with the glass lid. The surface should bubble gently, or only shiver. Cook until a fork pushed into a chunk meets little resistance and the skin yields to the fork, about 50 minutes. If a chunk is still firm, cook until the fork goes in, about 15 minutes more. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles, turn the left dial down 10°F, and press start. If the surface goes still, turn the left dial up 10°F. If the display reads E7, press pause/clear twice and wait until the fan stops. Seat the wok, set the same temperature, and press start.
8. Uncover the wok. Lift out the ginger, the scallions, the star anise, and the cassia bark. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Cook, uncovered, until the sauce looks syrupy and the pork sits in less liquid, about 6 minutes. If the sauce still looks thin, keep cooking. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles. Press start. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
9. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Cook until the sauce coats the chunks and a spoon dragged through the bottom leaves a clear trail, about 4 minutes. If the sauce still pools, keep cooking until it coats the chunks. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
10. Press on/off. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精). Spoon the pork onto a plate. The sauce thickens as it cools.
11. Brush the wok with hot water. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 300°F and dry it until the water is gone. Wipe on a little peanut oil, then wipe it back until the metal looks almost dry. Turn the left dial to 450°F. Stop when that film just smokes and the surface looks dry. If it has not smoked, turn the left dial up 10°F. If the smoke thickens, press pause/clear once. Press on/off. The fan may run about a minute. Set the wok on its stand.

---

Add 1 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out, with the ginger, the scallions, the star anise, and the cassia bark. Use whole dried erjingtiao marked 二荆条. Lift it out with the ginger and the spices.

When the Mosaic is off and the pork is still in the wok, add 1/2 tsp Zhenjiang vinegar (镇江香醋) and stir once.
