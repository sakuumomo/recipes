# Coconut shrimp (椰子虾仁)

Serves 2. About 35 minutes.

The shrimp fry on the Mosaic at 325°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Ingredients

**Shrimp**

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper

**Coat**

- 1/4 cup potato starch

## Shrimp

Do this before the wok is hot. Use large peeled shrimp with the tails off, and salted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper

1. If a dark vein remains along the back of a shrimp, pull it out. Pat the **large shrimp** dry.
2. Toss the **large shrimp** with the **Shaoxing wine**, the **kosher salt**, and the **ground white pepper** until the shrimp look moist.

## Coat

Use unsweetened shredded coconut.

- 1/4 cup potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 1 egg

<!-- cup -->

- 1 cup unsweetened shredded coconut (椰丝)

1. Put the **potato starch** in a wide bowl.
2. Crack the **egg** into a second bowl and beat it until the white and the yolk are mixed.
3. Put the **unsweetened shredded coconut** in a third bowl.

## Fry

- 3 cups peanut oil, for frying

<!-- cup -->

- 1/4 cup potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 1 egg

<!-- cup -->

- 1 cup unsweetened shredded coconut (椰丝)

<!-- cup -->

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, seasoned

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 325°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Put the **peanut oil for frying** in the wok. Set the thermometer so the probe is in the oil and off the steel. Stay with the thermometer until it reads 325°F. If the oil stalls short, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 325°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When it returns to 325°F, press start.
2. Roll 4 of the **seasoned large shrimp** in the **potato starch**, then in the **egg**, then in the **unsweetened shredded coconut**. Press so the coconut sticks.
3. Slide those **seasoned large shrimp** in, with room between them, so the oil covers them. Fry until the coconut is gold and the shrimp curl in a loose C, about 2 1/2 minutes, turning once with cooking chopsticks. If they curl into a tight ring, or the coconut darkens fast, lift them out. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
4. Bring the oil back to 325°F. Coat and fry the remaining **seasoned large shrimp** the same way, 4 at a time.
5. Press on/off. Eat them while the coconut is still crisp.

---

For a dip, stir 2 tbsp tomato ketchup (番茄酱), 1 tbsp rice vinegar (米醋), and 1 tsp sugar together until the sugar dissolves.
