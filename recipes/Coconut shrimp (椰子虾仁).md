# Coconut shrimp (椰子虾仁)

Serves 2. About 35 minutes.

The shrimp fry in a deep heavy saucepan on the coil.

Season the shrimp and set out the coating. Fry when the oil is ready.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Shrimp**

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper

**Coat**

- 1/4 cup potato starch
- 1 egg
- 1 cup unsweetened shredded coconut (椰丝)

**Fry**

- 3 cups peanut oil, for frying

---

## Shrimp

Use large peeled shrimp with the tails off. Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper

1. If a dark vein remains along the back of a shrimp, pull it out. Pat the **large shrimp** dry.
2. Toss the **large shrimp** with the **Shaoxing wine**, the **kosher salt**, and the **ground white pepper**.

## Coat

Use unsweetened shredded coconut.

- 1/4 cup potato starch
- 1 egg
- 1 cup unsweetened shredded coconut (椰丝)

1. Put the **potato starch** in a wide bowl.
2. Crack the **egg** into a second bowl and beat it until the white and the yolk are mixed.
3. Put the **unsweetened shredded coconut** in a third bowl.

## Fry

- 3 cups peanut oil, for frying
- 1/4 cup potato starch
- 1 egg
- 1 cup unsweetened shredded coconut (椰丝)
- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, seasoned

1. Put the **peanut oil for frying** in a deep heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to medium. Heat until a thermometer in the oil reads 325°F, about 10 minutes. If the oil smokes or climbs past 325°F, pull the saucepan off the coil until it returns to 325°F, then set it back.
2. Roll 4 of the **seasoned large shrimp** in the **potato starch**, then in the **egg**, then in the **unsweetened shredded coconut**. Press so the coconut sticks.
3. Slide those shrimp in, with room between them. Fry until the coconut is gold and the shrimp curl in a loose C, about 2 1/2 minutes, turning once. If they curl into a tight ring, lift them out. If the coconut darkens fast, pull the saucepan off the coil until the oil returns to 325°F, then set it back. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
4. Let the oil return to 325°F. Coat and fry the remaining **seasoned large shrimp** the same way, 4 at a time.
5. Turn the coil off. Eat them while the coconut is still crisp.
