# Quick pickled chilies (快速泡椒)

Makes 1 pint. About 1 day.

The brine heats in a small stainless saucepan. The chilies pack into a pint jar.

Snip the chilies. Heat the brine, pour it over them, and leave the jar until the next day.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Chilies

Use whole dried erjingtiao marked 二荆条. Use a clean pint jar.

- 10 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

1. Snip the **dried erjingtiao** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Put them in the pint jar. Go on to the brine.

## Brine

Use plain rice vinegar.

- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 in ginger, sliced
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

<!-- cup -->

- 3/4 cup rice vinegar (米醋)
- 3/4 cup water
- 1 tbsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 tbsp sugar

1. Smash the **garlic** with the side of the cleaver until the cloves split. Slice the **ginger**.
2. Put the **garlic**, the **ginger**, and the **Sichuan peppercorns** in the jar with the dried erjingtiao. The peppercorns stay whole.
3. Put the **rice vinegar**, the **water**, the **kosher salt**, and the **sugar** in a small stainless saucepan together.
4. Set the saucepan on a coil turned to medium. Heat until the salt dissolves and the surface shivers, about 3 minutes. If it boils hard, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the bubble stops, turn the coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on. If that setting boils hard again, do the same again. Turn the coil off.
5. Pour the **rice vinegar** and the **water** slowly over the dried erjingtiao. Press them down with a clean spoon until they are covered. The jar smells sharp. If the pods sit proud of the brine, mix those same amounts of rice vinegar, water, kosher salt, and sugar again, and use a jar that holds the pods and the brine. Do not add water.
6. Leave the jar on the counter until you can hold the glass in your hand, about 30 minutes.
7. Screw the lid on and refrigerate until a chili is plump and the center tastes sour, about 1 day. The chilies stay under the brine. If the center is still dry, leave it 1 day more.
8. Chop the chilies when a page asks for pickled red chilies. The sour taste is the vinegar. They keep in the refrigerator for 2 weeks.

---

For a hotter jar, use 24 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out, in place of the dried erjingtiao. Use small dried red chilies marked 朝天椒. Keep those 24 under the original brine. Do not pack the jar full, and do not add water. Where a page calls for 1 tbsp pickled red chilies, start with 2 tsp.

Fresh red chilies take a quart jar in place of the dried erjingtiao. Use 8, cut the stems off, and pat the skins dry. Snip each chili once. Double the brine: 1 1/2 cups rice vinegar (米醋), 1 1/2 cups water, 2 tbsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒), 4 cloves garlic, smashed, and 2 in ginger, sliced. They taste sour after about 4 hours in the refrigerator.
