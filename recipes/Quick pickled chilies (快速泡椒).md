# Quick pickled chilies (快速泡椒)

Makes 1 pint. About 1 day.

The brine heats in a small heavy saucepan. The chilies pack into a pint jar.

Snip the chilies. Heat the brine, pour it over them, and leave the jar until the next day.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

**Chilies**

## Chilies

Use small dried red chilies marked 朝天椒. Use a clean pint jar.

- 1 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

1. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Put them in the pint jar. Go on to the brine.

## Brine

Use plain rice vinegar.

- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 3/4 cup rice vinegar (米醋)
- 3/4 cup water
- 1 tbsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 tbsp sugar

1. Smash the **garlic**. Slice the **ginger**. Put them in the jar with the **Sichuan peppercorns** and the **dried facing-heaven chilies**.
2. Put the **rice vinegar**, the **water**, the **kosher salt**, and the **sugar** in a small heavy saucepan together.
3. Set the saucepan on a coil turned to medium. Heat until the salt dissolves and the surface shivers, about 3 minutes. If it boils hard, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the bubble stops, turn the coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on.
4. Turn the coil off.
5. Pour the **rice vinegar** and the **water** slowly over the chilies. Press the chilies down with a clean spoon until they are covered. The jar smells sharp.
6. Leave the jar on the counter until you can hold the glass in your hand, about 30 minutes.
7. Screw the lid on and refrigerate until a chili is plump and the center tastes sour, about 1 day. The chilies stay under the brine. If the center is still dry, leave it 1 day more.
8. Chop the chilies when a page asks for pickled red chilies. The sour taste is the vinegar. They keep in the refrigerator for 2 weeks.

---

Dried chilies marked erjingtiao (二荆条) use the same weight. They are the milder pepper this pickle is usually made from.

Fresh red chilies take a quart jar. Use 8 oz, cut the stems off, and pat the skins dry. Snip each chili once. Double the brine: 1 1/2 cups rice vinegar (米醋), 1 1/2 cups water, 2 tbsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒), 4 cloves garlic, smashed, and 1 oz ginger, sliced. They taste sour after about 4 hours in the refrigerator.

Facing-heaven chilies stay hot. Where a page calls for 1 tbsp pickled red chilies, start with 2 tsp.
