# Wok seasoning

Makes 1 wok. About 15 minutes.

The oven is the first season and the reset, about a day, bare at 550°F, then 450°F for each coat. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts, and that season starts at 300°F.

For a new wok or a reset, burn it off, wash it, and bake three coats. After a pot of water, a broth, a braise, or the steamer, do the Mosaic season and stop. An oil stir-fry does not get a season.

## Burn-off

Do this for a new wok or a reset. The wok is bare.

1. Put a rack in the center of the oven and a foil-lined sheet pan on a rack below it. Leave the induction base, the glass lid, the chrome rack, and the wok stand out.
2. Set the bare wok upside down on the center rack. Keep the silicone grip off the oven walls and away from the heating element.
3. Heat the oven to 550°F. When it reaches 550°F, bake until the steel looks blue-gray, 45 minutes to 1 hour. Run the hood. If the grip smells like hot plastic or looks shiny, take the wok out.
4. Turn the oven off. Leave the wok inside until you can hold it with a dry towel.

## Wash

The steel is bare, so the soap rinses off before any oil goes on.

1. Scrub the inside and outside with hot water and dish soap until the burned film and loose black dust are gone.
2. Rinse the wok and dry it with a towel. If an orange spot appears, scrub that spot and dry it again.

## Oven coats

Use refined peanut oil. Bake three coats, and cool the wok after each one.

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the first coat

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the second coat

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the third coat

1. Wipe the **peanut oil for the first coat** over the inside and the outside of the steel with a paper towel. Keep the oil off the silicone grip. Wipe again with a clean towel until the metal looks almost dry.
2. Set the wok upside down on the center rack, with the foil-lined sheet pan on the rack below. Heat the oven to 450°F. When it reaches 450°F, bake for 1 hour.
3. Turn the oven off and leave the wok inside until it is cool.
4. Wipe the **peanut oil for the second coat** over the inside and the outside of the steel with a paper towel. Keep the oil off the silicone grip. Wipe again with a clean towel until the metal looks almost dry.
5. Set the wok upside down on the center rack, with the foil-lined sheet pan on the rack below. Heat the oven to 450°F. When it reaches 450°F, bake for 1 hour.
6. Turn the oven off and leave the wok inside until it is cool.
7. Wipe the **peanut oil for the third coat** over the inside and the outside of the steel with a paper towel. Keep the oil off the silicone grip. Wipe again with a clean towel until the metal looks almost dry.
8. Set the wok upside down on the center rack, with the foil-lined sheet pan on the rack below. Heat the oven to 450°F. When it reaches 450°F, bake for 1 hour.
9. Turn the oven off and leave the wok inside until it is cool. The steel looks dry and smooth, brown to black. If a patch is tacky, scrub that patch with hot water, dry the wok, and bake one more coat the same way.

## Mosaic

Use refined peanut oil. Do this after a pot of water, a broth, a braise, or the steamer, once the food is out.

- 1 tsp peanut oil

1. Brush the wok with hot water. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 300°F and dry it until the water is gone. Wipe on the **peanut oil**, then wipe it back until the metal looks almost dry. Turn the left dial to 450°F. Stop when that film just smokes and the surface looks dry. If it has not smoked, turn the left dial up 10°F. If the smoke thickens, press pause/clear once. Press on/off. The fan may run about a minute. Set the wok on its stand.
