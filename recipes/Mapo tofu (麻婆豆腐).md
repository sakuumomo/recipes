# Mapo tofu (麻婆豆腐)

Serves 2. About 40 minutes.

Tofu in a heavy saucepan. Peppercorns in a small skillet. Stir-fry in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Work straight down. When the tofu timer rings, drain the tofu, then keep going.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines with no blank line between them share a cup.

## Ingredients

**Tofu**

- 14 oz soft tofu (嫩豆腐)

- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

**Sichuan pepper**

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

**Starch slurry**

- 1 tbsp potato starch
- 2 tbsp cold water

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the beef

- 4 oz ground beef

- 3 tbsp peanut oil, for the doubanjiang

- 2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped

- 2 tsp fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and lightly crushed
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 2 tsp garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ginger, minced

- 3/4 cup unsalted chicken stock
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper

- 3 scallions, cut into 3/4 in lengths

---

## Tofu

Use water-packed soft tofu.

- 14 oz soft tofu (嫩豆腐)

- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

1. Cut the **soft tofu** into 3/4 in cubes.
2. Put the **water** and the **kosher salt** in a heavy saucepan. Set the saucepan on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. When the water boils, pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to low. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on. The surface should only shiver.
3. Slide in the tofu and set a timer for 8 minutes. Go on to the Sichuan pepper. When the timer rings, lift the tofu out with a slotted spoon and let it drain.

## Sichuan pepper

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

1. Toast the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a small skillet over medium-low heat, shaking often, until they smell citrusy, about 2 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil and turn that coil off.
2. Cool them 1 minute. Put them in a small bowl and crush them to a powder with the back of a spoon.

## Starch slurry

- 1 tbsp potato starch
- 2 tbsp cold water

1. Stir the **potato starch** and the **cold water** together in a small bowl until the liquid is smooth. Stir it again just before it goes into the wok.

## Prep

Do this before the wok is hot. Use the coarse Pixian paste, and medium 二荆条 flakes marked 中辣.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the beef

- 4 oz ground beef

- 3 tbsp peanut oil, for the doubanjiang

- 2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped

- 2 tsp fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and lightly crushed
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 2 tsp garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ginger, minced

- 3/4 cup unsalted chicken stock
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper

- 3 scallions, cut into 3/4 in lengths

1. Put each group into one cup or pile, prepared the way the line says.
2. Set the ground Sichuan pepper and the starch slurry next to the cups. When the tofu timer rings, drain the tofu and set it with them.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the beef

- 4 oz ground beef

- 3 tbsp peanut oil, for the doubanjiang

- 2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped

- 2 tsp fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and lightly crushed
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 2 tsp garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ginger, minced

- 3/4 cup unsalted chicken stock
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper

- 3 scallions, cut into 3/4 in lengths

**Tofu**

- 14 oz soft tofu (嫩豆腐), drained

**Starch slurry**

- 1 tbsp potato starch
- 2 tbsp cold water

**Sichuan pepper**

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒), toasted and crushed

1. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium-high. Add the **peanut oil for the beef**. When it shimmers, about 2 minutes, add the **ground beef** and keep it in the bottom. Stir-fry, breaking it into small bits, until it is browned, about 2 minutes. If the smoke thickens, turn the coil off and stir until it thins. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high. Scoop it back into its bowl.
2. Add the **peanut oil for the doubanjiang**. When it shimmers, add the **Pixian doubanjiang** and fry, stirring, until the oil is deep red and smells toasty, about 30 seconds. If it smells sharp, turn the coil off and stir until the smell sweetens. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high.
3. Add the **fermented black beans**, **Sichuan chili flakes**, **garlic**, and **ginger** together. Stir-fry until fragrant, about 20 seconds.
4. Pour in the **chicken stock** and the **ground white pepper**. Bring it to a simmer.
5. Add the **drained tofu**. Simmer 3 minutes, until the tofu is red. Push the tofu up the sides, scrape the bottom, and fold the tofu back down. If the center boils hard, turn the coil off until the surface settles and the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high.
6. Return the **ground beef** and simmer 1 minute, folding the tofu through the sauce.
7. Stir the **starch slurry**. Pour in about a third and fold the tofu through the sauce. Add the rest in one or two more pours, and stop when the sauce is glossy and coats the back of a spoon. If the center starts to stick, turn the coil off and keep folding.
8. Turn the coil off. Add the **scallions**. Stir until they turn bright and smell sweet, about 15 seconds.
9. Spoon it into a bowl, sprinkle with the **ground Sichuan pepper**, and serve.
