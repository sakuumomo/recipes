# Mapo tofu (麻婆豆腐)

Serves 2. About 40 minutes.

Tofu in a heavy saucepan. Peppercorns in a small skillet. Stir-fry on the Mosaic at 375°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Work straight down. When the tofu timer rings, drain the tofu, then keep going.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Tofu

Use water-packed soft tofu.

- 14 oz soft tofu (嫩豆腐)

<!-- cup -->

- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

1. Cut the **soft tofu** into 3/4 in cubes.
2. Put the **water** and the **kosher salt** in a heavy saucepan. Set the saucepan fully on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. When the water boils, pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to low. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on. The surface should only shiver.
3. Slide in the **soft tofu** and set a timer for 8 minutes. Go on to the Sichuan pepper. When the timer rings, lift the tofu out with a slotted spoon and let it drain. Pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off.

## Sichuan pepper

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

1. Put the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a small skillet. Set the skillet fully on a coil turned to medium-low. Shake it often until the peppercorns smell citrusy, about 2 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil and turn that coil off.
2. Cool them 1 minute. Put them in a small bowl and crush them to a powder with the back of a spoon.

## Starch slurry

- 1 tbsp potato starch
- 2 tbsp cold water

1. Stir the **potato starch** and the **cold water** together in a small bowl until the liquid is smooth. Stir it again just before it goes into the wok.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the beef

<!-- cup -->

- 4 oz ground beef

<!-- cup -->

- 4 tbsp peanut oil, for the doubanjiang

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tsp fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and lightly crushed
- 2 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 2 tsp garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ginger, minced

<!-- cup -->

- 3/4 cup unsalted chicken stock
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper

<!-- cup -->

- whites of 3 scallions, halved lengthwise and cut into 1/4 in pieces
- greens of 3 scallions, cut into 3/4 in lengths

**Tofu**

- 14 oz soft tofu (嫩豆腐), drained

**Starch slurry**

- 1 tbsp potato starch
- 2 tbsp cold water

**Sichuan pepper**

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒), toasted and crushed

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Add the **peanut oil for the beef**. When it shimmers, add the **ground beef** and keep it in the bottom. Stir-fry, breaking it into small bits, until it is browned, about 2 minutes. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. Scoop it back into its bowl.
2. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **peanut oil for the doubanjiang**. When it shimmers and has stopped smoking, add the **Pixian doubanjiang** and fry, stirring, until the oil is deep red and smells toasty, about 30 seconds. If it smells sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start. If the oil has not turned red, turn the left dial up 10°F and keep frying until it is red.
3. Add the **fermented black beans**, **Sichuan chili flakes**, **garlic**, and **ginger** together. Stir-fry until fragrant, about 20 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
4. Pour in the **unsalted chicken stock** and the **ground white pepper**. When the surface simmers, go on. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles, turn the left dial down 10°F, and press start. If the surface goes still, turn the left dial up 10°F.
5. Add the **drained tofu**. Simmer until the tofu is red, about 3 minutes. Push the tofu up the sides, scrape the bottom, and fold the tofu back down. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles, turn the left dial down 10°F, and press start. If the surface goes still, turn the left dial up 10°F.
6. Return the **ground beef** and simmer about 1 minute, folding the tofu through the sauce.
7. Stir the **potato starch** and the **cold water** again. Pour in about a third and fold the tofu through the sauce. Add the rest in one or two more pours, and stop when the sauce is glossy and coats the back of a spoon. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
8. Add the **whites of 3 scallions** and the **greens of 3 scallions**. Stir until the greens turn bright and the whites smell sweet, about 15 seconds. Press on/off.
9. Add the **toasted and crushed Sichuan peppercorns**. Stir until the powder is mixed through the sauce. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精).
10. Spoon it into a bowl and serve.

---

Use 14 oz silken tofu (内酯豆腐) in place of the soft tofu. Cut it into 1 in cubes. Set the blanch timer for 2 minutes, and lift the cubes out with a slotted spoon when it rings. When the tofu goes into the wok, push the sauce under the cubes and leave them in the bottom. When the beef goes back, lay it over the tofu and simmer. Pour the slurry down the side of the wok in the same pours. When the scallions go in, push them through the sauce the same way. After the heat is off, stir the pepper through the sauce along the side of the wok.
