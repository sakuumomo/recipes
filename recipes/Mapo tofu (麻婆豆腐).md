# Mapo tofu (麻婆豆腐)

Serves 2. About 40 minutes.

Instant Pot rice, 1 cup water to 1 cup rice, high pressure for 3 minutes. Tofu in a heavy saucepan. Peppercorns in a small skillet. Stir-fry in a 10 in heavy skillet on the coil.

Start the rice and work straight down. When the tofu timer rings, drain the tofu, then keep going. Leave the rice lid on until you serve.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Rice**

- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 cup water

**Tofu**

- 14 oz soft tofu (嫩豆腐)
- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

**Sichuan pepper**

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

**Starch slurry**

- 1 tbsp potato starch
- 2 tbsp cold water

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the beef
- 4 oz ground beef
- 3 tbsp peanut oil, for the doubanjiang
- 2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped
- 2 tsp fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and lightly crushed
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 2 tsp garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ginger, minced
- 3/4 cup unsalted chicken stock
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 3 scallions, cut into 3/4 in lengths

---

## Rice

- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 cup water

1. Rinse the **jasmine rice** under cold water until the runoff is almost clear. Drain it well.
2. Put the rice and the **water** in the Instant Pot and stir once.
3. Lock the lid, set the valve to sealing, and pressure cook on high for 3 minutes. Go on to the tofu.
4. When it beeps, turn the valve to venting. Leave the lid on until you serve, then fluff the rice.

## Tofu

- 14 oz soft tofu (嫩豆腐)
- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

1. Cut the **soft tofu** into 3/4 in cubes.
2. Put the **water** and the **kosher salt** in a heavy saucepan. Bring them to a bare simmer over medium-high heat. When the water starts to boil, turn the coil down until the surface only shivers.
3. Slide in the tofu and set a timer for 8 minutes. Go on to the Sichuan pepper. When the timer rings, lift the tofu out with a slotted spoon and let it drain.

## Sichuan pepper

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

1. Toast the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a small skillet over medium-low heat, shaking often, until they smell citrusy, about 2 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil if they start to smoke.
2. Cool them 1 minute. Put them in a small bowl and crush them to a powder with the back of a spoon.

## Starch slurry

- 1 tbsp potato starch
- 2 tbsp cold water

1. Stir the **potato starch** and the **cold water** together in a small bowl until the liquid is smooth. Stir it again just before it goes into the skillet.

## Prep

Do this before the skillet is hot.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the beef
- 4 oz ground beef
- 3 tbsp peanut oil, for the doubanjiang
- 2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped
- 2 tsp fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and lightly crushed
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 2 tsp garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ginger, minced
- 3/4 cup unsalted chicken stock
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 3 scallions, cut into 3/4 in lengths

1. Put each ingredient into its own cup or pile, prepared the way the line says.
2. Set the ground Sichuan pepper and the starch slurry next to the cups. When the tofu timer rings, drain the tofu and set it with them.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the beef
- 4 oz ground beef
- 3 tbsp peanut oil, for the doubanjiang
- 2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped
- 2 tsp fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and lightly crushed
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 2 tsp garlic, minced
- 2 tsp ginger, minced
- 3/4 cup unsalted chicken stock
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 3 scallions, cut into 3/4 in lengths

**Tofu**

- 14 oz soft tofu (嫩豆腐), drained

**Starch slurry**

- 1 tbsp potato starch
- 2 tbsp cold water

**Sichuan pepper**

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒), toasted and crushed

1. Put an empty 10 in heavy skillet on a coil set to high. Heat it until a drop of water skitters and vanishes, about 3 minutes.
2. Add the **peanut oil for the beef**. When it shimmers and a thin wisp of smoke shows, add the **ground beef** and keep it over the center of the coil. Stir-fry, breaking it into small bits, until it is browned, about 2 minutes. Scoop it back into its bowl.
3. Pull the skillet off the coil and turn the coil to medium-low. When the coil stops glowing, set the skillet back on it.
4. Add the **peanut oil for the doubanjiang**. If it smokes the moment it hits, wait a few seconds. When it is shimmering, add the **Pixian doubanjiang** and fry, stirring, until the oil is deep red and smells toasty, about 30 seconds. If it smells sharp, pull the skillet off the coil and stir until the smell sweetens, then set it back on medium-low.
5. Add the **fermented black beans**, **Sichuan chili flakes**, **garlic**, and **ginger** together. Stir-fry until fragrant, about 20 seconds.
6. Pour in the **chicken stock** and the **ground white pepper**. Bring it to a simmer.
7. Add the **drained tofu** and the **scallions**. Simmer 3 minutes, until the tofu is red and the scallions have softened. Push the tofu aside, scrape the center, and fold the tofu back through the sauce. If the center boils hard, pull the skillet so it sits half on the coil.
8. Return the **ground beef** and simmer 1 minute, folding the tofu through the sauce.
9. Stir the **starch slurry**. Pour in about a third and fold the tofu through the sauce. Add the rest in one or two more pours, and stop when the sauce is glossy and coats the back of a spoon. If the center starts to stick, pull the skillet off the coil and keep folding.
10. Spoon it into a bowl, sprinkle with the **ground Sichuan pepper**, and serve with the rice.
