# Salt and pepper shrimp (椒盐虾)

Serves 2. About 30 minutes.

Peppercorns in a small skillet. Fry on the Mosaic at 350°F, then toss in that wok at 300°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Season the shrimp. Toast the peppercorns and cut the toss while they sit. When the timer rings, leave the shrimp and keep going. Heat the oil and stay with the thermometer. When the oil is ready and the shrimp have finished their 10 minutes, pat them dry, coat them, and fry them. Toss them as soon as they come out.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Shrimp

Use large peeled shrimp with the tails off, and salted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the shrimp

1. If a dark vein remains along the back of a shrimp, pull it out. Pat the **large shrimp** dry.
2. Add the **Shaoxing wine** and the **kosher salt for the shrimp**. Stir until the shrimp look moist. Set a timer for 10 minutes. Go on to the pepper salt. When the timer rings, leave the shrimp and keep going.

## Pepper salt

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/2 tsp ground white pepper

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 red chili (小米辣), sliced

<!-- cup -->

- white of 1 scallion, halved lengthwise and chopped
- green of 1 scallion, chopped

1. Put the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a small skillet. Set the skillet fully on a coil turned to medium-low. Shake it often until the peppercorns smell citrusy, about 1 minute. Slide the skillet off the coil and turn that coil off.
2. Cool them 1 minute. Put them in a small bowl and crush them to a powder with the back of a spoon.
3. Stir in the **kosher salt** and the **ground white pepper** until the powder looks even.
4. Slice the **garlic**. Slice the **red chili**. Halve the **white of 1 scallion** lengthwise and chop it. Chop the **green of 1 scallion**. They come from the same scallion. Leave the pepper salt in its bowl. Put the garlic and the red chili in one cup. Put the **white of 1 scallion** and the **green of 1 scallion** in a cup.

## Fry

- 2 cups peanut oil, for frying

<!-- cup -->

- 1/4 cup potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the toss

<!-- cup -->

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, seasoned

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 350°F. Turn the right dial to 30 minutes. Add the **peanut oil for frying**. Stay with the thermometer, the probe in the oil and off the steel, until the oil reads 350°F. If the oil stalls short of 350°F, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, turn the left dial up another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When the oil returns to 350°F, press start.
2. When the shrimp have finished their 10 minutes, pat them dry. Toss the **seasoned large shrimp** with the **potato starch** until each shrimp has a thin coat. Shake off the extra starch.
3. Slide in half of the **seasoned large shrimp** in one layer so the oil covers them. Leave the shrimp still until the coating sets, about 20 seconds, then turn them. Fry until they are pale gold, curled in a loose C, and the center is just opaque, about 40 seconds more. If they curl into a tight ring, lift them out. Lift the shrimp out with a slotted spoon.
4. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Fry the remaining **seasoned large shrimp** the same way. Lift them out.
5. Ladle the spare oil out with the wok ladle into a heatproof bowl until a thin film is left. Leave the wok seated.

## Toss

Do this as soon as the shrimp come out.

These amounts were measured above.

**Fry**

- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the toss

**Pepper salt**

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒), toasted and crushed

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/2 tsp ground white pepper

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 red chili (小米辣), sliced

<!-- cup -->

- white of 1 scallion, halved lengthwise and chopped
- green of 1 scallion, chopped

**Shrimp**

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, fried

1. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **peanut oil for the toss**. When it shimmers and has stopped smoking, about 30 seconds, add the **garlic** and the **red chili** together. Stir until the garlic is pale gold, about 40 seconds. If the garlic darkens, press pause/clear once and stir until it stops darkening. Press start.
2. Add the **fried large shrimp**, the **white of 1 scallion**, the **green of 1 scallion**, the **toasted and crushed Sichuan peppercorns**, the **kosher salt**, and the **ground white pepper** together. Stir until every shrimp is seasoned and the coating is still crisp, about 20 seconds.
3. Press on/off. Scrape the shrimp onto a plate. Set the wok on its stand. Eat them hot, while the coating is still crisp.

---

Stir 1/8 tsp five-spice powder (五香粉) in with the kosher salt and the ground white pepper.
