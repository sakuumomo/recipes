# Salt and pepper shrimp (椒盐虾)

Serves 2. About 30 minutes.

The shrimp fry in a deep heavy saucepan on the coil. The peppercorns toast in a small skillet. The toss is in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Season the shrimp. Toast the peppercorns while they sit. Heat the oil, coat the shrimp, and fry them. Toss them as soon as they come out.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Shrimp**

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the shrimp

**Pepper salt**

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/2 tsp ground white pepper

**Fry**

- 2 cups peanut oil, for frying
- 1/4 cup potato starch

**Toss**

- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the toss
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 red chili (小米辣), sliced
- 1 scallion, chopped

---

## Shrimp

Use large peeled shrimp with the tails off. Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the shrimp

1. If a dark vein remains along the back of a shrimp, pull it out. Pat the **large shrimp** dry.
2. Add the **Shaoxing wine** and the **kosher salt for the shrimp**. Stir until the shrimp look moist. Set a timer for 10 minutes. Go on to the pepper salt.

## Pepper salt

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/2 tsp ground white pepper

1. Toast the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a small skillet over medium-low heat, shaking often, until they smell citrusy, about 1 minute. Slide the skillet off the coil if they start to smoke.
2. Cool them 1 minute. Crush them to a powder with the back of a spoon.
3. Stir in the **kosher salt** and the **ground white pepper**.

## Fry

- 2 cups peanut oil, for frying
- 1/4 cup potato starch
- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, seasoned

1. Put the **peanut oil for frying** in a deep heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high. Heat until a thermometer in the oil reads 350°F, about 8 minutes. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, pull the saucepan off the coil until it returns to 350°F, then set it back.
2. When the shrimp have finished their 10 minutes, pat them dry. Toss the **seasoned large shrimp** with the **potato starch** until each shrimp has a thin coat. Shake off the extra starch.
3. Slide in half of the **seasoned large shrimp** in one layer. Leave them still until the coating sets, about 20 seconds, then turn them. Fry until they are pale gold, curled in a loose C, and the center is just opaque, about 40 seconds more. If they curl into a tight ring, lift them out. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, pull the saucepan off the coil until it returns to 350°F, then set it back. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
4. Let the oil return to 350°F. Fry the remaining **seasoned large shrimp** the same way. Lift them out.
5. Turn the coil off.

## Toss

Do this before the wok is hot.

- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the toss
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 red chili (小米辣), sliced
- 1 scallion, chopped
- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, fried
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒), toasted and crushed
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/2 tsp ground white pepper

1. Slice the **garlic**. Slice the **red chili**. Chop the **scallion**.
2. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium. Add the **peanut oil for the toss**. When it shimmers, about 2 minutes, add the **garlic** and the **red chili** together. Stir until the garlic is pale gold, about 40 seconds. If it darkens, turn the coil off and stir until it cools, then turn the coil back to medium.
3. Add the **fried large shrimp**, the **scallion**, the **toasted and crushed Sichuan peppercorns**, the **kosher salt**, and the **ground white pepper** together. Toss until every shrimp is seasoned and the coating is still crisp, about 20 seconds.
4. Eat them hot, while the coating is still crisp.
