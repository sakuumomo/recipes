# Sauce-braised eggplant (酱烧茄子)

Serves 2. About 35 minutes.

Stir-fry in the wok at 375°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Salt the eggplant. Mix the sauce cups while it sits. When the 15 minutes are up, starch the eggplant, then fry it and make the sauce in the same wok.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Eggplant

Use a long Chinese eggplant.

- 2 long Chinese eggplants
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 1/4 cup peanut oil, for the eggplant

1. With the 8 in cleaver, trim the **long Chinese eggplants** and cut them into pieces about 2 in long and 3/4 in thick. Toss the pieces with the **kosher salt**. Put the **potato starch** in one cup and the **peanut oil for the eggplant** in another. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the sauce.
2. When the timer rings, squeeze the pieces and pat them dry. Toss them with the **potato starch** until each piece has a thin coat. Shake off the extra starch.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above. Start when the eggplant is starched.

**Eggplant**

- 1/4 cup peanut oil, for the eggplant

<!-- cup -->

- 2 long Chinese eggplants, salted, squeezed, and starched

**Sauce**

- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped
- 1 tbsp sweet wheat sauce (甜面酱)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒)
- 2 tbsp water, for the sauce

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 1 tbsp cold water, for the sauce

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp Zhenjiang vinegar (镇江香醋)
- white of 1 scallion, halved lengthwise and chopped
- green of 1 scallion, chopped

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Add the **peanut oil for the eggplant**. When it shimmers, add a single layer of the **long Chinese eggplants** in the bottom. Cook until the underside is golden, about 2 minutes. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start.
2. Turn the pieces. Cook until both sides are golden and the center of a piece looks creamy, about 2 minutes more. If a center is still white and spongy, cook 1 minute more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. If the food stays pale after another minute at this temperature, turn the left dial to 425°F and keep going until a center looks creamy. Lift this layer out onto a plate.
3. If the dial is not at 375°F, turn the left dial back to 375°F. Cook the rest of the **long Chinese eggplants** one layer at a time until both sides are golden and the center of a piece looks creamy. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. If the food stays pale after another minute at this temperature, turn the left dial to 425°F and keep going. Lift each layer out onto the plate. If the dial is not at 375°F, turn the left dial back to 375°F before the next layer.
4. With the wok ladle, take the spare oil out into a heatproof bowl until about 1 tbsp remains. Leave the wok seated. If the wok is dry, pour back about 1 tbsp of that oil.
5. Turn the left dial to 300°F. When the oil shimmers and has stopped smoking, about 30 seconds, add the **ginger** and the **garlic** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 20 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until the color stops. Press start.
6. Add the **Pixian doubanjiang** and the **sweet wheat sauce** together. Stir until the oil turns red and the pastes smell sweet, about 45 seconds. If they smell sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start. If the oil has not turned red, turn the left dial up 10°F and keep frying until it is red.
7. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Add the **sugar**, the **light soy sauce**, the **dark soy sauce**, the **Shaoxing wine**, and the **water for the sauce** together. Stir until the sauce bubbles, about 20 seconds. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles. Press start.
8. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **long Chinese eggplants** back. Stir until the pieces are coated and soft, about 1 minute. If a center is still firm, cook 1 minute more. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
9. Stir the **potato starch for the sauce** and the **cold water for the sauce** again. Pour them in. Stir until the sauce looks glossy and clings to the pieces, about 20 seconds.
10. Press on/off. Add the **Zhenjiang vinegar**, the **white of 1 scallion**, and the **green of 1 scallion** together. Stir until the vinegar smells sharp and the green turns bright, about 10 seconds. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精).
