# Sichuan pickled chilies (泡椒)

Makes 1 pint. About 7 days.

The brine heats in a small stainless saucepan, then cools. The chilies pack into a pint jar.

Snip the chilies. Boil the brine and let it cool. Cut the garlic and the ginger while it cools. When the brine feels as cool as the counter, add the aromatics, stir in the baijiu, and pour.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Chilies

Use whole dried erjingtiao marked 二荆条. Use a clean pint jar.

- 10 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

1. Snip the **dried erjingtiao** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Put them in the pint jar. Go on to the brine.

## Brine

Use a clear grain liquor marked 白酒.

- 1 1/2 cups water

<!-- cup -->

- 2 1/2 tbsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 1/2 tsp sugar

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp baijiu (白酒)

1. Put the **water** in a small stainless saucepan. Set the saucepan on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. Heat until the water boils, about 4 minutes.
2. Pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. Add the **kosher salt** and the **sugar**. Stir until they dissolve, about 30 seconds.
3. Put the **baijiu** in a cup and set it by the saucepan.
4. Leave the saucepan off the coil until the brine feels as cool as the counter, about 1 hour. Go on to the aromatics. Come back when the brine is cool.

## Aromatics

- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 in ginger, sliced
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

1. Smash the **garlic** with the side of the cleaver until the cloves split. Slice the **ginger**. The **Sichuan peppercorns** stay whole. Put them in one cup.
2. If the brine is still warm, wait until it feels as cool as the counter.

## Pack

These amounts were measured above.

**Aromatics**

- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 in ginger, sliced
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

**Chilies**

- 10 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

**Brine**

- 1 1/2 cups water

<!-- cup -->

- 2 1/2 tbsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 1/2 tsp sugar

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp baijiu (白酒)

1. Add the **garlic**, the **ginger**, and the **Sichuan peppercorns** to the **dried erjingtiao** in the jar. The aromatics sit on the chilies.
2. Stir the **baijiu** into the **water** until the saucepan smells faintly of liquor, about 10 seconds.
3. Pour the **water**, the **kosher salt**, and the **sugar** over them together. Press the pods down with a clean spoon until they are covered. If the pods sit proud of the brine, make those same amounts of water, kosher salt, sugar, and baijiu again the same way, wait until that brine feels as cool as the counter, and use a jar that holds the pods and the brine. Do not add water. If they float, set a small clean dish on them so they stay under the brine.
4. Set the lid on loosely. The pods stay under the brine.
5. Leave the jar out of the sun until the brine smells sour and looks a little cloudy and a chili tastes sour, about 7 days. Small bubbles may sit on the chilies during the wait. If a chili tastes only salty, leave the jar 2 days more. Skim a flat white film with a clean spoon when the brine still smells sour. If the brine smells rotten, or the surface grows fuzzy spots or spots that are green, black, or pink, throw the jar out.
6. Screw the lid on and refrigerate. The brine smells sour.
7. Chop the chilies when a page asks for pickled red chilies. Leave the brine in the jar. They are salty, and they keep for a few months.

---

For a hotter jar, use 24 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out, in place of the dried erjingtiao. Use small dried red chilies marked 朝天椒. Keep those 24 under the original brine. Do not pack the jar full, and do not add water. Where a page calls for 1 tbsp pickled red chilies, start with 2 tsp.

Fresh red chilies are the classic pepper. Use a quart jar and 6 fresh red chilies in place of the dried erjingtiao. Cut the stems off and wait until the skins look matte, about 2 hours. Double the brine: 3 cups water, 5 tbsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, 1 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp baijiu (白酒), 4 cloves garlic, smashed, 2 in ginger, sliced, and 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒). They turn sour in about 10 days.

If there is no baijiu (白酒), use the same amount of vodka for that line.
