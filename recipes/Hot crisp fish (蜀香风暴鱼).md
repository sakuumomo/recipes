# Hot crisp fish (蜀香风暴鱼)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The fish fries in the wok on the Mosaic at 350°F. The toss is in that wok at 300°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Marinate the fish. Cut the chilies and the aromatics, and mix the batter, while it sits. When the timer rings, leave the fish and keep going. Heat the oil and stay with the thermometer. Coat the fish when the oil is ready, then fry it.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Fish

Use salted Shaoxing.

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, cut into 3/4 in pieces
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒), for the fish
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 egg white

1. If the **snakehead fillet** still has skin, cut the skin off. Cut the fillet into 3/4 in pieces with the boning knife.
2. Rinse the pieces under cold water until the water runs clear. Pat them dry.
3. Toss them with the **Shaoxing wine for the fish**, the **light soy sauce**, the **kosher salt for the fish**, the **ground white pepper**, and the **egg white**. Stir until the fish feels sticky. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the prep. When the timer rings, leave the fish and keep going.

## Prep

Do this while the fish marinates. Use whole dried erjingtiao marked 二荆条, and medium 二荆条 flakes marked 中辣.

- 2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 15 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced thin
- 1 in ginger, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒), for the toss
- 2 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths
- 1 tsp ground cumin (孜然)
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

1. Put the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a bowl.
2. Snip the **dried erjingtiao** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Put them in that bowl.
3. With the 8 in cleaver, slice the **garlic** and the **ginger** thin. Put them in one cup.
4. Put the **sugar**, the **Shaoxing wine for the toss**, the **ground cumin**, the **Sichuan chili flakes**, and the **white sesame seeds** in one cup.
5. With the 8 in cleaver, cut the **scallions** into 1 in lengths. Put them in that cup.

## Batter

Do this while the fish marinates.

- 1/4 cup potato starch, for the batter
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the batter

<!-- cup -->

- 3 tbsp cold water
- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the batter

1. Stir the **potato starch for the batter**, the **all-purpose flour**, the **baking powder**, and the **kosher salt for the batter** together until no dry streaks remain.
2. Add the **cold water** and the **peanut oil for the batter**. Stir until the batter is a thick paste and a ribbon off the spoon sits on the surface.

## Fry

- 3 cups peanut oil, for frying

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp potato starch, for the dust

<!-- cup -->

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, cut into 3/4 in pieces, marinated

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 350°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Add the **peanut oil for frying**. Stay with the thermometer, the probe in the oil and off the steel, until the oil reads 350°F. If the oil stalls short of 350°F, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, turn the left dial up another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When the oil returns to 350°F, press start.
2. If the fish has not finished its 15 minutes, wait until it has.
3. Pat the **marinated snakehead fillet** dry.
4. Toss it with the **potato starch for the dust** until the surface looks tacky.
5. Stir the batter. Turn half the **marinated snakehead fillet** in it until each piece wears a heavy coat. Lift the pieces and let the extra drip off.
6. Add that half in one layer in the bottom so the oil covers every piece. Leave the pieces still until the crust has set and turned faintly yellow, about 2 minutes.
7. Turn any pale side with cooking chopsticks. Fry until the crust is pale gold and the center is just opaque, about 1 minute more. Break one piece open. If the center is still translucent, fry 30 seconds more. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
8. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Coat and fry the remaining **marinated snakehead fillet** the same way. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
9. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Stay with the thermometer until it reads 375°F. If the oil stalls short of 375°F, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, turn the left dial up another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 375°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When it returns to 375°F, press start.
10. Return the first half of the **marinated snakehead fillet** to the bottom so the oil covers every piece. Fry until the crust is deep gold and crisp, about 30 seconds. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
11. Bring the oil back to 375°F. Fry the remaining **marinated snakehead fillet** in the bottom until the crust is deep gold and crisp, about 30 seconds. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
12. Ladle the spare **peanut oil for frying** into a heatproof bowl with the wok ladle until 2 tbsp remains. Leave the wok seated.

## Toss

These amounts were measured above. The wok is still seated.

**Fry**

- 3 cups peanut oil, for frying, all but 2 tbsp ladled out

**Prep**

- 2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 15 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced thin
- 1 in ginger, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒), for the toss
- 2 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths
- 1 tsp ground cumin (孜然)
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

**Fish**

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, cut into 3/4 in pieces, fried

1. Turn the left dial to 300°F. When the **peanut oil for frying** shimmers and has stopped smoking, go on.
2. Add the **Sichuan peppercorns** and the **dried erjingtiao** together. Stir until the chilies smell toasty and turn one shade darker, still red, about 30 seconds. If they blacken, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
3. Add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until fragrant, about 15 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
4. Add the **fried snakehead fillet**, the **sugar**, the **Shaoxing wine for the toss**, the **scallions**, the **ground cumin**, the **Sichuan chili flakes**, and the **white sesame seeds** together. Toss until the wine cooks off and the crust is still crisp, about 20 seconds. Press on/off.
5. Scoop the fish, the scallions, the chilies, and the peppercorns onto a plate. Set the wok on its stand. Eat the fish and the scallions. The chilies and the peppercorns stay on the plate for their aroma.
