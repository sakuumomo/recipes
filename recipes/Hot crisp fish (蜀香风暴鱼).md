# Hot crisp fish (蜀香风暴鱼)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The fish fries in a deep heavy saucepan on the coil. The toss is in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Marinate the fish. While it sits, cut the chilies and the aromatics, and mix the batter. Heat the oil when the batter is mixed. Coat the fish when the oil is ready, then fry it.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Fish

Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, cut into 3/4 in pieces
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the fish
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 egg white

1. If the **snakehead fillet** still has skin, cut the skin off. Cut it into 3/4 in pieces.
2. Rinse the pieces under cold water until the water runs clear. Pat them dry.
3. Toss them with the **Shaoxing wine for the fish**, the **light soy sauce**, the **kosher salt for the fish**, the **ground white pepper**, and the **egg white**. Stir until the fish feels sticky. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the prep.

## Prep

Do this while the fish marinates. Use medium 二荆条 flakes marked 中辣.

- 2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1 1/2 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the toss
- 3 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths
- 1 tsp ground cumin (孜然)
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

1. Put the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a cup.
2. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Put them in that cup.
3. Slice the **garlic** and the **ginger** thin.
4. Put the **sugar**, the **Shaoxing wine for the toss**, the **ground cumin**, the **Sichuan chili flakes**, and the **white sesame seeds** in one cup.
5. Cut the **scallions** into 1 in lengths. Put them in that cup.

## Batter

Do this while the fish marinates.

- 1/2 cup potato starch, for the batter
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the batter
- 1/3 cup cold water
- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the batter

1. Stir the **potato starch for the batter**, the **all-purpose flour**, the **baking powder**, and the **kosher salt for the batter** together.
2. Add the **cold water** and the **peanut oil for the batter**. Stir until the batter is a thick paste and a ribbon off the spoon sits on the surface.

## Fry

- 1/3 cup cold water
- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the batter

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, cut into 3/4 in pieces, marinated

1. Put the **peanut oil for frying** in a deep heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high. Stay with the thermometer. Heat until it reads 350°F, about 6 minutes. Pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to the setting that holds 350°F. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on.
2. When the oil is ready and the fish has finished its 15 minutes, pat the **marinated snakehead fillet** dry. Toss it with the **potato starch for the dust** until the surface looks tacky.
3. Stir the batter. Turn 6 oz of the fish in it until each piece wears a heavy coat. Lift the pieces and let the extra drip off.
4. Add that 6 oz at once. Leave the pieces still until the crust has set and turned faintly yellow, about 2 minutes. If the crust browns hard before then, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the oil returns to 350°F, turn the coil back to the setting that holds it and set the saucepan fully back on. Turn any pale side.
5. Fry until the crust is pale gold. Break one piece open. The center is just opaque, about 1 minute more. If the center is still translucent, fry 30 seconds more. Lift the fish out with a slotted spoon.
6. Let the oil return to 350°F on the setting that holds it. Coat and fry the remaining 6 oz the same way. Lift them out.
7. Turn the coil up and leave the saucepan fully on. Stay with the thermometer. When it reads 375°F, pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to the setting that holds 375°F. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on.
8. Return the first 6 oz. Fry until the crust is deep gold and crisp, about 30 seconds. If the oil climbs past 375°F, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the oil returns to 375°F, turn the coil back to the setting that holds it and set the saucepan fully back on. Lift them out.
9. Let the oil return to 375°F on the setting that holds it. Fry the remaining 6 oz until the crust is deep gold and crisp, about 30 seconds. If the oil climbs past 375°F, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the oil returns to 375°F, turn the coil back to the setting that holds it and set the saucepan fully back on. Lift them out.
10. Turn the coil off. Pour the oil slowly into a heatproof bowl until 2 tbsp remains in the saucepan.

## Toss

These amounts were measured above.

**Fish**

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, cut into 3/4 in pieces, fried

**Prep**

- 2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1 1/2 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the toss
- 3 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths
- 1 tsp ground cumin (孜然)
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

**Fry**

- 3 cups peanut oil, for frying, all but 2 tbsp poured off

1. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium-low. Pour in the remaining **peanut oil for frying**.
2. When the oil shimmers, add the **Sichuan peppercorns** and the **dried facing-heaven chilies** together. Stir until the chilies smell toasty and turn one shade darker, still red, about 30 seconds. If they blacken, turn the coil off and stir until they cool. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-low.
3. Add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until fragrant, about 15 seconds.
4. Add the **fried snakehead fillet**, the **sugar**, the **Shaoxing wine for the toss**, the **scallions**, the **ground cumin**, the **Sichuan chili flakes**, and the **white sesame seeds** together. Toss until the wine cooks off and the crust is still crisp, about 20 seconds.
5. Eat the fish and the scallions. The chilies and the peppercorns stay on the plate for their aroma.
