# Salted egg yolk shrimp (蛋黄焗明虾)

Serves 2. About 30 minutes.

The yolks steam in a heavy saucepan. The sear is the wok on the Mosaic at 375°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Steam the yolks. Season the shrimp while they steam. Sear when the yolks are mashed.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Yolks

Use the yolks from salted duck eggs.

- 2 cups water, for steaming

<!-- cup -->

- 3 salted duck egg yolks (咸蛋黄)

1. Put the **water for steaming** in a heavy saucepan. Set the saucepan on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. When the water boils, pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to low. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on. The surface should shiver.
2. Set a rack in the saucepan so it stands above the water. If you have no rack, set a small heatproof cup upside down in the water. Set a small heatproof plate on the rack or the cup so the plate stays dry. Put the **salted duck egg yolks** on the plate. Cover the saucepan. If the plate gets wet, the yolks mash instead of crumbling. Go on to the shrimp while it steams. A yolk that is already firm may be ready in about 2 minutes. When a yolk breaks into dry sandy crumbs when pressed, about 10 minutes, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. If you are mid-cut, finish that piece first.
3. Mash the **salted duck egg yolks** with a spoon until they are a fine paste.

## Shrimp

Use large peeled shrimp with the tails off, and unsalted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp potato starch

1. If a dark vein remains along the back of a shrimp, pull it out. Pat the **large shrimp** dry.
2. Add the **Shaoxing wine** and the **ground white pepper**. Stir until the shrimp look moist.
3. Add the **potato starch**. Toss until each shrimp has a thin coat.

## Toss

Do this before the wok is hot.

- 2 tbsp peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, coated

<!-- cup -->

- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 3 salted duck egg yolks (咸蛋黄), steamed and mashed
- 1/4 tsp sugar

<!-- cup -->

- 1 scallion, chopped

1. Mince the **garlic** with the cleaver. Chop the **scallion**. If a yolk has not broken into sandy crumbs, wait until it does, then pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. If the yolks are still whole, mash them until they are a fine paste. Put each group into one cup or pile.
2. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, add half of the **coated large shrimp** in one layer in the bottom. Cook until the undersides are pink, about 30 seconds. Turn them. Cook until they curl in a loose C and the center is just opaque, about 30 seconds more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. Scoop them onto a plate before they curl into a tight ring or brown hard.
3. When the oil shimmers, cook the remaining **coated large shrimp** the same way and scoop them onto the plate.
4. Turn the left dial to 300°F. When the oil shimmers and has stopped smoking, add the **garlic**, the **steamed and mashed salted duck egg yolks**, and the **sugar** together. Stir and press until the yolks foam and look sandy, about 1 minute. If they smell sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start.
5. Return the **coated large shrimp**. Toss until every shrimp wears an even gold coat, about 30 seconds.
6. Add the **scallion**. Toss until it smells sweet, about 15 seconds. Press on/off. Eat it hot.

---

Add 1 tbsp unsalted butter with the garlic, the steamed and mashed salted duck egg yolks, and the sugar.

For a looser coat, add 1 tbsp unsweetened evaporated milk with the garlic, the steamed and mashed salted duck egg yolks, and the sugar, instead of the butter.
