# Salted egg yolk shrimp (蛋黄焗明虾)

Serves 2. About 30 minutes.

The yolks steam in a heavy saucepan. The shrimp cook in a 10 in heavy skillet on the coil.

Steam the yolks. Season the shrimp while they steam. Sear when the yolks are mashed.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Yolks**

- 2 cups water, for steaming
- 3 salted duck egg yolks (咸蛋黄)

**Shrimp**

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tbsp potato starch

**Skillet**

- 2 tbsp peanut oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1 scallion, chopped

---

## Yolks

Use the yolks from salted duck eggs.

- 2 cups water, for steaming
- 3 salted duck egg yolks (咸蛋黄)

1. Put the **water for steaming** in a heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high.
2. When the water boils, set a small heatproof bowl in the saucepan so the bowl sits in the water and the water stays outside it. Put the **salted duck egg yolks** in the bowl. Cover. Steam until a yolk breaks into sandy crumbs when pressed, about 10 minutes. A yolk that is already firm may be ready in about 2 minutes. Go on to the shrimp while it steams.
3. When a yolk breaks into sandy crumbs, mash the **salted duck egg yolks** with a spoon until they are a fine paste. If you are mid-cut, finish that piece first.

## Shrimp

Use large peeled shrimp with the tails off. Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tbsp potato starch

1. If a dark vein remains along the back of a shrimp, pull it out. Pat the **large shrimp** dry.
2. Add the **Shaoxing wine** and the **ground white pepper**. Stir until the shrimp look moist.
3. Add the **potato starch**. Toss until each shrimp has a thin coat.

## Skillet

Do this before the skillet is hot.

- 2 tbsp peanut oil
- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, coated
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 3 salted duck egg yolks (咸蛋黄), steamed and mashed
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1 scallion, chopped

1. Mince the **garlic**. Chop the **scallion**. If the yolks are still whole, mash them until they are a fine paste.
2. Put a 10 in heavy skillet on a coil turned to medium. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, about 1 minute, add half of the **coated large shrimp** in one layer. Cook until the undersides are pink, about 30 seconds. Turn them. Cook until they curl in a loose C and the center is just opaque, about 30 seconds more. If they curl into a tight ring or brown hard, pull the skillet half off the coil. Scoop them onto a plate.
3. When the oil shimmers again, cook the remaining **coated large shrimp** the same way. Scoop them onto the plate.
4. Pull the skillet off the coil. Turn the coil off. When it stops glowing, turn it to medium-low and set the skillet back. Add the **garlic**, the **steamed and mashed salted duck egg yolks**, and the **sugar**. Stir and press until the yolks foam and look sandy, about 1 minute. If they smell sharp, pull the skillet off the coil and stir until the smell sweetens, then set it back.
5. Return the **coated large shrimp**. Toss until every shrimp wears an even gold coat, about 30 seconds.
6. Turn the coil off. Add the **scallion**. Toss until it smells sweet, about 15 seconds.
7. Eat it hot.
