# Clear-oil water-boiled fish (清油水煮鱼)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

Instant Pot rice, 1 cup water to 1 cup rice, high pressure for 3 minutes. The broth in a heavy saucepan. The pour oil in a small skillet.

Start the rice, then marinate the fish. While it sits, cut the chilies and the aromatics. Start the pour oil while the broth heats. Poach the fish when that oil is nearly ready. When the Instant Pot beeps, turn the valve to venting and leave the lid on until you serve.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Rice**

- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 cup water

**Fish**

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the fish
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper, for the fish
- 1 egg white
- 1 tbsp sweet potato starch (红薯淀粉)
- 2 tsp fragrant rapeseed oil (浓香菜籽油), for the fish

**Prep**

- 8 oz soybean sprouts (黄豆芽)
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced
- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, cut into 2 in lengths
- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tbsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)

**Broth**

- 3 cups unsalted chicken stock
- 1 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the broth
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, for the broth
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the broth
- 1/2 cup fragrant rapeseed oil (浓香菜籽油), for the pour

---

## Rice

- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 cup water

1. Rinse the **jasmine rice** under cold water until the runoff is almost clear. Drain it well.
2. Put the rice and the **water** in the Instant Pot and stir once.
3. Lock the lid, set the valve to sealing, and pressure cook on high for 3 minutes. Go on to the fish.
4. When it beeps, turn the valve to venting. Leave the lid on until you serve, then fluff the rice.

## Fish

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the fish
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper, for the fish
- 1 egg white
- 1 tbsp sweet potato starch (红薯淀粉)
- 2 tsp fragrant rapeseed oil (浓香菜籽油), for the fish

1. If the **snakehead fillet** still has skin, cut the skin off. Slice the fillet on a bias, 1/8 in thick.
2. Rinse the slices under cold water until the water runs clear. Pat them dry.
3. Add the **Shaoxing wine for the fish**, the **kosher salt for the fish**, and the **ground white pepper for the fish** together. Stir until the slices feel sticky.
4. Add the **egg white** and stir until every slice looks slick.
5. Add the **sweet potato starch** and stir until each slice has a thin coat.
6. Drizzle the **fragrant rapeseed oil for the fish** over the top and turn the slices once. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the prep. The fish can wait longer if the broth is not ready yet.

## Prep

Do this while the fish marinates.

- 8 oz soybean sprouts (黄豆芽)
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced
- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, cut into 2 in lengths
- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tbsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)

1. Rinse the **soybean sprouts**.
2. Slice the **ginger**.
3. Separate the whites and the greens of the **scallions**, and cut both into 2 in lengths. Keep the whites and the greens in two piles.
4. Slice the **garlic** thin.
5. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds.
6. Put the **green Sichuan peppercorns** in a cup. Set the cups next to a large heatproof bowl.

## Broth

- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced
- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, cut into 2 in lengths
- 3 cups unsalted chicken stock
- 1 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the broth
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, for the broth
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the broth
- 1/2 cup fragrant rapeseed oil (浓香菜籽油), for the pour
- 8 oz soybean sprouts (黄豆芽)

1. Put the **ginger**, the whites of the **scallions**, the **unsalted chicken stock**, the **kosher salt for the broth**, the **ground white pepper for the broth**, and the **Shaoxing wine for the broth** in a heavy saucepan together. Bring them to a simmer over high heat. When the center boils hard, pull the saucepan so it sits half on the coil.
2. While the stock heats, put the **fragrant rapeseed oil for the pour** in a small skillet on another coil turned to medium. If a wooden spoon handle makes small bubbles before the fish is in the bowl, pull that skillet off the coil.
3. Add the **soybean sprouts** to the simmering broth. Cook until they soften but still snap, about 1 minute. Lift them into the heatproof bowl.
4. Keep the broth at a shiver. If it rolls again, pull the saucepan so it sits half on the coil. Go on to the pour.

## Pour

These amounts were measured above.

**Fish**

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick, marinated

**Prep**

- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, cut into 2 in lengths
- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tbsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)

**Broth**

- 1/2 cup fragrant rapeseed oil (浓香菜籽油), for the pour

1. Slide the **snakehead fillet** into the shivering broth, one slice at a time. Don't stir. Skim off any foam. When the slices are white and the edges have curled, about 1 minute, pour the broth and the fish over the sprouts.
2. Set the small skillet back on the coil if it is off. The **fragrant rapeseed oil for the pour** is ready when a wooden spoon handle makes small bubbles and the oil does not smoke. If the handle stays quiet, leave it there until the bubbles come. If it is smoking, pull the skillet off the coil until the smoke stops, then set it back for a few seconds.
3. Scatter the greens of the **scallions**, the **garlic**, the **dried facing-heaven chilies**, and the **green Sichuan peppercorns** over the fish.
4. Pour the **fragrant rapeseed oil for the pour** over those aromatics. It should crackle as it lands. Serve with the rice. Eat the fish, the sprouts, and the scallion greens. The chilies and the peppercorns stay in the bowl for their aroma.
