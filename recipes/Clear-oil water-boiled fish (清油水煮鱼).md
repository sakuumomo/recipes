# Clear-oil water-boiled fish (清油水煮鱼)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The broth in a heavy saucepan. The pour oil in a small skillet.

Marinate the fish. While it sits, cut the chilies and the aromatics. Start the pour oil while the broth heats. Poach the fish when that oil is nearly ready.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Fish**

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the fish
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper, for the fish
- 1 egg white
- 1 tbsp sweet potato starch (红薯淀粉)
- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the fish

**Prep**

- 8 oz soybean sprouts (黄豆芽)
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced
- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, cut into 2 in lengths
- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tbsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)

**Broth**

- 3 cups unsalted chicken stock
- 1 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the broth
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, for the broth
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the broth
- 1/2 cup peanut oil, for the pour

## Fish

Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the fish
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper, for the fish
- 1 egg white
- 1 tbsp sweet potato starch (红薯淀粉)
- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the fish

1. If the **snakehead fillet** still has skin, cut the skin off. Slice the fillet on a bias, 1/8 in thick.
2. Rinse the slices under cold water until the water runs clear. Pat them dry.
3. Add the **Shaoxing wine for the fish**, the **kosher salt for the fish**, and the **ground white pepper for the fish** together. Stir until the slices feel sticky.
4. Add the **egg white** and stir until every slice looks slick.
5. Add the **sweet potato starch** and stir until each slice has a thin coat.
6. Drizzle the **peanut oil for the fish** over the top and turn the slices once. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the prep. The fish can wait longer if the broth is not ready yet.

## Prep

Do this while the fish marinates.

- 8 oz soybean sprouts (黄豆芽)
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced
- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, cut into 2 in lengths
- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tbsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)

1. Rinse the **soybean sprouts**.
2. Slice the **ginger**.
3. Separate the whites and the greens of the **scallions**, and cut both into 2 in lengths. Keep the whites and the greens in two piles.
4. Slice the **garlic** thin.
5. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds.
6. Put the **green Sichuan peppercorns** in a cup. Set the cups next to a large heatproof bowl.

## Broth

- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced
- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, cut into 2 in lengths
- 3 cups unsalted chicken stock
- 1 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the broth
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, for the broth
- 2 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the broth
- 1/2 cup peanut oil, for the pour
- 8 oz soybean sprouts (黄豆芽)

1. Put the **ginger**, the whites of the **scallions**, the **unsalted chicken stock**, the **kosher salt for the broth**, the **ground white pepper for the broth**, and the **Shaoxing wine for the broth** in a heavy saucepan together. Set the saucepan on high. Stay with it. When it boils, pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to low. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on. The surface should shiver.
2. Add the **soybean sprouts** to the simmering broth. Cook until they soften but still snap, about 1 minute. Lift them into the heatproof bowl.
3. Keep the broth at a shiver. If it rolls again, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the surface settles, turn the coil back to low and set the saucepan fully back on. If it rolls again, do the same again. Go on to the pour.

## Pour

These amounts were measured above.

**Fish**

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick, marinated

**Prep**

- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, cut into 2 in lengths
- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tbsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)

**Broth**

- 1/2 cup peanut oil, for the pour

1. Slide in half of the **snakehead fillet**, a few slices at a time. Don't stir. Skim off any foam. When the slices are white and the edges have curled, about 30 seconds, lift them onto the sprouts. If a slice is still translucent, leave it 15 seconds more. Cook the remaining half the same way. Pour the broth over the sprouts.
2. Put the **peanut oil for the pour** in a small skillet. Set it on a coil turned to medium. Stay with it. The oil is ready when a dry wooden spoon handle makes small bubbles and the oil has not smoked, about 3 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil and turn that coil off. If the oil is smoking, wait until the smoke stops. Leave the skillet off the coil.
3. Scatter the greens of the **scallions**, the **garlic**, the **dried facing-heaven chilies**, and the **green Sichuan peppercorns** over the fish.
4. Pour the **peanut oil for the pour** over those aromatics. It should crackle as it lands. Eat the fish, the sprouts, and the scallion greens. The chilies and the peppercorns stay in the bowl for their aroma.
