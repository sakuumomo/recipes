# Spicy shrimp with potatoes (一品香辣虾)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The potatoes soak in a bowl. The shrimp and the potatoes fry on the Mosaic at 350°F, then the toss stays in that wok. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Soak the potatoes. Season the shrimp and cut the aromatics while they soak. When the timer rings, drain the potatoes and pat them dry, then keep going. Fry the shrimp, then the potatoes. Ladle the spare oil out and toss as soon as the potatoes come out.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

**Shrimp**

## Potatoes

Use a waxy potato so the pieces stay whole.

- 1 potato
- 2 cups cold water, for soaking

1. Peel the **potato**. Cut it into 1/2 in batons with the 8 in cleaver.
2. Put the batons in a bowl and cover them with the **cold water for soaking**. Set a timer for 10 minutes. Go on to the shrimp.
3. When the timer rings, drain the **potato** and pat the batons very dry. If you are mid-cut, finish that piece first.

## Shrimp

Use large peeled shrimp with the tails off, and unsalted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper

1. If a dark vein remains along the back of a shrimp, pull it out. Pat the **large shrimp** dry.
2. Add the **Shaoxing wine**, the **kosher salt**, and the **ground white pepper**. Stir until the shrimp look moist.

## Prep

Do this while the potatoes soak. Use plain red chili oil, and whole dried erjingtiao marked 二荆条.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the toss

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1/2 in ginger, sliced
- 6 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 1 handful Chinese celery (香芹), cut into 1 1/2 in lengths
- 1/2 cup roasted salted peanuts
- 1 scallion, cut into 1 in lengths

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp chili oil (红油)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

1. With the 8 in cleaver, slice the **garlic** and the **ginger**. Snip the **dried erjingtiao** in half and shake out the loose seeds.
2. With the 8 in cleaver, cut the **Chinese celery** into 1 1/2 in lengths and the **scallion** into 1 in lengths.
3. Put the **peanut oil for the toss** in one cup. Put the **garlic**, the **ginger**, and the **dried erjingtiao** in another. Put the **Chinese celery**, the **roasted salted peanuts**, and the **scallion** in another. Put the **light soy sauce**, the **sugar**, the **chili oil**, and the **toasted sesame oil** in another. Put the **white sesame seeds** in another.

## Fry

- 3 cups peanut oil, for frying

<!-- cup -->

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, seasoned

<!-- cup -->

- 1 potato, drained

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 350°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Add the **peanut oil for frying**. Stay with the thermometer. Keep the probe in the oil and off the steel. Heat until the oil reads 350°F. If the oil stalls short of 350°F, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, turn the left dial up another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When the oil returns to 350°F, press start.
2. Slide in half of the **seasoned large shrimp** so the oil covers them. Fry until they are pink, curled in a loose C, and the center is just opaque, about 45 seconds. If a shrimp curls into a tight ring, lift it out. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
3. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Fry the remaining **seasoned large shrimp** the same way. Lift them out.
4. If the timer has not rung, wait until it does. If the **potato** is still soaking, drain it and pat the batons very dry.
5. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Slide in half of the **drained potato** so the oil covers them. Leave the batons still for 1 minute so the outsides set, then turn them. Fry until the outside is gold and a baton is soft in the middle, about 4 minutes. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
6. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Fry the remaining **drained potato** the same way. Lift them out.
7. Use the wok ladle to move the spare **peanut oil for frying** into a heatproof bowl until a thin film is left. Leave the wok seated.

## Toss

Do this as soon as the potatoes come out.

These amounts were measured above.

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the toss

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1/2 in ginger, sliced
- 6 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 1 handful Chinese celery (香芹), cut into 1 1/2 in lengths
- 1/2 cup roasted salted peanuts
- 1 scallion, cut into 1 in lengths

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp chili oil (红油)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

**Potatoes**

- 1 potato, fried

**Shrimp**

- 10 oz large shrimp, peeled and deveined, fried

1. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **peanut oil for the toss**. When it shimmers, about 30 seconds, add the **garlic**, the **ginger**, and the **dried erjingtiao** together. Stir until the garlic smells sweet and the chilies turn one shade darker, still red, about 30 seconds. If they blacken, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
2. Add the **Chinese celery**, the **roasted salted peanuts**, and the **scallion**. Stir until the celery is bright green, about 30 seconds.
3. Add the **light soy sauce**, the **sugar**, the **chili oil**, and the **toasted sesame oil** together. Stir until the soy bubbles, about 10 seconds.
4. Add the **fried potato** and the **fried large shrimp** together. Toss until every piece looks glossy and the shrimp are still tender, about 30 seconds. If the shrimp have cooled, toss until they are hot again, about 30 seconds more. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精).
5. Press on/off. Add the **white sesame seeds**. Toss until they stick, about 10 seconds.
6. Scrape it onto a plate. Set the wok on its stand. Eat it hot.
