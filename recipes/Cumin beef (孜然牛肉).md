# Cumin beef (孜然牛肉)

Serves 2. About 40 minutes.

The beef marinates in a bowl. The peppercorns toast in a small skillet. The stir-fry is in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Marinate the beef first. Toast the peppercorns and cut the onion while it rests. Stir-fry when the beef looks tacky.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Beef**

- 10 oz flank steak
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp water, for the beef
- 1 tsp potato starch
- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the beef

**Sichuan pepper**

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

**Stir-fry**

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry
- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the spices
- 1 tsp cumin seeds (孜然)
- 6 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 2 tsp ginger, minced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 small red onion, cut into 1/2 in pieces
- 1 tbsp ground cumin (孜然)
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

## Beef

Use flank steak so the slices soften in this time.

- 10 oz flank steak
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp water, for the beef
- 1 tsp potato starch
- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the beef

1. Slice the **flank steak** against the grain, 1/4 in thick and about 2 in long.
2. Add the **kosher salt**, the **Shaoxing wine**, the **light soy sauce**, the **water for the beef**, the **potato starch**, and the **peanut oil for the beef**. Stir in one direction until the beef is tacky and no liquid pools in the bowl, about 1 minute.
3. Rest 15 minutes. Go on to the Sichuan pepper.

## Sichuan pepper

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

1. Toast the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a small skillet over medium-low heat, shaking often, until they smell citrusy, about 2 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil if they start to smoke.
2. Cool them 1 minute. Put them in a small bowl and crush them to a powder with the back of a spoon.

## Stir-fry

Do this before the wok is hot.

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry
- 10 oz flank steak, marinated
- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the spices
- 1 tsp cumin seeds (孜然)
- 6 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 2 tsp ginger, minced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 small red onion, cut into 1/2 in pieces
- 1 tbsp ground cumin (孜然)
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒), toasted and crushed
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

1. Put each ingredient into its own cup or pile, prepared the way the line says. Set the marinated beef and the crushed peppercorns with them.
2. If the beef is not tacky yet, wait. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to high. Heat it until a drop of water flicked into the center vanishes, about 5 minutes.
3. Add 1 tbsp of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry**. Swirl it over the bottom. Add half of the **marinated flank steak** in one layer. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 45 seconds. Toss until the pink is gone, about 45 seconds more. Scoop it onto a plate. If the oil smokes, turn the coil off and stir until the smoke stops, then turn the coil back to high. When the oil shimmers, keep going.
4. Add the rest of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** and the rest of the **marinated flank steak**. Cook the beef the same way, about 1 minute. Scoop it onto the plate.
5. Turn the coil off. When it stops glowing, turn it to medium-low. Add the **peanut oil for the spices**, the **cumin seeds**, and the **dried facing-heaven chilies** together. Stir until the seeds smell toasty and the chilies turn one shade darker, still red, about 20 seconds. If they blacken, turn the coil off and stir until they cool, then turn the coil back to medium-low.
6. Add the **ginger** and the **garlic** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 15 seconds.
7. Add the **red onion**. Stir until the edges soften and the pieces still have bite, about 1 minute.
8. Add the **marinated flank steak**, the **ground cumin**, the **Sichuan chili flakes**, the **sugar**, and the **toasted and crushed Sichuan peppercorns** together. Toss until the spices cling and the beef smells toasty, about 30 seconds.
9. Turn the coil off. Add the **cilantro**. Toss until it wilts, about 15 seconds.
10. Eat it hot.
