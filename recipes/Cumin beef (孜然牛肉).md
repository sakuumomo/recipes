# Cumin beef (孜然牛肉)

Serves 2. About 40 minutes.

The beef marinates in a bowl. The peppercorns toast in a small skillet. The stir-fry is on the Mosaic at 500°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Marinate the beef first. Toast the peppercorns and cut the onion while it rests. Stir-fry when the beef looks tacky.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Beef

Use flank steak so the slices soften in this time, and unsalted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz flank steak
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp water, for the beef
- 1 tsp potato starch
- 2 tsp peanut oil, for the beef

1. Slice the **flank steak** against the grain with the 8 in cleaver, 1/4 in thick and about 2 in long.
2. Add the **kosher salt**, the **Shaoxing wine**, the **light soy sauce**, the **water for the beef**, the **potato starch**, and the **peanut oil for the beef**. Stir in one direction until the beef is tacky and no liquid pools in the bowl, about 1 minute.
3. Rest 15 minutes. Go on to the Sichuan pepper.

## Sichuan pepper

- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

1. Toast the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a small skillet over medium-low heat, shaking often, until they smell citrusy, about 2 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil and turn that coil off.
2. Cool them 1 minute. Put them in a small bowl and crush them to a powder with the back of a spoon.

## Stir-fry

Do this before the wok is hot. Use whole dried erjingtiao marked 二荆条, and medium 二荆条 flakes marked 中辣.

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry

- 10 oz flank steak, marinated

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the spices

- 2 tsp ginger, minced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced

- 1 small red onion, cut into 1/2 in pieces

- 1 tbsp ground cumin (孜然)
- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒), toasted and crushed

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

1. With the 8 in cleaver, put each group into one cup or pile, prepared the way the line says. Set the marinated beef and the crushed peppercorns with them.
2. If the beef is not tacky yet, wait. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 500°F. Heat the empty wok until a drop of water flicked into the center vanishes.
3. Add 1 tbsp of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry**. Swirl it over the bottom. Add half of the **marinated flank steak** in one layer. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 30 seconds. Toss until the pink is gone, about 30 seconds more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. Scoop it onto a plate.
4. Add the rest of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry**. Swirl it over the bottom. Add the rest of the **marinated flank steak** in one layer. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 30 seconds. Toss until the pink is gone, about 30 seconds more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. Scoop it onto the plate.
5. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **peanut oil for the spices**. When it shimmers and has stopped smoking, about 30 seconds, go on.
6. Add the **ginger** and the **garlic** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 15 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
7. Add the **red onion**. Stir until the edges soften and the pieces still have bite, about 1 minute.
8. Add the **cumin seeds** and the **dried erjingtiao** together. Stir until the seeds smell toasty and the pods turn one shade darker, still red, about 20 seconds. If they blacken, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
9. Add the **marinated flank steak**, the **ground cumin**, the **Sichuan chili flakes**, the **sugar**, and the **toasted and crushed Sichuan peppercorns** together. Toss until the spices cling and the beef smells toasty, about 30 seconds.
10. Press on/off. Add the **cilantro**. Toss until it wilts, about 15 seconds. Eat it hot.
