# Hualiu fish slices (滑溜鱼片)

Serves 2. About 40 minutes.

The wood ear soaks in a bowl. Slip on the Mosaic at 275°F. The glaze is in that wok at 300°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Soak the wood ear. Slice and marinate the fish. While it sits, finish the prep. When the wood ear timer rings, drain and tear it, then keep going. Heat the slip oil and stay with it. Slip the fish, ladle the spare oil out, and glaze the slices while they are still soft.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Wood ear

- 1 handful dried wood ear (干黑木耳)
- 2 cups hot water

1. Put the **dried wood ear** in a bowl and cover it with the **hot water**. Set a timer for 20 minutes. Go on to the fish.
2. When the timer rings, drain the **dried wood ear** and rinse it. Pinch off any hard knobs and tear the pieces into bites. If you are mid-cut, finish that piece first.

## Fish

Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the fish
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, for the fish

<!-- cup -->

- 1 egg white

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tsp sweet potato starch (红薯淀粉), for the fish

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the fish

1. If the **snakehead fillet** still has skin, cut the skin off. Slice it with the boning knife on a bias into pieces about 2 in long and 1/8 in thick.
2. Rinse the slices under cold water until the water runs clear. Pat them dry.
3. Add the **Shaoxing wine for the fish**, the **kosher salt for the fish**, and the **ground white pepper for the fish** together. Stir until the slices feel sticky.
4. Add the **egg white** and stir until every slice looks slick.
5. Add the **sweet potato starch for the fish** and stir until each slice has a thin coat.
6. Drizzle the **peanut oil for the fish** over the top and turn the slices once. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the prep.

## Prep

Do this while the fish marinates. When the wood ear timer rings, drain and tear the wood ear, then keep going.

- 1 in ginger, sliced
- 1 scallion, cut into 1 in lengths

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 cup unsalted chicken stock
- 1/4 cup winter bamboo shoots (冬笋), sliced thin
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the sauce
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the sauce
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, for the sauce

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp sweet potato starch (红薯淀粉), for the sauce
- 1 tbsp cold water

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

1. Slice the **ginger** and cut the **scallion** into 1 in lengths with the 8 in cleaver. Put them in one cup.
2. If the **winter bamboo shoots** still have a husk, peel it off. If they are canned or packed in water, rinse them. Slice them thin with the 8 in cleaver.
3. Put the **unsalted chicken stock**, the **winter bamboo shoots**, the **Shaoxing wine for the sauce**, the **kosher salt for the sauce**, and the **ground white pepper for the sauce** in one cup.
4. Stir the **sweet potato starch for the sauce** and the **cold water** together in a small bowl until the liquid is smooth. Stir it again just before it goes into the wok.
5. Put the **toasted sesame oil** in a spoon.

## Slip

- 4 cups peanut oil, for slipping

<!-- cup -->

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick, marinated

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 275°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Pour in the **peanut oil for slipping**. Stay with the thermometer. Keep the probe in the oil and off the steel. Heat until the oil reads 275°F and the fish has finished its 15 minutes. If the oil stalls short of 275°F, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, turn the left dial up another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 275°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When the oil returns to 275°F, press start.
2. Slide in half of the **marinated snakehead fillet**. Keep the slices in the bottom, where the oil covers them. Nudge them apart. When they are white and still soft, with no browning, about 30 seconds, lift them out with a slotted spoon onto a plate. If a slice is still translucent, leave it 15 seconds more.
3. Bring the oil back to 275°F. Slip the remaining half of the **marinated snakehead fillet** the same way. Lift them out onto the plate.

## Glaze

These amounts were measured above.

**Slip**

- 4 cups peanut oil, for slipping

**Prep**

- 1 in ginger, sliced
- 1 scallion, cut into 1 in lengths

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 cup unsalted chicken stock
- 1/4 cup winter bamboo shoots (冬笋), sliced thin
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the sauce
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the sauce
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, for the sauce

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp sweet potato starch (红薯淀粉), for the sauce
- 1 tbsp cold water

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

**Wood ear**

- 1 handful dried wood ear (干黑木耳), soaked

**Fish**

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick, slipped

1. Use the wok ladle to move the spare **peanut oil for slipping** into a heatproof bowl until a thin film is left. Leave the wok seated.
2. Turn the left dial to 300°F. When the film shimmers and has stopped smoking, about 30 seconds, add the **ginger** and the **scallion** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 20 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start. Lift them out with cooking chopsticks.
3. Add the **unsalted chicken stock**, the **winter bamboo shoots**, the **soaked dried wood ear**, the **Shaoxing wine for the sauce**, the **kosher salt for the sauce**, and the **ground white pepper for the sauce** together. Simmer until the bamboo is hot, about 1 minute. If it is still hard and bright, simmer another minute. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles, turn the left dial down 10°F, and press start. If the surface goes still, turn the left dial up 10°F.
4. Slide in the **slipped snakehead fillet**. Fold once or twice until every slice is heated through and still soft, about 30 seconds.
5. Stir the **sweet potato starch for the sauce** and the **cold water**. Pour in about half and fold. Add the rest only if the sauce does not yet flow off a spoon in a thin ribbon. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精).
6. Add the **toasted sesame oil** and stir until it sheets through the sauce. Press on/off. Serve.
