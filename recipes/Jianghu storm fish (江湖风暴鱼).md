# Jianghu storm fish (江湖风暴鱼)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The vegetables and the fish poach in a heavy saucepan. The oil heats in a small skillet. The fish finishes on the vegetables in a heatproof bowl.

Marinate the fish. Snip the chilies while it sits. Blanch the vegetables when the fish is ready, then poach the slices and pour the hot oil.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Fish

Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 tsp ground cumin (孜然), for the fish

<!-- cup -->

- 1 egg white

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the fish

1. If the **snakehead fillet** still has skin, cut the skin off. Slice the fillet on a bias into pieces about 2 in long and 1/8 in thick.
2. Rinse the slices under cold water until the water runs clear. Pat them dry.
3. Add the **kosher salt**, the **Shaoxing wine**, the **ground white pepper**, and the **ground cumin for the fish** together. Stir until the slices feel sticky.
4. Add the **egg white** and stir until every slice looks slick.
5. Add the **potato starch** and stir until each slice has a thin coat.
6. Drizzle the **peanut oil for the fish** over the top and turn the slices once. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the vegetables.

## Vegetables

Use the thick stem of celtuce, and fresh shiitake.

- 6 cups water, for blanching
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the blanch

<!-- cup -->

- 4 oz celtuce stem (莴笋)
- 2 oz shiitake mushrooms (香菇)
- 2 oz onion, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 3 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇)

1. Put the **water for blanching** and the **kosher salt for the blanch** in a heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. If it boils before the vegetables are cut, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off.
2. Peel the **celtuce stem** until the pale flesh shows. Slice it 1/4 in thick, in pieces about 2 in long.
3. Trim the base off the **enoki mushrooms**. Tear them into small bunches and rinse them.
4. Slice the **shiitake mushrooms** thin. Slice the **onion** thin.
5. Pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. Go on to the storm oil.
6. When the storm oil is done, set the saucepan on high and stay with it. When the water boils, if the fish has not finished its 15 minutes, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off.
7. When the fish has finished its 15 minutes, set the saucepan on high and stay with it until the water boils. Add the **celtuce stem**, the **shiitake mushrooms**, and the **onion** together. Cook until the celtuce is crisp-tender and the shiitake have softened, about 2 minutes. Lift them out and let them drain.
8. Add the **enoki mushrooms**. Lift them out as soon as they wilt, about 15 seconds. Longer than that and they turn to slime.

## Storm oil

Do this while the fish marinates.

- 6 tbsp peanut oil, for the pour

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1 tsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds (孜然), for the oil
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp ginger, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 scallion, chopped
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

1. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds.
2. Slice the **garlic**. Slice the **ginger**. Chop the **scallion**.
3. Put the **peanut oil for the pour** in one cup. Put the **dried facing-heaven chilies**, the **Sichuan peppercorns**, the **green Sichuan peppercorns**, the **cumin seeds for the oil**, the **garlic**, and the **ginger** in another. Put the **toasted sesame oil** in another. Put the **scallion** and the **white sesame seeds** in another.

## Pour

These amounts were measured above.

**Fish**

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick, marinated

**Vegetables**

- 6 cups water, for blanching

<!-- cup -->

- 4 oz celtuce stem (莴笋), blanched
- 2 oz shiitake mushrooms (香菇), blanched
- 2 oz onion, sliced thin, blanched
- 3 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇), blanched

**Storm oil**

- 6 tbsp peanut oil, for the pour

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1 tsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds (孜然), for the oil
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp ginger, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 scallion, chopped
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

1. Pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to low. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on. The **water for blanching** should shiver. If the surface is still, turn the coil one setting higher and leave the saucepan fully on. Slide in half of the **marinated snakehead fillet**, a few slices at a time. When the slices are white and firm, about 30 seconds, lift them out with a slotted spoon. If a slice is still translucent, leave it 15 seconds more. Cook the remaining half the same way.
2. Spread the **blanched celtuce stem**, the **blanched enoki mushrooms**, the **blanched shiitake mushrooms**, and the **blanched onion** in a wide heatproof bowl. Lay the slices over them in one overlapping layer.
3. Put the **peanut oil for the pour** in a small skillet. Set it on a coil turned to medium. The oil is ready when a dry wooden spoon handle makes small bubbles and the oil does not smoke, about 3 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil and turn that coil off. If the oil is smoking, let it cool until the smoke stops before the spices go in. Add the **dried facing-heaven chilies**, the **Sichuan peppercorns**, the **green Sichuan peppercorns**, the **cumin seeds for the oil**, the **garlic**, and the **ginger** together. Stir until the seeds smell toasty and the chilies turn one shade darker, still red, about 15 seconds. If they blacken, keep stirring until they cool.
4. Pour the **peanut oil for the pour**, spices and all, evenly over the slices.
5. Drizzle the **toasted sesame oil** over the top. Scatter the **scallion** and the **white sesame seeds**. Eat it hot.
