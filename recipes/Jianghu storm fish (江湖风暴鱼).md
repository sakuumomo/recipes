# Jianghu storm fish (江湖风暴鱼)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The vegetables and the fish poach in a heavy saucepan. The oil heats in a small skillet. The fish finishes in a heatproof bowl.

Marinate the fish. While it sits, cut the vegetables and set out the storm oil. When the timer rings, blanch the vegetables, poach the slices, and pour the oil.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Fish

Use salted Shaoxing.

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒)
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin (孜然), for the fish

<!-- cup -->

- 1 egg white

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tsp potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the fish

1. If the **snakehead fillet** still has skin, cut the skin off with the boning knife. Slice the fillet on a bias into pieces about 2 in long and 1/8 in thick.
2. Rinse the slices under cold water until the water runs clear. Pat them dry.
3. Add the **kosher salt**, the **Shaoxing wine**, the **ground white pepper**, and the **ground cumin for the fish** together. Stir until the slices feel sticky.
4. Add the **egg white** and stir until every slice looks slick.
5. Add the **potato starch** and stir until each slice has a thin coat.
6. Drizzle the **peanut oil for the fish** over the top and turn the slices once. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the vegetables.

## Vegetables

Do this while the fish marinates. Use the thick stem of celtuce, and fresh shiitake.

- 6 cups water, for blanching
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the blanch

<!-- cup -->

- 1 cup celtuce stem (莴笋)
- 3 shiitake mushrooms (香菇)
- 1/4 cup onion, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1 handful enoki mushrooms (金针菇)

1. Peel the **celtuce stem** until the pale flesh shows. Slice it 1/4 in thick, in pieces about 2 in long, until you have 1 cup.
2. Slice the **shiitake mushrooms** thin.
3. Slice the **onion** thin, until you have 1/4 cup.
4. Take a handful of the **enoki mushrooms**. Trim the base off. Tear them into small bunches and rinse them.
5. Put the **water for blanching** and the **kosher salt for the blanch** in a heavy saucepan. Leave the saucepan off the coil. Go on to the storm oil.

## Storm oil

Do this while the fish marinates. Use small dried red chilies marked 朝天椒.

- 6 tbsp peanut oil, for the pour

<!-- cup -->

- 12 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1 tsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds (孜然), for the oil
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1/2 in ginger, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

<!-- cup -->

- white of 1 scallion, halved lengthwise and chopped
- green of 1 scallion, chopped
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

1. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds.
2. Slice the **garlic**.
3. Slice the **ginger**.
4. Halve the **white of 1 scallion** lengthwise and chop it. Chop the **green of 1 scallion**. They come from the same scallion.
5. Put the **peanut oil for the pour** in one cup. Put the **dried facing-heaven chilies**, the **Sichuan peppercorns**, the **green Sichuan peppercorns**, the **cumin seeds for the oil**, the **garlic**, and the **ginger** in another. Put the **toasted sesame oil** in another. Put the **white of 1 scallion**, the **green of 1 scallion**, and the **white sesame seeds** in another. When the fish has finished its 15 minutes, go on to the pour. If it has not, wait.

## Pour

These amounts were measured above.

**Vegetables**

- 6 cups water, for blanching
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the blanch

<!-- cup -->

- 1 cup celtuce stem (莴笋)
- 3 shiitake mushrooms (香菇)
- 1/4 cup onion, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1 handful enoki mushrooms (金针菇)

**Fish**

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/8 in thick, marinated

**Storm oil**

- 6 tbsp peanut oil, for the pour

<!-- cup -->

- 12 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 1 tsp green Sichuan peppercorns (青花椒)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds (孜然), for the oil
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1/2 in ginger, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

<!-- cup -->

- white of 1 scallion, halved lengthwise and chopped
- green of 1 scallion, chopped
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

1. If the fish has not finished its 15 minutes, wait. Set the saucepan on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. When the water boils, go on.
2. Add the **celtuce stem**, the **shiitake mushrooms**, and the **onion** together. Cook until the celtuce is crisp-tender and the shiitake have softened, about 2 minutes. Lift them out with a slotted spoon and let them drain.
3. Add the **enoki mushrooms**. Lift them out with a slotted spoon as soon as they wilt, about 15 seconds. Longer than that and they turn to slime.
4. Pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to low. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on. The water should shiver. If the surface is still, turn the coil one setting higher and leave the saucepan fully on. If the center boils hard, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the bubble stops, turn the coil back to the same setting and set the saucepan fully back on. If that setting boils hard again, do the same again.
5. Slide in half of the **marinated snakehead fillet**, a few slices at a time. When the slices are white and firm, about 30 seconds, lift them out with a slotted spoon. If a slice is still translucent, leave it 15 seconds more. Cook the remaining half the same way. Turn the saucepan's coil off.
6. Spread the **celtuce stem**, the **enoki mushrooms**, the **shiitake mushrooms**, and the **onion** together in a wide heatproof bowl.
7. Lay the **marinated snakehead fillet** over them in one overlapping layer.
8. Put the **peanut oil for the pour** in a small skillet. Set it on a coil turned to medium. The oil is ready when a dry cooking chopstick makes small bubbles and the oil has not yet smoked, about 3 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil and turn that coil off. If the oil is smoking, let it cool until the smoke stops.
9. Add the **dried facing-heaven chilies**, the **Sichuan peppercorns**, the **green Sichuan peppercorns**, the **cumin seeds for the oil**, the **garlic**, and the **ginger** together. Stir until the seeds smell toasty and the chilies turn one shade darker, still red, about 15 seconds. If they blacken, keep stirring until they cool.
10. Pour the **peanut oil for the pour**, spices and all, evenly over the slices.
11. Drizzle the **toasted sesame oil** over the top.
12. Scatter the **white of 1 scallion**, the **green of 1 scallion**, and the **white sesame seeds**. Eat it hot.
