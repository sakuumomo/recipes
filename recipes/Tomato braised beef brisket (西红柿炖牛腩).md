# Tomato braised beef brisket (西红柿炖牛腩)

Serves 2. About 120 minutes.

The beef blanches in a heavy saucepan. The same saucepan stews it.

Blanch the beef first. Cut the tomatoes while the water heats. Stew when the beef is rinsed.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Beef**

- 16 oz beef brisket (牛腩)
- 8 cups water, for the blanch

**Tomatoes**

- 16 oz tomatoes

**Stew**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil
- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 2 scallions, cut into 2 in lengths
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 star anise (八角)
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 cups water, for the stew
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

---

## Beef

Use the fatty plate sold as 牛腩, and leave the fat on.

- 16 oz beef brisket (牛腩)
- 8 cups water, for the blanch

1. Cut the **beef brisket** into 1 1/2 in chunks.
2. Put the **beef brisket** and the **water for the blanch** in a heavy saucepan. Set it on high. Go on to the tomatoes. When the water boils, skim the gray foam and cook 2 minutes.
3. Lift the beef out. Rinse it under hot water until the scum is gone, and drain it. Pour off the blanch water and wipe the saucepan dry.

## Tomatoes

Use ripe tomatoes that smell sweet.

- 16 oz tomatoes

1. Core the **tomatoes** and cut them into 1 in wedges.

## Stew

Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil
- 16 oz beef brisket (牛腩), blanched
- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 2 scallions, cut into 2 in lengths
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 star anise (八角)
- 16 oz tomatoes, cut
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 cups water, for the stew
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

1. Set the saucepan on medium. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, about 1 minute, add the **blanched beef brisket**. Stir until the cut edges brown, about 4 minutes. If the oil smokes, pull the saucepan off the coil until the smoke stops, then set it back.
2. Add the **ginger**, **scallions**, **garlic**, and **star anise** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 1 minute.
3. Add the **cut tomatoes**. Stir until they collapse and the oil turns red, about 5 minutes.
4. Add the **Shaoxing wine**, **light soy sauce**, **dark soy sauce**, and **sugar** together. Stir until the beef looks reddish-brown, about 30 seconds.
5. Pour in the **water for the stew**. Press the beef under the surface. Turn the coil to high. When it boils, skim any foam. Pull the saucepan so it sits half on the coil. The surface should bubble gently. Cover it.
6. Simmer until a fork pushed into a chunk meets little resistance, about 75 minutes. If the center boils hard, pull the saucepan farther off the coil. If the beef is still firm, cover and cook 15 minutes more.
7. Add the **kosher salt**. Uncover. Set the saucepan fully on the coil at medium and cook, stirring, until the sauce coats a spoon and the broth is red, about 8 minutes. A few soft pieces of tomato are fine. If it starts to stick, pull the saucepan half off the coil.
8. Pick out the star anise. Eat it hot.
