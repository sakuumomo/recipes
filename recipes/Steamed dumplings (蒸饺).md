# Steamed dumplings (蒸饺)

Serves 2. About 85 minutes.

The filling is mixed in a bowl. The dumplings steam in a bamboo steamer in the wok on the Mosaic at 375°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Salt the cabbage first. Mince the scallions and ginger while it weeps. When the timer rings, squeeze the cabbage, then keep going. Steam 8 dumplings at a time.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Filling

Use ground pork that shows some fat.

- 2 1/4 cups napa cabbage, chopped fine
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the cabbage

<!-- cup -->

- 2 scallions, minced
- 1 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

<!-- cup -->

- 8 oz ground pork
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp cold water, for the pork

1. Cut the core from the **napa cabbage**. Slice the leaves thin, then chop them fine.
2. Toss the **napa cabbage** with the **kosher salt for the cabbage** in a colander. Set the colander in the sink. Set a timer for 10 minutes. Go on to the next step.
3. Mince the **scallions**.
4. Mince the **ginger**.
5. When the timer rings, the cabbage should look wet and slumped. If it has not rung, wait. If it is still dry and crisp, wait until it slumps. Squeeze it by the handful until it stops dripping and a handful feels dry.
6. Put the **ground pork** in a bowl. Add the **ginger**, the **light soy sauce**, and the **ground white pepper** together. Stir in one direction until the meat turns sticky and pale, about 1 minute.
7. Add the **cold water for the pork** a spoon at a time, still stirring in one direction, until the liquid disappears and the paste looks glossy.
8. Add the squeezed **napa cabbage**, the **scallions**, and the **toasted sesame oil** together. Stir until the cabbage is even through the meat.

## Dumplings

Use round white Shanghai-style wheat wrappers from the refrigerator or freezer case. If they are frozen, thaw them covered until a wrapper bends without cracking; a thick square egg roll wrapper will not pleat.

- 16 dumpling wrappers (饺子皮)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp water, for the seal

**Filling**

- 2 1/4 cups napa cabbage, chopped fine, mixed
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the cabbage, mixed

<!-- cup -->

- 2 scallions, minced, mixed
- 1 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油), mixed

<!-- cup -->

- 8 oz ground pork, mixed
- 1 tsp ginger, minced, mixed
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽), mixed
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, mixed

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp cold water, for the pork, mixed

1. Put the **water for the seal** in a cup.
2. Divide the mixed filling into 16 even portions.
3. Keep the **dumpling wrappers** covered with a damp towel.
4. Lay one wrapper flat. Set one portion in the center and leave a bare rim.
5. Wet the rim with the **water for the seal**. Fold the wrapper in half so the edges meet. Press the middle of the seam shut and push the air out.
6. Starting at one end of the seam, make 4 pleats along the near edge, pressing each pleat onto the far edge. The dumpling sits on a flat bottom with the pleats on top.
7. Set it pleat side up and keep it covered. Repeat with the remaining **dumpling wrappers**. If a wrapper tears, press the tear shut and pleat it again.

## Steam

Tool: bamboo steamer.

Cut the parchment before the wok is hot.

- 6 cups water, for the steamer

<!-- cup -->

- 16 dumpling wrappers (饺子皮), wrapped

1. Cut a round of parchment to fit the steamer. Poke about 8 holes in it so the steam can pass.
2. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Set the steamer in the wok. Pour in the **water for the steamer** until it sits about 1 inch below the basket. Lift the steamer out. Leave any unused water in the cup. Stay until the water boils. Turn the left dial to 275°F. The water should keep boiling.
3. Line the steamer with the parchment. Set 8 of the **dumpling wrappers** on it, pleat side up, with space between them.
4. Set the steamer over the wok and cover the steamer with its lid. The water should keep boiling, and the lid should drip. If the boil dies, turn the left dial up 10°F. If the boil reaches the basket, turn the left dial down 10°F. Watch the water so the wok does not boil dry.
5. Steam until the wrappers look glossy and a dumpling feels firm when pressed, about 10 minutes. If a wrapper still looks chalky, cover the steamer and steam 2 minutes more.
6. Lift that batch onto a plate and cover the plate.
7. If the water sits lower than about 1 inch below the basket, set the steamer in the wok and pour in more of the **water for the steamer** until the gap is about 1 inch. Lift the steamer out. If the water is not boiling, turn the left dial to 375°F and stay until it boils. Turn the left dial to 275°F. The water should keep boiling.
8. Set the parchment back in the steamer. Set the remaining **dumpling wrappers** on it, pleat side up, with space between them. Set the steamer over the wok and cover the steamer with its lid. The water should keep boiling, and the lid should drip. If the boil dies, turn the left dial up 10°F. If the boil reaches the basket, turn the left dial down 10°F. Watch the water so the wok does not boil dry.
9. Steam until the wrappers look glossy and a dumpling feels firm when pressed, about 10 minutes. If a wrapper still looks chalky, cover the steamer and steam 2 minutes more.
10. Lift that batch onto the plate and cover the plate.
11. Use the wok ladle to move the **water for the steamer** into a heatproof bowl until the wok is empty. Press on/off.
12. Set the wok on its stand. Eat them hot with Dumpling dipping sauce (饺子蘸料).

---

Use salted Shaoxing. Add 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒) with the light soy sauce and the ground white pepper.
