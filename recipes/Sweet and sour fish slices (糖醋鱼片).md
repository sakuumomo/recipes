# Sweet and sour fish slices (糖醋鱼片)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The fish fries in a deep heavy saucepan on the coil. The glaze is in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Marinate the fish. Mix the sauce while it sits. Coat and fry the fish when the oil is ready, then glaze it.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Fish**

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/4 in thick
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the fish
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 egg white

**Sauce**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the sauce
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp tomato ketchup (番茄酱)
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar (米醋)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the sauce
- 3 tbsp water, for the sauce
- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 1 tbsp cold water, for the slurry
- 1 scallion, chopped

**Fry**

- 3 cups peanut oil, for frying
- 1/3 cup potato starch, for the coating

---

## Fish

Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/4 in thick
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the fish
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 egg white

1. If the **snakehead fillet** still has skin, cut the skin off. Slice the fillet on a bias into pieces about 2 in long and 1/4 in thick.
2. Rinse the slices under cold water until the water runs clear. Pat them dry.
3. Add the **kosher salt for the fish**, the **Shaoxing wine for the fish**, the **ground white pepper**, and the **egg white** together. Stir until the slices feel sticky. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the sauce.

## Sauce

Do this while the fish marinates. Use tomato ketchup. Use plain rice vinegar.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the sauce
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp tomato ketchup (番茄酱)
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar (米醋)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the sauce
- 3 tbsp water, for the sauce
- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 1 tbsp cold water, for the slurry
- 1 scallion, chopped

1. Stir the **tomato ketchup**, the **sugar**, the **light soy sauce**, the **kosher salt for the sauce**, and the **water for the sauce** together until the sugar dissolves. Leave the **rice vinegar** in its cup.
2. Stir the **potato starch for the sauce** and the **cold water for the slurry** together until smooth. Stir it again just before it goes into the wok.
3. Mince the **ginger**. Mince the **garlic**. Chop the **scallion**.

## Fry

- 3 cups peanut oil, for frying
- 1/3 cup potato starch, for the coating
- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/4 in thick, marinated

1. Put the **peanut oil for frying** in a deep heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high. Heat until a thermometer in the oil reads 350°F, about 8 minutes. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, pull the saucepan off the coil until it returns to 350°F, then set it back.
2. When the oil is ready and the fish has finished its 15 minutes, toss the **marinated snakehead fillet** with the **potato starch for the coating** until each slice has a thin, even coat. Shake off the extra starch.
3. Slide in half of the **marinated snakehead fillet** in one layer. Leave the slices still until the coating sets, about 30 seconds, then turn any pale side. Fry until the coating is pale gold. Break one slice open. The center is just opaque, about 45 seconds more. If the center is still translucent, fry 20 seconds more. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, pull the saucepan off the coil until it returns to 350°F, then set it back. Lift the slices out with a slotted spoon.
4. Let the oil return to 350°F. Fry the remaining half the same way. Lift them out.
5. Set the coil on high. When a thermometer in the oil reads 375°F, return half of the slices. Fry until the coating is light gold and crisp, about 20 seconds. If the oil climbs past 375°F, pull the saucepan off the coil until it returns to 375°F, then set it back. Lift them out.
6. Let the oil return to 375°F. Fry the remaining half the same way. Lift them out.
7. Turn the coil off.

## Glaze

These amounts were measured above.

**Sauce**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the sauce
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp tomato ketchup (番茄酱)
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar (米醋)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the sauce
- 3 tbsp water, for the sauce
- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 1 tbsp cold water, for the slurry
- 1 scallion, chopped

**Fish**

- 12 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/4 in thick, fried

1. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium. Add the **peanut oil for the sauce**. When it shimmers, about 1 minute, add the **ginger** and the **garlic** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 15 seconds. If they smell sharp, turn the coil off and stir until the smell sweetens, then turn the coil back to medium.
2. Stir the **potato starch for the sauce** and the **cold water for the slurry** again. Pour in the **tomato ketchup**, the **sugar**, the **light soy sauce**, the **kosher salt for the sauce**, the **water for the sauce**, the **potato starch for the sauce**, and the **cold water for the slurry** together. Stir until the sauce is glossy and a spoon dragged through it leaves a clear trail, about 1 minute. If the center boils hard, turn the coil off until it calms, then turn the coil back to medium.
3. Turn the coil off. Add the **rice vinegar**. Stir until the sauce smells sharp, about 5 seconds.
4. Add the **fried snakehead fillet** and the **scallion**. Fold until every slice is coated and the sauce clings, about 20 seconds.
5. Eat it hot, while the coating is still crisp.
