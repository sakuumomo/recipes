# Sweet and sour fish slices (糖醋鱼片)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

Fry on the Mosaic at 350°F, then glaze in that wok. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Marinate the fish. Mix the sauce while it sits, then start the fry oil. When the fish timer rings and the oil reads 350°F, coat and fry the slices. Ladle the spare oil out and glaze them while the coating is still crisp.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Fish

Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/4 in thick
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the fish
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the fish
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 egg white

1. If the **snakehead fillet** still has skin, cut the skin off. Slice it with the boning knife on a bias into pieces about 2 in long and 1/4 in thick.
2. Rinse the slices under cold water until the water runs clear. Pat them dry.
3. Add the **kosher salt for the fish**, the **Shaoxing wine for the fish**, the **ground white pepper**, and the **egg white** together. Stir until the slices feel sticky. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the sauce.

## Sauce

Do this while the fish marinates. Use tomato ketchup, and plain rice vinegar.

- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 1 clove garlic, minced

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp tomato ketchup (番茄酱)
- 4 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the sauce
- 2 tbsp water, for the sauce

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 1 tbsp cold water, for the slurry

<!-- cup -->

- 1 scallion, chopped

<!-- cup -->

- 4 tsp rice vinegar (米醋)

1. Mince the **ginger**. Mince the **garlic**. Put them in one cup.
2. Stir the **tomato ketchup**, the **sugar**, the **light soy sauce**, the **kosher salt for the sauce**, and the **water for the sauce** together in one cup until the sugar dissolves.
3. Stir the **potato starch for the sauce** and the **cold water for the slurry** together in a small bowl until smooth. Stir it again just before it goes into the wok.
4. Chop the **scallion** and put it in a cup. Leave the **rice vinegar** in its cup.

## Fry

- 4 cups peanut oil, for frying

<!-- cup -->

- 1/4 cup potato starch, for the coating

<!-- cup -->

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/4 in thick, marinated

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 350°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Pour in the **peanut oil for frying**. Stay with the thermometer. Keep the probe in the oil and off the steel. Heat until the oil reads 350°F and the fish has finished its 15 minutes. If the oil stalls short of 350°F, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, turn the left dial up another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When the oil returns to 350°F, press start.
2. Toss the **marinated snakehead fillet** with the **potato starch for the coating** until each slice has a thin, even coat. Shake off the extra starch.
3. Slide in half of the **marinated snakehead fillet** in one layer so the oil covers them. Leave the slices still until the coating sets, about 30 seconds, then turn any pale side with cooking chopsticks. Fry until the coating is pale gold, about 45 seconds more. Break one slice open. The center is just opaque. If the center is still translucent, fry 20 seconds more. Lift the slices out with a slotted spoon onto a plate.
4. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Fry the remaining half of the **marinated snakehead fillet** the same way. Lift them out onto the plate.
5. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Slide half of the **marinated snakehead fillet** back in one layer. Fry until the coating is light gold and crisp, about 20 seconds. Lift them out onto the plate.
6. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Fry the remaining half of the **marinated snakehead fillet** the same way. Lift them out onto the plate.

## Glaze

Do this as soon as the slices come out.

These amounts were measured above.

**Fry**

- 4 cups peanut oil, for frying

**Sauce**

- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 1 clove garlic, minced

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp tomato ketchup (番茄酱)
- 4 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the sauce
- 2 tbsp water, for the sauce

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 1 tbsp cold water, for the slurry

<!-- cup -->

- 1 scallion, chopped

<!-- cup -->

- 4 tsp rice vinegar (米醋)

**Fish**

- 8.8 oz snakehead fillet (黑鱼), skinless and boneless, sliced 1/4 in thick, fried

1. With the wok ladle, take the spare **peanut oil for frying** out into a heatproof bowl until about 1 tbsp remains. Leave the wok seated. If the wok is dry, pour back about 1 tbsp of that oil.
2. Turn the left dial to 300°F. When the film shimmers and has stopped smoking, about 30 seconds, add the **ginger** and the **garlic** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 15 seconds. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start.
3. Add the **tomato ketchup**, the **sugar**, the **light soy sauce**, the **kosher salt for the sauce**, and the **water for the sauce** together. Stir until the sauce bubbles and smells sweet, about 30 seconds. If it smells sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start.
4. Stir the **potato starch for the sauce** and the **cold water for the slurry** again. Pour them in. Stir until the sauce is glossy and a spoon dragged through it leaves a clear trail, about 30 seconds. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles. Press start.
5. Add the **fried snakehead fillet** and the **scallion** together. Fold until every slice is coated and the sauce clings, about 20 seconds.
6. Press on/off. Add the **rice vinegar**. Stir until the sauce smells sharp, about 5 seconds. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt.
7. Scrape the fish onto a plate. Set the wok on its stand. Eat it hot, while the coating is still crisp.

---

Add 1/4 cup pineapple canned in juice, drained and cut into 1/2 in pieces, with the rice vinegar.
