# Enoki with fatty beef (金针肥牛)

Serves 2. About 20 minutes.

Stir-fry on the Mosaic at 375°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Separate the beef while it is still cold. The mushrooms wait in a bowl while the beef cooks.

2. Trim the base off the **enoki mushrooms**. Tear that bunch into small bunches. Rinse them and shake them dry.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Prep**

- 1 bunch enoki mushrooms (金针菇)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 in ginger, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 8 oz hot-pot beef slices (肥牛)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

<!-- cup -->

- 1 scallion, minced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Add the **enoki mushrooms**. Stir until they wilt, about 2 minutes, then push them up the sides until the liquid in the bottom cooks away, about 2 minutes more. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start. Scoop them into a bowl.
2. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 30 seconds. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start.
3. Add half of the **hot-pot beef slices** in one layer in the bottom. Stir until the pink is gone and the slices are still tender, about 45 seconds. If they brown hard, press pause/clear once and stir until they stop browning. Press start. Push that half up the sides.
4. Add the remaining **hot-pot beef slices** in one layer in the bottom. Stir until the pink is gone and the slices are still tender, about 45 seconds. Fold the first half down.
5. Add the **enoki mushrooms** from the bowl, the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce**, the **sugar**, the **ground white pepper**, and the **kosher salt** together. Stir until the sauce clings and the mushrooms look glossy, about 45 seconds. If liquid is still pooling, push the beef up the sides until the bottom reduces, then fold it back down. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
6. Add the **scallion** and the **toasted sesame oil** together. Stir until the scallion smells sweet, about 15 seconds. Press on/off. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精).
7. Spoon it onto a plate.
