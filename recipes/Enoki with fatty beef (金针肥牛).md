# Enoki with fatty beef (金针肥牛)

Serves 2. About 20 minutes.

The mushrooms and the beef cook in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Dry the mushrooms first. Separate the beef when the mushrooms are out of the wok.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Mushrooms**

- 6 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇)

**Stir-fry**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp ginger, sliced
- 8 oz hot-pot beef slices (肥牛)
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 scallion, minced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

---

## Mushrooms

- 6 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇)

1. Trim the base off the **enoki mushrooms**. Tear them into small bunches. Rinse them and shake them dry.
2. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium-high. Add the **enoki mushrooms**. Stir until they wilt, about 2 minutes, then push them up the sides until the liquid in the bottom cooks away, about 2 minutes more. If they brown, turn the coil off and stir until they cool, then turn the coil back to medium-high. Scoop them into a bowl.

## Stir-fry

Use the thin marbled slices sold for hot pot.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp ginger, sliced
- 8 oz hot-pot beef slices (肥牛)
- 6 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇), wilted
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 scallion, minced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

1. If the **hot-pot beef slices** are frozen together, separate them while they are still cold. Pat them dry. Mince the **scallion**. Slice the **garlic** and the **ginger**.
2. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium-high. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, about 1 minute, add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 30 seconds. If they color, turn the coil off and stir until they cool, then turn the coil back to medium-high.
3. Add half of the **hot-pot beef slices** in one layer in the bottom. Stir until the pink is gone and the slices are still tender, about 45 seconds. If they brown hard, turn the coil off and stir until they cool, then turn the coil back to medium-high. Push that half up the sides.
4. Add the remaining **hot-pot beef slices** in one layer in the bottom. Stir until the pink is gone and the slices are still tender, about 45 seconds. If they brown hard, turn the coil off and stir until they cool, then turn the coil back to medium-high. Fold the first half down.
5. Add the **wilted enoki mushrooms**, the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce**, the **sugar**, and the **ground white pepper** together. Stir until the sauce clings and the mushrooms look glossy, about 45 seconds. If liquid is still pooling, push the beef up the sides until the bottom reduces, then fold it back down.
6. Turn the coil off. Add the **scallion** and the **toasted sesame oil** together. Stir until the scallion smells sweet, about 15 seconds.
7. Eat it hot.
