# Braised pork ribs with green beans (排骨炖豆角)

Serves 2. About 90 minutes.

The ribs blanch in a heavy saucepan. The same saucepan fries the beans, then stews them.

Blanch the ribs first. Snap the beans while the water heats. When the ribs are rinsed, fry the beans, then stew.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Ribs**

- 16 oz pork spare ribs
- 8 cups water, for the blanch

**Beans**

- 8 oz green beans (豆角)
- 1 tbsp peanut oil

**Stew**

- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 2 scallions, cut into 2 in lengths
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 star anise (八角)
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 4 cups water, for the stew
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

---

## Ribs

- 16 oz pork spare ribs
- 8 cups water, for the blanch

1. Cut the **pork spare ribs** between the bones into pieces about 1 1/2 in long.
2. Put the **pork spare ribs** and the **water for the blanch** in a heavy saucepan. Set it on high. Skim the gray foam as it rises. When the water boils, cook 2 minutes. Turn the coil off.
3. Lift the ribs out. Rinse them under hot water until the scum is gone, and drain them. Pour off the blanch water and wipe the saucepan dry.

## Beans

Flat green beans and common green beans both turn silky in this stew.

- 8 oz green beans (豆角)
- 1 tbsp peanut oil

1. Snap the ends off the **green beans** and break them into 2 in lengths. Pat them dry.
2. Put the saucepan on medium. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, about 2 minutes, add the **green beans**. If the oil smokes, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the smoke stops, turn the coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on.
3. Stir until the beans are wrinkled and olive and smell sweet, about 5 minutes. If they start to scorch, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When they stop scorching, turn the coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on. Scoop them into a bowl.

## Stew

Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 16 oz pork spare ribs, blanched
- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 2 scallions, cut into 2 in lengths
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 star anise (八角)
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 4 cups water, for the stew
- 8 oz green beans (豆角), fried
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

1. Set the saucepan fully on the coil at medium. Add the **blanched pork spare ribs**. Stir until the meat turns pale and the cut edges brown, about 4 minutes.
2. Add the **ginger**, **scallions**, **garlic**, and **star anise** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 1 minute.
3. Add the **Shaoxing wine**, **light soy sauce**, **dark soy sauce**, and **sugar** together. Stir until the ribs look reddish-brown, about 30 seconds.
4. Pour in the **water for the stew**. Press the ribs under the surface. Turn the coil to high. When it boils, skim any foam. Pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to low. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on. The surface should bubble gently. Cover it.
5. Simmer until a fork pushed into a rib meets little resistance, about 40 minutes. If the center boils hard, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the bubble stops, turn the coil back to low and set the saucepan fully back on. If it boils hard again, do the same again. If the surface goes still, turn the coil one setting higher and leave the saucepan fully on.
6. Add the **fried green beans** and the **kosher salt** together. Stir. Cover and simmer until the beans are silky all the way through and a rib twists at the bone, about 15 minutes. If a bean is still bright and firm, cover and cook 5 minutes more. If the center boils hard, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the bubble stops, turn the coil back to low and set the saucepan fully back on.
7. Uncover. If the sauce is still thin, turn the coil to medium and leave the saucepan fully on. Cook, stirring, until the sauce coats a spoon, about 5 minutes. If it starts to stick, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. Stir until it releases. Turn the coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on.
8. Pick out the star anise. Eat it hot.
