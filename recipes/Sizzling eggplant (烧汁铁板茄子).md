# Sizzling eggplant (烧汁铁板茄子)

Serves 2. About 35 minutes.

The eggplant fries in the wok at 375°F. The sauce cooks in a small heavy saucepan. The sizzle is a 10 in heavy skillet. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Salt the eggplant. Make the sauce while it sits. When the 15 minutes are up, fry the eggplant, then heat the empty skillet and pour the hot sauce over.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Eggplant

Use a long Chinese eggplant. Salt and starch the sticks before the wok is hot.

- 2 long Chinese eggplants
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 1/4 cup peanut oil, for the eggplant

1. With the 8 in cleaver, trim the **long Chinese eggplants** and cut them into sticks about 1/2 in thick and 2 in long. Toss the sticks with the **kosher salt**. Put the **potato starch** in one cup and the **peanut oil for the eggplant** in another. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the sauce.
2. When the timer rings, squeeze the sticks and pat them dry. Toss them with the **potato starch** until each stick has a thin coat. Shake off the extra starch.
3. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Add the **peanut oil for the eggplant**. When it shimmers, add half of the **long Chinese eggplants** in one layer in the bottom. Cook until the underside is golden, about 2 minutes. Turn the sticks. Cook until both sides are golden and the center of a stick looks creamy, about 2 minutes more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. If the center is still white and spongy, cook 1 minute more. If the food stays pale after another minute at this temperature, turn the left dial to 425°F and keep going. Lift the sticks out onto a plate.
4. If the dial is not at 375°F, turn the left dial back to 375°F. Add the remaining **long Chinese eggplants**. Cook them the same way, until both sides are golden and the center of a stick looks creamy. Lift the sticks out onto the plate.
5. With the wok ladle, move the spare oil into a heatproof bowl. Press on/off.

## Sauce

Do this while the eggplant sits.

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the sauce

<!-- cup -->

- 1/4 cup onion, diced
- 1/4 cup red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

<!-- cup -->

- 3 tbsp water
- 1 tsp potato starch
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 2 tsp sugar

1. With the 8 in cleaver, dice the **onion** and the **red bell pepper**. Mince the **ginger** and the **garlic**. Put the **onion**, the **red bell pepper**, the **ginger**, and the **garlic** in one cup. In another cup, stir the **potato starch** into the **water** until it is smooth, then stir in the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce**, the **dark soy sauce**, and the **sugar**.
2. Put the **peanut oil for the sauce** in a small heavy saucepan. Set the saucepan fully on a coil turned to medium. When the oil shimmers, about 1 minute, add the **onion**, the **red bell pepper**, the **ginger**, and the **garlic** together. Stir until the onion looks clear and smells sweet, about 1 minute. If the onion starts to brown, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. Stir until the color stops deepening. Turn the coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on. If it starts to brown again, do the same again.
3. Stir the **water**, the **potato starch**, the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce**, the **dark soy sauce**, and the **sugar** again. Pour them in. Stir until the sauce bubbles and looks glossy, about 30 seconds. If the center boils hard, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the bubble stops, turn the coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on. If that setting boils hard again, do the same again. If the bottom starts to catch, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. Stir until it releases. Turn the coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on. When the sauce looks glossy, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. If the timer has not rung, wait until it does. Go back and finish the eggplant, then go on to the sizzle.

## Sizzle

Chop the scallion before the skillet is hot.

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the skillet

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp Zhenjiang vinegar (镇江香醋)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 scallion, chopped
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

**Eggplant**

- 2 long Chinese eggplants, fried

**Sauce**

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the sauce

<!-- cup -->

- 1/4 cup onion, diced
- 1/4 cup red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

<!-- cup -->

- 3 tbsp water
- 1 tsp potato starch
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 2 tsp sugar

1. With the 8 in cleaver, chop the **scallion**. Put the **peanut oil for the skillet** in one cup, the **Zhenjiang vinegar** in another, and the **scallion** with the **white sesame seeds** in another.
2. Set the empty 10 in skillet fully on a coil turned to high. Set the saucepan fully on another coil turned to medium and stir the sauce. If the bottom starts to catch, pull the saucepan fully off and turn that coil off. Stir until it releases. Turn that coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on. When the sauce bubbles, pull the saucepan fully off and turn that coil off. Stay with the skillet until a drop of water skitters and vanishes, about 3 minutes. The oil goes in only when the eggplant and the sauce are ready to follow it.
3. Add the **peanut oil for the skillet** and swirl it over the bottom. Add the **long Chinese eggplants**. Pour the saucepan over them at once: the **peanut oil for the sauce**, the **onion**, the **red bell pepper**, the **ginger**, the **garlic**, the **water**, the **potato starch**, the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce**, the **dark soy sauce**, and the **sugar** together. It should sizzle. Stir until the glaze clings, about 15 seconds. If the glaze smells sharp or turns dull, pull the skillet fully off and turn that coil off.
4. If the skillet is still on the coil, pull it fully off and turn that coil off. Add the **Zhenjiang vinegar**. Stir until it smells sharp, about 10 seconds. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精).
5. Scatter the **scallion** and the **white sesame seeds**. Eat it hot.
