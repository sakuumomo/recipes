# Sizzling eggplant (烧汁铁板茄子)

Serves 2. About 35 minutes.

The eggplant fries in a 10 in heavy skillet on the coil. The sauce cooks in a small heavy saucepan. The same skillet takes the sizzle.

Salt the eggplant. Make the sauce while it sits. Fry the eggplant, then heat the empty skillet and pour the sauce over.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Eggplant**

- 12 oz eggplant
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 2 tbsp potato starch
- 1/4 cup peanut oil, for the eggplant

**Sauce**

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the sauce
- 2 oz onion, diced
- 2 oz red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 2 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp water
- 1 tsp potato starch

**Sizzle**

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the skillet
- 1 tsp Zhenjiang vinegar (镇江香醋)
- 1 scallion, chopped
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

---

## Eggplant

Use a long Chinese eggplant.

- 12 oz eggplant
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 2 tbsp potato starch
- 1/4 cup peanut oil, for the eggplant

1. Trim the **eggplant** and cut it into sticks about 1/2 in thick and 2 in long. Toss the sticks with the **kosher salt**. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the sauce.
2. When the timer rings, squeeze the sticks and pat them dry. Toss them with the **potato starch** until each stick has a thin coat. Shake off the extra starch.
3. Set a 10 in heavy skillet on a coil turned to medium. Add the **peanut oil for the eggplant**. When it shimmers, about 2 minutes, add half of the **eggplant** in one layer. Cook until the underside is golden, about 2 minutes. Turn the sticks. Cook until both sides are golden and the center of a stick looks creamy, about 2 minutes more. If the oil smokes, pull the skillet fully off and turn the coil off. When the smoke stops, turn the coil back to medium and set the skillet fully back on. If the center is still white and spongy, cook 1 minute more. Lift the sticks out.
4. Add the remaining half of the **eggplant**. Cook it the same way. Lift the sticks out.
5. Pour off the oil. Wipe the skillet until a dry towel comes away dry. Go on to the sizzle.

## Sauce

Do this while the eggplant sits.

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the sauce
- 2 oz onion, diced
- 2 oz red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 2 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp water
- 1 tsp potato starch

1. Dice the **onion** and the **red bell pepper**. Mince the **ginger** and the **garlic**. Stir the **potato starch** into the **water** until it is smooth, then stir in the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce**, the **dark soy sauce**, and the **sugar**.
2. Put the **peanut oil for the sauce** in a small heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to medium. When the oil shimmers, about 1 minute, add the **onion**, the **red bell pepper**, the **ginger**, and the **garlic** together. Stir until the onion looks clear and smells sweet, about 1 minute. If the onion browns, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. Stir until it looks clear. Turn the coil back to medium and set the saucepan fully back on.
3. Stir the **sauce mixture** again. Pour it in. Stir until it bubbles and looks glossy, about 30 seconds. If it boils hard, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off, and keep stirring until it looks glossy. If the saucepan is still on the coil, pull it fully off and turn the coil off. Go back and finish the eggplant, then go on to the sizzle.

## Sizzle

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the skillet
- 12 oz eggplant, fried
- 1 tsp Zhenjiang vinegar (镇江香醋)
- 1 scallion, chopped
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

**Sauce**

- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the sauce
- 2 oz onion, diced
- 2 oz red bell pepper, diced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 2 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp water
- 1 tsp potato starch

1. Chop the **scallion**.
2. Set the empty skillet on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. Set the saucepan on another coil turned to medium. Stir the **sauce mixture**. When it bubbles, about 1 minute, pull the saucepan fully off and turn that coil off. If it sticks before then, pull the saucepan fully off and turn that coil off. When a drop of water flicked into the skillet skitters and vanishes, about 3 minutes, go on. The oil goes in only when the eggplant and the sauce are ready to follow it.
3. Add the **peanut oil for the skillet** and swirl it over the bottom. Add the **fried eggplant**. Pour the **sauce mixture** over it at once. It should sizzle. Stir until the glaze clings, about 15 seconds. If the glaze smells sharp or turns dull, pull the skillet fully off and turn that coil off.
4. If the skillet is still on the coil, pull it fully off and turn that coil off. Add the **Zhenjiang vinegar**. Stir until it smells sharp, about 10 seconds.
5. Scatter the **scallion** and the **white sesame seeds**. Eat it hot.
