# Black pepper beef (黑胡椒牛肉)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The beef marinates in a bowl. The sauce is mixed in a bowl. The stir-fry is in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Marinate the beef first. Mix the sauce and cut the vegetables while it rests. Stir-fry when the beef looks tacky.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Beef

Use flank steak so the slices soften in this time. Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz flank steak
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the beef
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the beef
- 1 tsp potato starch, for the beef
- 1 tsp water, for the beef
- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the beef

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry

1. Slice the **flank steak** against the grain, 1/8 in thick and about 2 in long. If the steak bends under the knife, freeze it 15 minutes, then slice.
2. Add the **Shaoxing wine for the beef**, the **light soy sauce for the beef**, the **potato starch for the beef**, the **water for the beef**, and the **peanut oil for the beef**. Stir in one direction until the beef is tacky and no liquid pools in the bowl, about 1 minute.
3. Rest 15 minutes. Go on to the sauce. Put the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** in a cup.

## Sauce

- 2 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the sauce
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 3 tbsp water, for the sauce
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

1. Stir the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce for the sauce**, the **sugar**, the **potato starch for the sauce**, the **water for the sauce**, and the **toasted sesame oil** together until the liquid is smooth. Stir it again just before it goes into the wok.

## Vegetables

Grind the pepper coarsely so it stays fragrant.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the vegetables

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 1/2 tsp black pepper, coarsely ground

<!-- cup -->

- 1 small onion, sliced
- 4 oz red bell pepper, cut into 1/2 in strips

1. Slice the **garlic**. Slice the **onion**. Cut the **red bell pepper** into 1/2 in strips. Grind the **black pepper** coarsely if it is still whole.
2. Put the **peanut oil for the vegetables** in a cup.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Beef**

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry

<!-- cup -->

- 10 oz flank steak, marinated

**Vegetables**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the vegetables

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 1/2 tsp black pepper, coarsely ground

<!-- cup -->

- 1 small onion, sliced
- 4 oz red bell pepper, cut into 1/2 in strips

**Sauce**

- 2 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the sauce
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 3 tbsp water, for the sauce
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

1. If the beef is not tacky yet, wait. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to high. Heat it until a drop of water flicked into the center vanishes, about 2 minutes. The coil is glowing red by then. Stop there.
2. Add 1 tbsp of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry**. Swirl it over the bottom. Add half of the **marinated flank steak** in one layer. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 30 seconds. Toss until the red is gone, about 30 seconds more. If the oil smokes, turn the coil off and stir until the smoke stops. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to high. When the oil shimmers, keep going. Scoop it onto a plate.
3. Add the rest of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** and the rest of the **marinated flank steak**. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 30 seconds. Toss until the red is gone, about 30 seconds more. If the oil smokes, turn the coil off and stir until the smoke stops. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to high. When the oil shimmers, keep going. Scoop it onto the plate.
4. Turn the coil off. Add the **peanut oil for the vegetables**. Wait until the oil stops smoking. When the coil stops glowing, turn it to medium. Add the **garlic** and the **black pepper** together. Stir until the pepper smells fragrant, about 20 seconds. If it smells sharp, turn the coil off and stir until the smell sweetens. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium.
5. Add the **onion** and the **red bell pepper** together. Stir until the onion is translucent and the pepper is still crisp, about 1 minute.
6. Add the **marinated flank steak**. Stir the sauce again and pour in the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce for the sauce**, the **sugar**, the **potato starch for the sauce**, the **water for the sauce**, and the **toasted sesame oil** together. Toss until the sauce clings and the beef is glossy, about 30 seconds. If the center boils hard, turn the coil off and keep tossing until the sauce clings.
7. Eat it hot.
