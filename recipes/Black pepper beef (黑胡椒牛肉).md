# Black pepper beef (黑胡椒牛肉)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

Beef in a bowl. Sauce in a bowl. Stir-fry on the Mosaic at 500°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Marinate the beef first. Mix the sauce and cut the vegetables while it rests. Stir-fry when the beef looks tacky.

## Beef

Use flank steak so the slices soften in this time, and unsalted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz flank steak
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the beef
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the beef
- 1 tsp potato starch, for the beef
- 1 tsp water, for the beef
- 1 tsp peanut oil, for the beef

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry

1. Slice the **flank steak** against the grain with the 8 in cleaver, 1/8 in thick and about 2 in long. If the steak bends, freeze it 15 minutes, then slice.
2. Add the **Shaoxing wine for the beef**, the **light soy sauce for the beef**, the **potato starch for the beef**, the **water for the beef**, and the **peanut oil for the beef**. Stir in one direction until the beef is tacky and no liquid pools in the bowl, about 1 minute.
3. Put the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** in a cup.
4. Rest 15 minutes. Go on to the sauce.

## Sauce

- 2 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the sauce
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 3 tbsp water, for the sauce
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

1. Stir the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce for the sauce**, the **kosher salt**, the **sugar**, the **potato starch for the sauce**, the **water for the sauce**, and the **toasted sesame oil** together until the liquid is smooth. Stir it again just before it goes into the wok.

## Vegetables

Do this before the wok is hot. Grind the pepper coarsely so it stays fragrant.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the vegetables

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 1/2 tsp black pepper, coarsely ground

<!-- cup -->

- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1/2 red bell pepper, cut into 1/2 in strips

1. Slice the **garlic** and the **onion** with the 8 in cleaver. Cut the **red bell pepper** into 1/2 in strips. Grind the **black pepper** coarsely if it is still whole.
2. Put the **peanut oil for the vegetables** in a cup, the **garlic** and the **black pepper** in a cup, and the **onion** and the **red bell pepper** in a cup.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Beef**

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the stir-fry

<!-- cup -->

- 10 oz flank steak, marinated

**Vegetables**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the vegetables

<!-- cup -->

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 1/2 tsp black pepper, coarsely ground

<!-- cup -->

- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1/2 red bell pepper, cut into 1/2 in strips

**Sauce**

- 2 tbsp oyster sauce (蚝油)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), for the sauce
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp potato starch, for the sauce
- 3 tbsp water, for the sauce
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

1. If the beef is not tacky yet, wait. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 500°F. Heat the empty wok until a drop of water flicked into the center vanishes.
2. Add 1 tbsp of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** and swirl it over the bottom. Add half of the **marinated flank steak** in one layer. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 30 seconds. Toss until the red is gone, about 30 seconds more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. Scoop it onto a plate.
3. Add the rest of the **peanut oil for the stir-fry** and swirl it over the bottom. Add the rest of the **marinated flank steak** in one layer. Leave it still until the underside browns, about 30 seconds. Toss until the red is gone, about 30 seconds more. Scoop it onto the plate.
4. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **peanut oil for the vegetables**. When it shimmers and has stopped smoking, about 30 seconds, go on.
5. Add the **garlic** and the **black pepper** together. Stir until the pepper smells fragrant, about 20 seconds. If it smells sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start.
6. Add the **onion** and the **red bell pepper** together. Stir until the onion is translucent and the pepper is still crisp, about 1 minute.
7. Add the **marinated flank steak**. Stir the sauce again and pour in the **oyster sauce**, the **light soy sauce for the sauce**, the **kosher salt**, the **sugar**, the **potato starch for the sauce**, the **water for the sauce**, and the **toasted sesame oil** together. Toss until the sauce clings and the beef is glossy, about 30 seconds. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles. Press start.
8. Press on/off. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精). Eat it hot.
