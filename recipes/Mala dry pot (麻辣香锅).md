# Mala dry pot (麻辣香锅)

Serves 2. About 40 minutes.

The wood ear soaks in a bowl. The vegetables blanch in a heavy saucepan. The sear starts in the wok at 375°F on the Mosaic at 1500 watts, and the toss stays in that wok at 300°F.

Soak the wood ear. Slice the potato and cut the seasoning while it soaks, then blanch. When the wood ear timer rings, finish the lift or the batch you are on, then drain the wood ear. Sear, then toss.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Wood ear

- 1 handful dried wood ear (干黑木耳)
- 2 cups hot water

1. Put the **dried wood ear** in a bowl and cover it with the **hot water**. Set a timer for 20 minutes. Go on to the potato.
2. When the timer rings, if you are mid-blanch or mid-sear, finish that lift or that batch first. Drain the **dried wood ear** and rinse it. Pinch off any hard knobs and tear the pieces into bites. Shake the pieces until they stop dripping.

## Potato

Use a waxy potato so the pieces stay whole.

- 1 small potato, sliced 1/4 in thick
- 2 cups cold water, for the potato

1. Peel the **potato** and slice it 1/4 in thick.
2. Put the slices in a bowl and cover them with the **cold water for the potato**. Leave them 10 minutes. Go on to the seasoning. Drain the potato when the 10 minutes are up and the blanch is done. Pat it very dry.

## Seasoning

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the toss

1. Snip the **dried erjingtiao** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Slice the **ginger** and the **garlic**. Chop the **Pixian doubanjiang**. Halve the **white of 1 scallion** lengthwise and cut it into 1/4 in pieces. Cut the **green of 1 scallion** into 1/2 in lengths. They come from the same scallion. Chop the **cilantro**. If the **hot-pot beef slices** are frozen together, separate them while they are still cold. Pat them dry.
2. Put each group into one cup or pile. Go on to the blanch.

## Blanch

- 6 cups water, for the blanch
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

<!-- cup -->

- 1 handful cauliflower, cut into 1 in florets

<!-- cup -->

- 2 handfuls napa cabbage, cut into 2 in pieces

<!-- cup -->

- 1 handful enoki mushrooms (金针菇)

1. Cut the **cauliflower** into 1 in florets. Cut the **napa cabbage** into 2 in pieces. Trim the base off the **enoki mushrooms**, tear them into small bunches, and rinse them.
2. Put the **water for the blanch** and the **kosher salt** in a heavy saucepan. Set the saucepan fully on a coil turned to high. Stay with it until the water boils.
3. Add the **cauliflower**. Cook until a knife meets a little resistance in a stem, about 2 minutes. If the stem is still hard, cook 1 minute more. Lift it out with a slotted spoon. Shake it until it stops dripping.
4. Add the **napa cabbage**. Cook until the leaves wilt and the stems are still crisp, about 20 seconds. Lift it out with a slotted spoon. Press it gently until it stops dripping.
5. Add the **enoki mushrooms**. Cook until they wilt, about 15 seconds. Lift them out with a slotted spoon. Press them gently until they stop dripping. Pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. If the potato has soaked 10 minutes, drain it and pat it dry. If the wood ear timer has rung, drain the wood ear, rinse it, pinch off any hard knobs, and tear it into bites.

## Sear

Use canned pork luncheon meat.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the sear

<!-- cup -->

- 1 small potato, sliced 1/4 in thick, drained

<!-- cup -->

- 3 oz luncheon meat (午餐肉), sliced 1/4 in thick

1. Slice the **luncheon meat** 1/4 in thick. If the potato is still in the water, drain it. Pat the **drained potato** very dry. If the wood ear is still soaking and the timer has rung, drain it, rinse it, pinch off any hard knobs, and tear it into bites.
2. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Add the **peanut oil for the sear**. When it shimmers, add half of the **drained potato** in one layer in the bottom. Cook until the underside is gold, about 2 minutes. Turn the slices. Cook until both sides are gold and the center is tender, about 2 minutes more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start. If the food stays pale after another minute at this temperature, turn the left dial to 425°F and keep going. Scoop them onto a plate.
3. If the dial is not at 375°F, turn the left dial back to 375°F. Add the remaining half of the **drained potato** in one layer in the bottom. Cook until the underside is gold, about 2 minutes. Turn the slices. Cook until both sides are gold and the center is tender, about 2 minutes more. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start. If the food stays pale after another minute at this temperature, turn the left dial to 425°F and keep going. Scoop them onto the plate.
4. If the dial is not at 375°F, turn the left dial back to 375°F. Add the **luncheon meat** in one layer in the bottom. Cook until the edges brown, about 1 minute. Turn the slices and brown the other side, about 1 minute. If the edges blacken, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start. Scoop them onto the plate.

**Blanch**

- 1 handful cauliflower, cut into 1 in florets, blanched

<!-- cup -->

- 2 handfuls napa cabbage, cut into 2 in pieces, blanched

<!-- cup -->

- 1 handful enoki mushrooms (金针菇), blanched

**Wood ear**

- 1 handful dried wood ear (干黑木耳), soaked

**Potato**

- 1 small potato, sliced 1/4 in thick, seared

**Sear**

- 3 oz luncheon meat (午餐肉), sliced 1/4 in thick, seared

1. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **peanut oil for the toss**. When it shimmers and has stopped smoking, about 30 seconds, go on.
2. Add the **dried erjingtiao** and the **Sichuan peppercorns** together. Stir until the peppercorns smell citrusy and the chilies turn one shade darker, still red, about 20 seconds. If they blacken, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
3. Add the **ginger** and the **garlic** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 20 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
4. Add the **Pixian doubanjiang**. Stir until the oil turns red and the paste smells sweet, about 45 seconds. If it smells sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start. If the oil has not turned red, turn the left dial up 10°F and keep frying until it is red.
5. Add the **Sichuan chili flakes**, the **ground cumin**, the **sugar**, the **light soy sauce**, and the **Shaoxing wine** together. Stir until the wine cooks off, about 15 seconds.
6. Add the **blanched cauliflower**, the **blanched napa cabbage**, the **blanched enoki mushrooms**, the **soaked dried wood ear**, the **seared potato**, and the **seared luncheon meat** together. Stir until every piece is coated and no liquid pools, about 45 seconds. If liquid pools, keep the food up the sides and let the bottom cook until it looks dry. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
7. Push the pot up the sides. Add the **hot-pot beef slices** in the bottom. Stir until the pink is gone and the slices are still tender, about 30 seconds. Fold the pot back down. If they stiffen, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
8. Press on/off. Add the **chili oil**, the **toasted sesame oil**, the **white sesame seeds**, the **white of 1 scallion**, the **green of 1 scallion**, and the **cilantro** together. Stir until the green turns bright, the white smells sweet, and the cilantro wilts, about 15 seconds. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精). Eat the pot hot. The chilies and the peppercorns stay in it for their aroma.
