# Mala dry pot (麻辣香锅)

Serves 2. About 40 minutes.

The wood ear soaks in a bowl. The vegetables blanch in a heavy saucepan. The potatoes, the luncheon meat, and the toss are in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Soak the wood ear. Cut the vegetables and the toss ingredients while it soaks, and blanch them. Sear in the wok, then toss in the same wok.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines with no blank line between them share a cup.

## Ingredients

**Wood ear**

- 1/2 oz dried wood ear (干黑木耳)
- 2 cups hot water

**Potatoes**

- 4 oz potatoes
- 2 cups cold water, for the potatoes

**Blanch**

- 6 cups water, for the blanch
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

- 4 oz cauliflower, cut into 1 in florets

- 4 oz napa cabbage, cut into 2 in pieces

- 3 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇)

**Sear**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the sear

- 3 oz luncheon meat (午餐肉), sliced 1/4 in thick

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the toss

**Toss**

- 6 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced

- 1 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped

- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1 tsp ground cumin (孜然)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)

- 4 oz hot-pot beef slices (肥牛)

- 1 tbsp chili oil (红油)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)
- 1 scallion, cut into 1/2 in lengths
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

---

## Wood ear

- 1/2 oz dried wood ear (干黑木耳)
- 2 cups hot water

1. Put the **dried wood ear** in a bowl and cover it with the **hot water**. Set a timer for 20 minutes. Go on to the potatoes.
2. When the timer rings, drain the **dried wood ear** and rinse it. Pinch off any hard knobs and tear the pieces into bites. If you are mid-blanch, finish that lift first. Shake the pieces until they stop dripping.

## Potatoes

Use a waxy potato so the pieces stay whole.

- 4 oz potatoes
- 2 cups cold water, for the potatoes

1. Peel the **potatoes** and slice them 1/4 in thick.
2. Put the slices in the **cold water for the potatoes**. Leave them 10 minutes. Go on to the blanch. Drain them when the 10 minutes are up and the blanch is done. Pat them very dry.

## Blanch

- 6 cups water, for the blanch
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

- 4 oz cauliflower, cut into 1 in florets

- 4 oz napa cabbage, cut into 2 in pieces

- 3 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇)

1. Cut the **cauliflower** into 1 in florets. Cut the **napa cabbage** into 2 in pieces. Trim the base off the **enoki mushrooms**, tear them into small bunches, and rinse them.
2. Put the **water for the blanch** and the **kosher salt** in a heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high.
3. When the water boils, add the **cauliflower**. Cook until a knife meets a little resistance in a stem, about 2 minutes. If the stem is still hard, cook 1 minute more. Lift it out. Shake it until it stops dripping.
4. Add the **napa cabbage**. Cook until the leaves wilt and the stems are still crisp, about 20 seconds. Lift it out. Press it gently until it stops dripping.
5. Add the **enoki mushrooms**. Cook until they wilt, about 15 seconds. Lift them out. Press them gently until they stop dripping. Turn the coil off. If the potatoes have soaked 10 minutes, drain them and pat them dry.

## Sear

Use canned pork luncheon meat.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the sear

- 4 oz potatoes, drained

- 3 oz luncheon meat (午餐肉), sliced 1/4 in thick

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the toss

1. Slice the **luncheon meat** 1/4 in thick. If the potatoes are still in the water, drain them. Pat the **drained potatoes** very dry.
2. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium-high. Add the **peanut oil for the sear**. When it shimmers, about 2 minutes, add half of the **drained potatoes** in one layer in the bottom. Cook until the underside is gold, about 2 minutes. Turn the slices. Cook until both sides are gold and the center is tender, about 2 minutes more. If they scorch, turn the coil off and stir until they cool. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high. Lift them out.
3. Add the remaining half of the **drained potatoes**. Cook them the same way. Lift them out.
4. Add the **luncheon meat** in one layer in the bottom. Cook until the edges brown, about 1 minute. Turn the slices and brown the other side, about 1 minute. If they blacken, turn the coil off and stir until they cool. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high. Lift them out.
5. Turn the coil off. Add the **peanut oil for the toss**.

## Toss

Cut these while the coil cools. Use the thin marbled slices sold for hot pot, the coarse Pixian paste, medium 二荆条 flakes marked 中辣, plain red chili oil, and unsalted Shaoxing.

- 2 tbsp peanut oil, for the toss

- 6 dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced

- 1 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped

- 1 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)
- 1 tsp ground cumin (孜然)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)

- 4 oz cauliflower, cut into 1 in florets, blanched
- 4 oz napa cabbage, cut into 2 in pieces, blanched
- 3 oz enoki mushrooms (金针菇), blanched
- 1/2 oz dried wood ear (干黑木耳), soaked
- 4 oz potatoes, seared
- 3 oz luncheon meat (午餐肉), sliced 1/4 in thick, seared

- 4 oz hot-pot beef slices (肥牛)

- 1 tbsp chili oil (红油)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)
- 1 scallion, cut into 1/2 in lengths
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

1. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Slice the **ginger** and the **garlic**. Chop the **Pixian doubanjiang**. Cut the **scallion** into 1/2 in lengths. Chop the **cilantro**. If the **hot-pot beef slices** are frozen together, separate them while they are still cold. Pat them dry. If the wood ear is still soaking, wait until the timer rings, then drain it, rinse it, and tear it into bites.
2. When the **peanut oil for the toss** has stopped smoking and the coil has stopped glowing, turn the coil to medium-low. When the oil shimmers, add the **dried facing-heaven chilies** and the **Sichuan peppercorns** together. Stir until the peppercorns smell citrusy and the chilies turn one shade darker, still red, about 20 seconds. If they blacken, turn the coil off and stir until they cool. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-low.
3. Add the **ginger** and the **garlic** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 20 seconds.
4. Add the **Pixian doubanjiang**. Stir until the oil turns red and the paste smells sweet, about 45 seconds. If it smells sharp, turn the coil off and stir until the smell sweetens. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-low.
5. Add the **Sichuan chili flakes**, the **ground cumin**, the **sugar**, the **light soy sauce**, and the **Shaoxing wine** together. Stir until the wine cooks off, about 15 seconds.
6. Turn the coil to medium. Add the **blanched cauliflower**, the **blanched napa cabbage**, the **blanched enoki mushrooms**, the **soaked wood ear**, the **seared potatoes**, and the **seared luncheon meat** together. Toss until every piece is coated and no liquid pools, about 45 seconds. If liquid pools, keep the food up the sides and let the bottom cook until it looks dry.
7. Push the pot up the sides. Add the **hot-pot beef slices** in the bottom. Toss until the pink is gone and the slices are still tender, about 30 seconds. Fold the pot back down. If they stiffen, turn the coil off.
8. Turn the coil off. Add the **chili oil**, the **toasted sesame oil**, the **white sesame seeds**, the **scallion**, and the **cilantro** together. Toss until the scallion smells sweet and the cilantro wilts, about 15 seconds.
9. Eat the pot hot. The chilies and the peppercorns stay in it for their aroma.
