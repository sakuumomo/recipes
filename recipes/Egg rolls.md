# Egg rolls

Serves 2. About 75 minutes.

The vegetables blanch in a deep heavy saucepan. Dry that saucepan, then fry the rolls in it.

Cut the filling, then blanch it and stay with the pot. Wrap the rolls, then heat the oil.

## Filling

- 8 oz green cabbage, shredded
- 1 oz carrot, shredded
- 2 oz celery, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1 scallion, chopped

<!-- cup -->

- 4 oz char siu (叉烧), cut into 1/4 in dice

<!-- cup -->

- 6 cups water, for blanching

<!-- cup -->

- 6 cups cold water, for cooling

<!-- cup -->

- 1 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 3/4 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)
- 1 1/2 tsp peanut oil, for the filling
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper

1. Shred the **green cabbage** into 1/4 in strips.
2. Shred the **carrot** into thin strips.
3. Slice the **celery** thin.
4. Chop the **scallion** fine.
5. Cut the **char siu** into 1/4 in dice.
6. Put the **water for blanching** in a deep heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. When the water boils, add the **green cabbage**, the **carrot**, and the **celery** together. Cook until the cabbage is limp and still bright, about 2 minutes.
7. Lift the vegetables into the **cold water for cooling**. Stir until they are cool to the touch, about 1 minute.
8. Drain them. Squeeze them in a clean kitchen towel until they stop dripping and the ball feels dry. If the towel still drips, squeeze once more.
9. Empty the saucepan. Turn the coil off. Wipe it until a dry towel comes away dry.
10. Put the **green cabbage**, the **carrot**, and the **celery** in a bowl. Add the **scallion**, the **char siu**, the **kosher salt**, the **sugar**, the **toasted sesame oil**, the **peanut oil for the filling**, and the **ground white pepper** together. Toss until the mixture looks evenly coated, about 30 seconds.

## Rolls

Use thick square wrappers from the refrigerator case. Thin frozen sheets and rice paper will not blister.

- 6 egg roll wrappers

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp water, for the seal

**Filling**

- 8 oz green cabbage, shredded, mixed
- 1 oz carrot, shredded, mixed
- 2 oz celery, sliced thin, mixed

<!-- cup -->

- 1 scallion, chopped, mixed

<!-- cup -->

- 4 oz char siu (叉烧), cut into 1/4 in dice, mixed

<!-- cup -->

- 1 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, mixed
- 1/2 tsp sugar, mixed
- 3/4 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油), mixed
- 1 1/2 tsp peanut oil, for the filling, mixed
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, mixed

1. Put the **water for the seal** in a cup.
2. Keep the **egg roll wrappers** covered with a dry towel so the edges stay soft.
3. Divide the mixed filling into 6 even logs.
4. Set one of the **egg roll wrappers** down as a diamond, with a corner toward you. Set one log across the lower third.
5. Roll the near corner over the log and tuck it under. Fold the left and right corners in so the ends of the log are closed.
6. Wet the far corner with the **water for the seal**. Roll forward until that corner lies flat and the seam is closed. Set the roll seam side down.
7. Repeat with the remaining **egg roll wrappers** and logs. Keep the finished rolls covered.

## Fry

- 4 cups peanut oil, for frying

<!-- cup -->

- 6 egg roll wrappers, rolled

1. Put the **peanut oil for frying** in the dry saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high. Stay with the thermometer. Heat until it reads 350°F, about 8 minutes. Pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to the setting that holds 350°F. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on.
2. Slide 3 of the **egg roll wrappers** in, seam side down, away from you. Leave room between them. Fry until the bottoms are deep golden, about 2 minutes, then turn them. Fry until they are deep golden all over and the bubbles around them get louder, about 3 minutes more. If a side stays pale, turn it. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the oil returns to 350°F, turn the coil back to the setting that holds it and set the saucepan fully back on. Lift them out with a slotted spoon and stand them on a plate.
3. Let the oil return to 350°F on the setting that holds it. Fry the remaining **egg roll wrappers** the same way. Stand them on the plate.
4. Turn the coil off. Eat them while the wrappers are still crisp.
