# Egg rolls

Serves 2. About 75 minutes.

The vegetables blanch in a deep heavy saucepan. The rolls fry on the Mosaic at 350°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

## Filling

- 3 1/4 cups green cabbage, shredded
- 1/4 cup carrot, shredded
- 1/2 cup celery, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- white of 1 scallion, halved lengthwise and chopped fine
- green of 1 scallion, chopped fine

<!-- cup -->

- 4 oz char siu (叉烧), cut into 1/4 in dice

<!-- cup -->

- 6 cups cold water, for cooling

<!-- cup -->

- 6 cups water, for blanching

<!-- cup -->

- 1 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 3/4 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)
- 1 1/2 tsp peanut oil, for the filling
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper

1. With the 8 in cleaver, shred the **green cabbage** into 1/4 in strips.
2. With the 8 in cleaver, shred the **carrot** into thin strips.
3. With the 8 in cleaver, slice the **celery** thin.
4. With the 8 in cleaver, halve the **white of 1 scallion** lengthwise and chop it fine. Chop the **green of 1 scallion** fine. They come from the same scallion.
5. With the 8 in cleaver, cut the **char siu** into 1/4 in dice.
6. Put the **cold water for cooling** in a bowl.
7. Put the **water for blanching** in a deep heavy saucepan. Set the saucepan fully on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. When the water boils, add the **green cabbage**, the **carrot**, and the **celery** together. Cook until the cabbage is limp and still bright, about 2 minutes. Lift the vegetables into the **cold water for cooling**.
8. Pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil off. Empty it. Wipe the saucepan until a dry towel comes away dry.
9. Stir the vegetables until they are cool to the touch, about 1 minute.
10. Drain them. Squeeze them in a clean kitchen towel until they stop dripping and the ball feels dry. If the towel still drips, squeeze once more.
11. Put the **green cabbage**, the **carrot**, and the **celery** in a bowl. Add the **white of 1 scallion**, the **green of 1 scallion**, the **char siu**, the **kosher salt**, the **sugar**, the **toasted sesame oil**, the **peanut oil for the filling**, and the **ground white pepper** together. Toss until the mixture looks evenly coated, about 30 seconds.

## Rolls

Use thick square wrappers from the refrigerator case. Thin frozen sheets and rice paper will not blister.

- 6 egg roll wrappers

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp water, for the seal

**Filling**

- 3 1/4 cups green cabbage, shredded, mixed
- 1/4 cup carrot, shredded, mixed
- 1/2 cup celery, sliced thin, mixed

<!-- cup -->

- white of 1 scallion, halved lengthwise and chopped fine, mixed
- green of 1 scallion, chopped fine, mixed

<!-- cup -->

- 4 oz char siu (叉烧), cut into 1/4 in dice, mixed

<!-- cup -->

- 1 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, mixed
- 1/2 tsp sugar, mixed
- 3/4 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油), mixed
- 1 1/2 tsp peanut oil, for the filling, mixed
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, mixed

1. Put the **water for the seal** in a cup.
2. Keep the **egg roll wrappers** covered with a damp towel so the edges stay soft.
3. Divide the mixed filling into 6 even logs.
4. Set one of the **egg roll wrappers** down as a diamond, with a corner toward you. Set one log across the lower third.
5. Roll the near corner over the log and tuck it under. Fold the left and right corners in so the ends of the log are closed.
6. Wet the far corner with the **water for the seal**. Roll forward until that corner lies flat and the seam is closed. Set the roll seam side down.
7. Repeat with the remaining **egg roll wrappers** and logs. Keep the finished rolls covered.

## Fry

- 4 cups peanut oil, for frying

<!-- cup -->

- 6 egg roll wrappers, rolled

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 350°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Add the **peanut oil for frying**. Stay with the thermometer. Keep the probe in the oil and off the steel. Heat until the oil reads 350°F. If the oil stalls short, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When it returns, press start.
2. Slide 3 of the **egg roll wrappers** in, seam side down, away from you. Leave room between them so the oil covers them. Fry until the bottoms are deep golden, about 2 minutes, then turn them with cooking chopsticks. Fry until they are deep golden all over and the bubbles around them get louder, about 3 minutes more. If a side stays pale, turn it. Lift them out with a slotted spoon and stand them on a plate.
3. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Fry the remaining **egg roll wrappers** the same way. Stand them on the plate.
4. Press on/off. Eat them while the wrappers are still crisp.

---

Add 1 tsp ginger, minced, with the scallion white, the scallion green, and the char siu.
