# Ants climbing a tree (蚂蚁上树)

Serves 2. About 30 minutes.

Noodles soak in a bowl. Stir-fry on the Mosaic at 300°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Soak the noodles first. Mince while they soften. When a strand bends without breaking, drain them, then keep going.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Noodles

Use thin threads with mung bean starch first. A little pea starch is fine, and thick sweet potato noodles go soft and gluey.

- 4 oz mung bean vermicelli (绿豆粉丝)
- 4 cups water, for the noodles

1. Put the **mung bean vermicelli** in a bowl. If the bundle is tied, cut off the tie and loosen the strands.
2. Pour the **water for the noodles** over them. Go on to the stir-fry. They are ready when a strand bends without breaking, about 10 minutes.

## Stir-fry

Use ground pork that shows some fat, the coarse Pixian paste, medium 二荆条 flakes marked 中辣, and unsalted Shaoxing.

- 1 1/2 tbsp peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan chili flakes (四川辣椒面)

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp ginger, minced

<!-- cup -->

- 5 oz ground pork

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 1/2 cups unsalted chicken stock

<!-- cup -->

- 4 oz mung bean vermicelli (绿豆粉丝), drained

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tsp garlic, minced
- 2 scallions, minced

1. Put each group into one cup or pile, prepared the way the line says. When a noodle strand bends without breaking, drain the noodles and set them with the cups. If a strand is still stiff, wait.
2. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, add the **Pixian doubanjiang** and the **Sichuan chili flakes** together. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start.
3. Fry, stirring, until the oil is red and smells toasty, about 30 seconds. If it smells sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start. If the oil has not turned red, turn the left dial up 10°F and keep frying until it is red.
4. Add the **ginger**. Stir until it smells sweet, about 15 seconds. If it colors, press pause/clear once and stir until it cools. Press start.
5. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Add the **ground pork**. Break it into small bits and stir until the bits are browned, about 3 minutes. If the bits are still pink, cook until the pink is gone, about 1 minute more.
6. Add the **Shaoxing wine** and the **light soy sauce** together. Stir until the liquid is gone, about 30 seconds.
7. Add the **sugar**, the **kosher salt**, the **ground white pepper**, and the **unsalted chicken stock** together. Stay until the surface simmers. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles. Press start.
8. Add the **drained mung bean vermicelli**. Stir until the noodles are translucent and only a thin sauce is left, about 6 minutes. If the sauce is still pooling, push the noodles up the sides and let the bottom reduce, then fold them back down. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles. Press start. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
9. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **garlic** and the **scallions** together. Stir until the scallions smell sweet, about 30 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start. Press on/off. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精).
10. Eat it hot.
