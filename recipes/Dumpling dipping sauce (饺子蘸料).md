## Sauce

Use plain red chili oil. Mince before the skillet is hot.

- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- 1 scallion, minced
- 1/8 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tbsp Zhenjiang vinegar (镇江香醋)
- 1/2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp chili oil (红油)
- 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)

1. Mince the **garlic** and the **scallion** into a heatproof bowl. Add the **kosher salt**.
2. Put the **peanut oil** in a small skillet. Set the skillet on a coil turned to medium. Heat until a dry cooking chopstick makes small bubbles and the oil has not yet smoked, about 2 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil and turn that coil off. If the oil is smoking, wait until the smoke stops.
3. Pour the **peanut oil** over the **garlic** and the **scallion**. It should sizzle, and the raw garlic smell should turn sweet, about 30 seconds.
4. Add the **Zhenjiang vinegar**, the **light soy sauce**, the **chili oil**, and the **toasted sesame oil** together. Stir until the sauce looks reddish.
5. Leave the bowl 10 minutes. The garlic smells sweet and the vinegar smells malty. Stir once more. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精).
