# Braised beef brisket with potatoes (土豆烧牛腩)

Serves 2. About 130 minutes.

The beef blanches in a heavy saucepan. The same saucepan stews it.

Blanch the beef first. Peel the potatoes when the beef has cooked about 70 minutes.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Beef**

- 16 oz beef brisket (牛腩)
- 8 cups water, for the blanch

**Stew**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 star anise (八角)
- 1 piece cassia bark (桂皮), 2 in
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 3 1/2 cups water, for the stew
- 12 oz potatoes
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

---

## Beef

Leave the fat on so the chunks stay moist.

- 16 oz beef brisket (牛腩)
- 8 cups water, for the blanch

1. Cut the **beef brisket** into 1 1/2 in chunks.
2. Put the **beef brisket** and the **water for the blanch** in a heavy saucepan. Set it on high. Skim the gray foam as it rises. When the water boils, cook 2 minutes.
3. Lift the beef out. Rinse it under hot water until the scum is gone, and drain it. Pour off the blanch water and wipe the saucepan dry.

## Stew

Use a waxy potato so the chunks stay whole.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 star anise (八角)
- 1 piece cassia bark (桂皮), 2 in
- 16 oz beef brisket (牛腩), blanched
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 1 tsp sugar
- 3 1/2 cups water, for the stew
- 12 oz potatoes
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

1. Slice the **onion**. Slice the **ginger**. Smash the **garlic**.
2. Set the saucepan on medium. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, about 1 minute, add the **onion**. Stir until it is soft and the edges brown, about 4 minutes. If it starts to scorch, pull the saucepan half off the coil.
3. Add the **ginger**, **garlic**, **star anise**, and **cassia bark** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 1 minute.
4. Add the **blanched beef brisket**. Stir until the cut edges brown, about 4 minutes. If the oil smokes, pull the saucepan off the coil until the smoke stops, then set it back.
5. Add the **Shaoxing wine**, **light soy sauce**, **dark soy sauce**, and **sugar** together. Stir until the beef looks reddish-brown, about 30 seconds.
6. Pour in the **water for the stew**. Press the beef under the surface. Turn the coil to high. When it boils, skim any foam. Pull the saucepan so it sits half on the coil. The surface should bubble gently. Cover it.
7. Simmer until a fork pushed into a chunk meets little resistance, about 80 minutes. If the center boils hard, pull the saucepan farther off the coil. When the beef has cooked about 70 minutes, peel the **potatoes** and cut them into 1 1/2 in chunks, then keep the simmer going. If the beef is still firm, cover and cook 15 minutes more before you add the potatoes.
8. Add the **potatoes** and the **kosher salt**. Press the potatoes under the surface. Cover and cook until a potato breaks under a spoon, about 15 minutes. If a potato is still firm, cover and cook 5 minutes more.
9. Uncover. Set the saucepan fully on the coil at medium and cook, stirring, until the sauce coats a spoon, about 10 minutes. If it starts to stick, pull the saucepan half off the coil.
10. Pick out the star anise and the cassia bark. Eat it hot.
