# Braised beef brisket with potatoes (土豆烧牛腩)

Serves 2. About 140 minutes.

The beef blanches in the heavy saucepan. The stew browns in the wok on the Mosaic at 1500 watts, starting at 375°F.

Blanch the beef first. When the timer rings, peel the potatoes and cut them, then keep the simmer going. Boil the sauce down after the potatoes come out, then put them back.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Beef

Use the fatty plate sold as 牛腩, and leave the fat on.

- 16 oz beef brisket (牛腩)

<!-- cup -->

- 8 cups water, for the blanch

1. Cut the **beef brisket** into 1 1/2 in chunks with the cleaver.
2. Put the **beef brisket** and the **water for the blanch** together in a heavy saucepan. Set the saucepan fully on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. Skim the gray foam as it rises. When the water boils, cook 2 minutes. Pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off.
3. Lift the beef out. Rinse it under hot water until the scum is gone, and drain it. Pour off the blanch water and wipe the saucepan dry.

## Stew

Use a waxy potato so the pieces stay whole, and salted Shaoxing.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 1 small onion, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 in ginger, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 star anise (八角)
- 1 piece cassia bark (桂皮), 2 in

<!-- cup -->

- 16 oz beef brisket (牛腩), blanched

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒)
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽)
- 1 tsp sugar

<!-- cup -->

- 2 1/2 cups water, for the stew

<!-- cup -->

- 3 medium potatoes
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal

1. Slice the **onion** and the **ginger** with the cleaver. Smash the **garlic** with the side of the cleaver until the cloves split. Put each group into one cup or pile, prepared the way the line says.
2. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Turn the right dial to 3 hours. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, add the **onion**. Stir until it is soft and the edges brown, about 4 minutes. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
3. Add the **blanched beef brisket**. Stir until the cut edges brown, about 4 minutes. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start.
4. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **ginger**, **garlic**, **star anise**, and **cassia bark** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 1 minute. If they smell sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start.
5. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Add the **Shaoxing wine**, **light soy sauce**, **dark soy sauce**, and **sugar** together. Stir until the beef looks reddish-brown, about 30 seconds.
6. Pour in the **water for the stew**. Pile the beef in the bottom and press it into the liquid. Stay with it. When it boils, skim any foam.
7. Press watts until 600 shows. Turn the left dial to 185°F. Cover the wok with the glass lid. When the center boils, press pause/clear once. Press start when the surface has fallen back to a small bubble. If the surface stays still and never boils, turn the left dial up 10°F.
8. Set a timer for 70 minutes. When the timer rings, peel the potatoes, and cut them into 1 1/2 in chunks with the cleaver, then keep the simmer going until a fork pushed into the beef meets little resistance, about 10 minutes more. If the beef is still firm, cover and cook until a fork pushed into the beef meets little resistance, about 15 minutes more, before you add the potatoes. If the center boils, press pause/clear once. Press start when the surface has fallen back to a small bubble. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start. If the display reads 0, press start, turn the left dial to 185°F, and set the remaining time on the right dial. If 600 is not showing, press watts until it does. If the display reads E7, press pause/clear twice and wait until the fan stops. Seat the wok, press start, and turn the left dial to 185°F. If 600 is not showing, press watts until it does.
9. Add the **potatoes** and the **kosher salt**. Press the potatoes into the liquid. Cover the wok with the glass lid and cook until a potato breaks under a spoon, about 15 minutes. If a potato is still firm, cover and cook until it breaks under a spoon, about 5 minutes more. If the center boils, press pause/clear once. Press start when the surface has fallen back to a small bubble.
10. Uncover. Lift the potatoes out with a slotted spoon and set them aside.
11. If 1500 is not showing, press watts until it does. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Cook until the sauce coats a spoon, about 8 minutes. If it still runs like water, keep cooking until it coats a spoon. If the center boils hard, press pause/clear once until the surface settles. Press start. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
12. Fold the **potatoes** back in. If 1500 is not showing, press watts until it does. Press on/off. Pick out the star anise and the cassia bark. Taste. If it is flat, stir in one more pinch of the kosher salt. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精). Spoon it into a bowl and eat it hot. Set the wok on its stand.

---

Use the coarse Pixian paste. Add 2 tsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped, after the ginger, the garlic, the star anise, and the cassia bark smell sweet. Stir until the oil turns red, about 30 seconds. If it smells sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start. If the oil has not turned red, turn the left dial up 10°F and keep frying until it is red. Then go on to the Shaoxing wine.
