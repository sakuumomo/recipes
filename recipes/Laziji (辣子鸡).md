# Laziji (辣子鸡)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The chicken fries in a deep heavy saucepan on the coil. The toss is in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Marinate the chicken. Cut the chilies and the aromatics while it sits. Heat the oil and stay with the thermometer. Coat the chicken when the oil is ready, then fry it.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Chicken

Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 egg white

1. Cut the **chicken thighs** into 3/4 in pieces.
2. Toss them with the **Shaoxing wine**, the **light soy sauce**, the **kosher salt**, and the **egg white**. Stir until the chicken feels sticky. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the prep.

## Prep

Do this while the chicken marinates.

- 1 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 2 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the toss
- 3 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths

1. Put the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a cup.
2. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Put them in that cup.
3. Slice the **garlic** and the **ginger** thin.
4. Put the **sugar** and the **Shaoxing wine for the toss** in one cup.
5. Cut the **scallions** into 1 in lengths. Put them in that cup.

## Fry

- 3 cups peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 4 tbsp potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces, marinated

1. Put the **peanut oil** in a deep heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high. Stay with the thermometer. Heat until it reads 350°F, about 8 minutes. Pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to the setting that holds 350°F. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on.
2. When the oil is ready and the chicken has finished its 15 minutes, toss the chicken with the **potato starch** until each piece has a thin, even coat.
3. Add 8 oz of the **marinated chicken thighs** in one layer so the oil covers every piece. Leave them still for 1 minute so the coating sets, then turn any pale side. Fry until the coating is set, the chicken is cooked through, and the pieces are pale gold, about 4 minutes. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the oil returns to 350°F, turn the coil back to the setting that holds it and set the saucepan fully back on. Lift the chicken out with a slotted spoon.
4. Let the oil return to 350°F on the setting that holds it. Fry the remaining **marinated chicken thighs** the same way. Lift them out.
5. Turn the coil up and leave the saucepan fully on. Stay with the thermometer. When it reads 375°F, pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to the setting that holds 375°F. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on.
6. Return 8 oz of the **marinated chicken thighs**. Fry until deep golden and crisp, about 1 minute. If the oil smokes or climbs past 375°F, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the oil returns to 375°F, turn the coil back to the setting that holds it and set the saucepan fully back on. Lift them out.
7. Let the oil return to 375°F on the setting that holds it. Fry the remaining **marinated chicken thighs** until deep golden and crisp, about 1 minute. If the oil smokes or climbs past 375°F, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the oil returns to 375°F, turn the coil back to the setting that holds it and set the saucepan fully back on. Lift them out.
8. Turn the coil off. Pour the oil slowly into a heatproof bowl until 2 tbsp remains in the saucepan.

## Toss

These amounts were measured above.

**Chicken**

- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces, fried

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 2 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the toss
- 3 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths

**Fry**

- 3 cups peanut oil, all but 2 tbsp poured off

1. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium-low. Pour in the remaining **peanut oil**.
2. When the oil shimmers, add the **Sichuan peppercorns** and the **dried facing-heaven chilies** together. Stir until the chilies smell toasty and turn one shade darker, still red, about 30 seconds. If they blacken, turn the coil off and stir until they cool. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-low.
3. Add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until fragrant, about 15 seconds.
4. Add the **fried chicken thighs**, the **sugar**, the **Shaoxing wine for the toss**, and the **scallions** together. Toss until the chicken is coated and the wine has cooked off, about 1 minute.
5. Eat the chicken and the scallions. The chilies and the peppercorns stay on the plate for their aroma.
