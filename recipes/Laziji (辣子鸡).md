# Laziji (辣子鸡)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The chicken fries in the wok on the Mosaic at 350°F. The toss is in that wok at 300°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Marinate the chicken. Cut the chilies and the aromatics while it sits. When the timer rings, leave the chicken and keep going. Heat the oil and stay with the thermometer. Coat the chicken when the oil is ready, then fry it.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Chicken

Use salted Shaoxing.

- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒)
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 egg white

1. Cut the **chicken thighs** into 3/4 in pieces with the 8 in cleaver.
2. Toss them with the **Shaoxing wine**, the **light soy sauce**, the **kosher salt**, and the **egg white**. Stir until the chicken feels sticky. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the prep. When the timer rings, leave the chicken and keep going.

## Prep

Do this while the chicken marinates. Use whole dried erjingtiao marked 二荆条.

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced thin
- 1 in ginger, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 20 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒), for the toss
- whites of 3 scallions, halved lengthwise and cut into 1/2 in pieces
- greens of 3 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths

1. With the 8 in cleaver, slice the **garlic** and the **ginger** thin. Put them in one cup.
2. Put the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a bowl.
3. Snip the **dried erjingtiao** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Put them in that bowl.
4. Put the **sugar** and the **Shaoxing wine for the toss** in one cup.
5. With the 8 in cleaver, halve the **whites of 3 scallions** lengthwise and cut them into 1/2 in pieces. Cut the **greens of 3 scallions** into 1 in lengths. They come from the same 3 scallions. Put them in that cup.

## Fry

- 3 cups peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 4 tbsp potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces, marinated

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 350°F. Turn the right dial to 40 minutes. Put the **peanut oil** in the wok. Set the thermometer so the probe is in the oil and off the steel. Stay with the thermometer until it reads 350°F. If the oil stalls short, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When it returns to 350°F, press start.
2. If the chicken has not finished its 15 minutes, wait. Toss the **marinated chicken thighs** with the **potato starch** until each piece has a thin, even coat.
3. Add 8 oz of the **marinated chicken thighs** in one layer in the bottom so the oil covers every piece. Leave them still for 1 minute so the coating sets, then turn any pale side with cooking chopsticks. Fry until the coating is set, the chicken is cooked through, and the pieces are pale gold, about 4 minutes. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
4. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Fry the remaining **marinated chicken thighs** the same way. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
5. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Stay with the thermometer until it reads 375°F. If the oil stalls short, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 375°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When it returns to 375°F, press start.
6. Return 8 oz of the **marinated chicken thighs** to the bottom so the oil covers every piece. Fry until deep golden and crisp, about 1 minute. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
7. Bring the oil back to 375°F. Fry the remaining **marinated chicken thighs** in the bottom until deep golden and crisp, about 1 minute. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
8. Ladle the spare **peanut oil** into a heatproof bowl with the wok ladle until 2 tbsp remains. Leave the wok seated.

## Toss

These amounts were measured above. The wok is still seated.

**Fry**

- 3 cups peanut oil, all but 2 tbsp ladled out

**Prep**

- 3 cloves garlic, sliced thin
- 1 in ginger, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 20 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒), for the toss
- whites of 3 scallions, halved lengthwise and cut into 1/2 in pieces
- greens of 3 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths

**Chicken**

- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces, fried

1. Turn the left dial to 300°F. When the remaining **peanut oil** shimmers and has stopped smoking, go on.
2. Add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until fragrant, about 15 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
3. Add the **Sichuan peppercorns** and the **dried erjingtiao** together. Stir until the chilies smell toasty and turn one shade darker, still red, about 30 seconds. If they blacken, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
4. Add the **fried chicken thighs**, the **sugar**, the **Shaoxing wine for the toss**, the **whites of 3 scallions**, and the **greens of 3 scallions** together. Toss until the chicken is coated and the wine has cooked off, about 1 minute. Press on/off.
5. Scoop the chicken, the scallion whites, the scallion greens, the chilies, and the peppercorns onto a plate. Set the wok on its stand. Eat the chicken, the scallion whites, and the scallion greens. The chilies and the peppercorns stay on the plate for their aroma.
