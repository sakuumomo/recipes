# Laziji (辣子鸡)

Serves 2. About 45 minutes.

The chicken fries in a deep heavy saucepan on the coil. The toss is in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Marinate the chicken. While it sits, cut the chilies and the aromatics, and start the oil. Coat the chicken when the oil is ready, then fry it.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Chicken**

- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 egg white

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 2 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced thin
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the toss
- 3 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths

**Fry**

- 2 cups peanut oil
- 4 tbsp potato starch

---

## Chicken

- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)
- 2 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1 egg white

1. Cut the **chicken thighs** into 3/4 in pieces.
2. Toss them with the **Shaoxing wine**, the **light soy sauce**, the **kosher salt**, and the **egg white**. Stir until the chicken feels sticky. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Go on to the prep.

## Prep

Do this while the chicken marinates.

- 1 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 2 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced thin
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the toss
- 3 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths

1. Put the **Sichuan peppercorns** in a cup.
2. Snip the **dried facing-heaven chilies** in half and shake out the loose seeds.
3. Slice the **garlic** and the **ginger** thin.
4. Put the **sugar** and the **Shaoxing wine** in cups.
5. Cut the **scallions** into 1 in lengths. Set them with the other cups.

## Fry

- 2 cups peanut oil
- 4 tbsp potato starch
- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces, marinated

1. Put the **peanut oil** in a deep heavy saucepan. Set it on a coil turned to high. Heat until a wooden spoon handle bubbles hard as soon as it goes in, about 8 minutes. If the oil smokes, pull the saucepan off the coil until the smoke stops, then set it back.
2. When the oil is ready and the chicken has finished its 15 minutes, toss the chicken with the **potato starch** until each piece has a thin, even coat.
3. Add 8 oz of the **chicken thighs** at once. Leave them still for 1 minute so the coating sets, then turn any pale side. Fry until the coating is set, the chicken is cooked through, and the pieces are pale gold, about 4 minutes. If the oil smokes, pull the saucepan off the coil until the smoke stops, then set it back. Lift the chicken out with a slotted spoon.
4. Let the oil return to a hard bubble. Fry the remaining 8 oz the same way. Lift them out.
5. When the spoon handle bubbles hard again, return 8 oz of the chicken. Fry until deep golden and crisp, about 1 minute. Lift them out.
6. Let the oil return to a hard bubble. Fry the remaining 8 oz until deep golden and crisp, about 1 minute. Lift them out.
7. Turn the coil off. Pour the oil slowly into a heatproof bowl until 2 tbsp remains in the saucepan.

## Toss

These amounts were measured above.

**Chicken**

- 16 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3/4 in pieces, fried

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)
- 2 oz dried facing-heaven chilies (干朝天椒), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1/2 oz garlic, sliced thin
- 1/2 oz ginger, sliced thin
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒), for the toss
- 3 scallions, cut into 1 in lengths

**Fry**

- 2 cups peanut oil, all but 2 tbsp poured off

1. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium-low. Pour in the remaining **peanut oil**.
2. When the oil shimmers, add the **Sichuan peppercorns** and the **dried facing-heaven chilies** together. Stir until the chilies smell toasty and turn one shade darker, still red, about 30 seconds. If they blacken, turn the coil off and stir until they cool, then turn the coil back to medium-low.
3. Add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until fragrant, about 15 seconds.
4. Add the **chicken thighs**, the **sugar**, the **Shaoxing wine**, and the **scallions** together. Toss until the chicken is coated and the wine has cooked off, about 1 minute.
5. Eat the chicken and the scallions. The chilies and the peppercorns stay on the plate for their aroma.
