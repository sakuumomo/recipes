# Fried dumplings (锅贴)

Serves 2. About 75 minutes.

The filling is mixed in a bowl. The dumplings fry, then steam, in a 10 in heavy skillet on the coil.

Salt the cabbage first. Mince the scallions and ginger while it weeps, then wrap. Fry 8 dumplings at a time.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Filling**

- 8 oz napa cabbage
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the cabbage
- 2 scallions, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 8 oz ground pork
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tbsp cold water, for the pork

**Dumplings**

- 16 dumpling wrappers (饺子皮)
- 1 tbsp water, for the seal

**Fry**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the first batch
- 1/2 cup water, for the first batch
- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the second batch
- 1/2 cup water, for the second batch

## Filling

Use ground pork that shows some fat.

- 8 oz napa cabbage
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the cabbage
- 2 scallions, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 8 oz ground pork
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油)
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tbsp cold water, for the pork

1. Cut the core from the **napa cabbage**. Slice the leaves thin, then chop them fine.
2. Toss the cabbage with the **kosher salt for the cabbage** in a colander. Set the colander in the sink. Set a timer for 10 minutes. Go on to the next step.
3. Mince the **scallions**.
4. Mince the **ginger**.
5. When the timer rings, squeeze the cabbage by the handful until it stops dripping and a handful feels dry. If it has not rung, wait. The cabbage should look wet and slumped.
6. Put the **ground pork** in a bowl. Add the **light soy sauce**, the **toasted sesame oil**, and the **ground white pepper** together. Stir in one direction until the meat turns sticky and pale, about 1 minute.
7. Add the **cold water for the pork** a spoon at a time, still stirring in one direction, until the liquid disappears and the paste looks glossy.
8. Add the squeezed **napa cabbage**, the **scallions**, and the **ginger** together. Stir until the cabbage is even through the meat.

## Dumplings

Use round white Shanghai-style wheat wrappers from the refrigerator or freezer case. If they are frozen, thaw them covered until a wrapper bends without cracking; a thick square egg roll wrapper will not pleat.

- 16 dumpling wrappers (饺子皮)
- 1 tbsp water, for the seal

**Filling**

- 8 oz napa cabbage, mixed
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, for the cabbage, mixed
- 2 scallions, minced, mixed
- 1 tsp ginger, minced, mixed
- 8 oz ground pork, mixed
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce (生抽), mixed
- 1 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油), mixed
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, mixed
- 2 tbsp cold water, for the pork, mixed

1. Put the **water for the seal** in a cup.
2. Divide the mixed filling into 16 even portions.
3. Keep the **dumpling wrappers** covered with a dry towel.
4. Lay one wrapper flat. Set one portion in the center and leave a bare rim.
5. Wet the rim with the **water for the seal**. Fold the wrapper in half so the edges meet. Press the middle of the seam shut and push the air out.
6. Starting at one end of the seam, make 4 pleats along the near edge, pressing each pleat onto the far edge. The dumpling sits on a flat bottom with the pleats on top.
7. Set it pleat side up and keep it covered. Repeat with the remaining **dumpling wrappers**. If a wrapper tears, press the tear shut and pleat it again.

## Fry

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the first batch
- 1/2 cup water, for the first batch
- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the second batch
- 1/2 cup water, for the second batch
- 16 dumpling wrappers (饺子皮), wrapped

1. Put the **peanut oil for the first batch** in a 10 in heavy skillet. Set the skillet on medium. Heat until the oil shimmers, about 2 minutes. If it smokes, pull the skillet fully off and turn the coil off. When the smoke stops, turn the coil back to medium and set the skillet fully back on.
2. Set 8 of the **dumpling wrappers** in, flat bottom down, with space between them. Fry without moving them until the bottoms are pale gold, about 2 minutes.
3. Hold the lid in front of the skillet. Pour in the **water for the first batch** and cover at once. The oil will spit.
4. Cook until the water is gone and the tops look glossy, about 6 minutes. If the bottoms smell sharp before the water is gone, pull the skillet fully off and turn the coil off. When the smell sweetens, turn the coil back to medium and set the skillet fully back on.
5. Take the lid off. Cook until the bottoms are deep golden and the skillet sounds dry, about 1 minute. If a dumpling sticks, leave it 30 seconds and lift again. Lift them out crisp side up and cover the plate.
6. Wipe out any stuck bits. Put the **peanut oil for the second batch** in the skillet. Set it back on medium. Heat until the oil shimmers, about 1 minute. If it smokes, pull the skillet fully off and turn the coil off. When the smoke stops, turn the coil back to medium and set the skillet fully back on.
7. Set the remaining **dumpling wrappers** in, flat bottom down, with space between them. Fry without moving them until the bottoms are pale gold, about 2 minutes.
8. Hold the lid in front of the skillet. Pour in the **water for the second batch** and cover at once.
9. Cook until the water is gone and the tops look glossy, about 6 minutes. If the bottoms smell sharp, pull the skillet fully off and turn the coil off. When the smell sweetens, turn the coil back to medium and set the skillet fully back on.
10. Take the lid off. Cook until the bottoms are deep golden and the skillet sounds dry, about 1 minute. Lift them out crisp side up.
11. Eat them hot with Dumpling dipping sauce (饺子蘸料).
