# Twice-cooked pork (回锅肉)

Serves 2. About 60 minutes.

The pork simmers in a heavy saucepan. Stir-fry on the Mosaic at 375°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Start the pork. While it simmers, set out the stir-fry. When the timer rings, check the pork, then slice it and stir-fry.

## Pork

Leave the skin on, and use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 12 oz pork belly

<!-- cup -->

- 6 cups water

<!-- cup -->

- 1 in ginger, sliced
- 2 scallions, cut into 2 in lengths
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)

1. With the 8 in cleaver, slice the **ginger**. Cut the **scallions** into 2 in lengths. Put them in one cup with the **Shaoxing wine**. Leave the **pork belly** in one piece.
2. Put the **pork belly** and the **water** in a heavy saucepan, and add the **ginger**, the **scallions**, and the **Shaoxing wine** with them. Set the saucepan fully on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. Skim the gray foam as it rises. When the water boils, pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to low. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on. The surface should bubble gently. Cover it. Set a timer for 30 minutes. Go on to Prep. If the center boils hard while you are away, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the bubble stops, turn the coil back to low and set the saucepan fully back on. If it boils hard again, do the same again.
3. When the timer rings, push a cooking chopstick into the thickest part. It should go through, and the juice should run clear. If the juice is still pink, cook until it runs clear, about 10 minutes more. If the surface goes still, turn the coil one setting higher and leave the saucepan fully on. If the slab starts to split apart, take it out.
4. Turn the coil off. Lift the pork onto a plate. Pour off the water. Discard the ginger and the scallions.
5. Cool until the slab is firm enough to slice, about 15 minutes. If it smears under the knife, cool until it slices cleanly, about 10 minutes more.
6. With the 8 in cleaver, slice it across the layers, about 1/8 in thick, so each slice shows skin, fat, and meat. Pat the slices dry. Put them in a pile.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 1 1/2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tsp sweet wheat sauce (甜面酱)
- 2 tsp fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and lightly crushed

<!-- cup -->

- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1/2 in ginger, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp sugar

<!-- cup -->

- 1 1/2 handfuls garlic sprouts (蒜苗), cut into 1 1/2 in lengths

**Pork**

- 12 oz pork belly, sliced

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, add half of the **sliced pork belly** in one layer in the bottom. Stir until those slices cup at the edges, the fat looks translucent, and a few spots are golden, about 3 minutes. If they are still flat and the fat is opaque, cook until they cup, about 2 minutes more. If the oil smokes or the edges blacken, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops and the edges cool. Press start. Lift them onto a plate.
2. Add the rest of the **sliced pork belly** in one layer in the bottom. Stir until those slices cup at the edges, the fat looks translucent, and a few spots are golden, about 3 minutes. If they are still flat and the fat is opaque, cook until they cup, about 2 minutes more. If the oil smokes or the edges blacken, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops and the edges cool. Press start.
3. Push the slices up the sides. With the wok ladle, take the extra fat out into a heatproof bowl until a thin layer is left. The wok stays seated.
4. Turn the left dial to 300°F. When the fat in the bottom shimmers and has stopped smoking, add the **Pixian doubanjiang**. Stir until the oil turns red and smells sweet, about 30 seconds. If it smells sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start. If the oil has not turned red, turn the left dial up 10°F and keep frying until it is red.
5. Add the **sweet wheat sauce** and the **fermented black beans** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 20 seconds. If they smell sharp, press pause/clear once and stir until the smell sweetens. Press start.
6. Add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 20 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
7. Add the **light soy sauce** and the **sugar** together. Fold the pork on the sides down into the sauce, and add the pork from the plate. Stir until the slices look glossy, about 30 seconds.
8. Add the **garlic sprouts**. Stir until they turn bright green and wilt but still have a bite, about 1 minute. If they are still raw and sharp, cook until they wilt, about 1 minute more. If they brown, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
9. Press on/off. Taste. If it is flat, add a few drops of the light soy sauce. If you want it, stir in one pinch of MSG (味精). Eat it hot. The sprouts soften as it sits.

---

Add 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce (老抽) with the light soy sauce and the sugar.

When the Mosaic is off, add 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油) and stir once.

If there are no garlic sprouts, use thick Chinese scallions so the pieces stay crisp. Cut 1 1/2 handfuls thick Chinese scallions (大葱) into 1 1/2 in lengths and use them in that step. Add another 2 cloves garlic, sliced, with the white pieces. Stir until the whites smell sweet, about 30 seconds, then add the green pieces and stir until they are bright and still have bite, about 30 seconds.
