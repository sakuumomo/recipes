# Twice-cooked pork (回锅肉)

Serves 2. About 60 minutes.

The pork simmers in a heavy saucepan. The stir-fry is in the wok, and the wok stays in its ring.

Start the pork. While it simmers, set out the stir-fry. When the timer rings, check the pork, then slice it and stir-fry.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Pork

Leave the skin on. Use unsalted Shaoxing.

- 12 oz pork belly
- 6 cups water

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tbsp ginger, sliced
- 2 scallions, cut into 2 in lengths
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴酒)

1. Slice the **ginger**. Cut the **scallions** into 2 in lengths. Put them in one cup with the **Shaoxing wine**. Leave the **pork belly** in one piece.
2. Put the **pork belly** and the **water** in a heavy saucepan, and add the **ginger**, the **scallions**, and the **Shaoxing wine** with them. Set the saucepan on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. Skim the gray foam as it rises. When the water boils, pull the saucepan fully off. Turn the coil to low. If the coil is glowing, wait until it stops. Set the saucepan fully back on. The surface should bubble gently. Cover it. Set a timer for 30 minutes. Go on to Prep. If the center boils hard while you are away, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the bubble stops, turn the coil back to low and set the saucepan fully back on.
3. When the timer rings, push a chopstick into the thickest part. It should go through, and the juice should run clear. If the juice is still pink, cook until it runs clear, about 10 minutes more. If the center boils hard, pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off. When the bubble stops, turn the coil back to low and set the saucepan fully back on. If it boils hard again, do the same again. If the surface goes still, turn the coil one setting higher and leave the saucepan fully on. If the slab starts to split apart, take it out.
4. Turn the coil off. Lift the pork onto a plate. Pour off the water. Discard the ginger and the scallions.
5. Cool until the slab is firm enough to slice, about 15 minutes. If it smears under the knife, cool until it slices cleanly, about 10 minutes more.
6. Slice it across the layers, about 1/8 in thick, so each slice shows skin, fat, and meat. Pat the slices dry. Put them in a pile.

3. If the timer has not rung, go back to the pork. Come back to the stir-fry when the slices are cut.

## Stir-fry

These amounts were measured above.

**Pork**

- 12 oz pork belly, sliced

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil

<!-- cup -->

- 1 1/2 tbsp Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱), chopped

<!-- cup -->

- 2 tsp sweet wheat sauce (甜面酱)
- 2 tsp fermented black beans (豆豉), rinsed and lightly crushed

<!-- cup -->

- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 2 tsp ginger, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/2 tsp sugar

<!-- cup -->

- 6 oz garlic sprouts (蒜苗), cut into 1 1/2 in lengths

1. Set the wok in the ring on a coil turned to medium-high. Add the **peanut oil**. When it shimmers, about 2 minutes, add half of the **sliced pork belly** in one layer in the bottom.
2. Stir until those slices cup at the edges, the fat looks translucent, and a few spots are golden, about 3 minutes. If they are still flat and the fat is opaque, cook until they cup, about 2 minutes more. If the oil smokes or the edges blacken, turn the coil off and stir until the smoke stops and the edges cool. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high. Lift them onto a plate.
3. Add the rest of the **sliced pork belly** in one layer in the bottom. Stir until those slices cup at the edges, the fat looks translucent, and a few spots are golden, about 3 minutes. If they are still flat and the fat is opaque, cook until they cup, about 2 minutes more. If the oil smokes or the edges blacken, turn the coil off and stir until the smoke stops and the edges cool. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high.
4. Push the slices up the sides. Spoon the extra fat into a heatproof bowl until a thin layer is left. The wok stays in the ring.
5. Add the **Pixian doubanjiang** to the bottom. Stir until the oil turns red and smells sweet, about 30 seconds. If it smells sharp, turn the coil off and stir until the smell sweetens. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high.
6. Add the **sweet wheat sauce** and the **fermented black beans** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 20 seconds.
7. Add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 20 seconds. If they color, turn the coil off and stir until they cool. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high.
8. Add the **light soy sauce** and the **sugar** together. Fold the pork on the sides down into the sauce, and add the pork from the plate. Stir until the slices look glossy, about 30 seconds.
9. Add the **garlic sprouts**. Stir until they turn bright green and wilt but still have a bite, about 1 minute. If they are still raw and sharp, cook until they wilt, about 1 minute more. If they brown, turn the coil off and stir until they cool. Wait until the coil stops glowing, then turn the coil back to medium-high.
10. Turn the coil off. Eat it hot. The sprouts soften as it sits.
