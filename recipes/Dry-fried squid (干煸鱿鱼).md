# Dry-fried squid (干煸鱿鱼)

Serves 2. About 35 minutes.

The squid blanches in a deep heavy saucepan. The fry starts on the Mosaic at 350°F, and the toss stays in that wok at 300°F. The Mosaic is at 1500 watts.

Cut the squid before the water goes on high. Cut the toss before the oil is hot, then fry, and toss the squid as soon as it comes out.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

Lines set close together share a cup.

## Squid

Use cleaned squid tubes, and salted Shaoxing.

- 10 oz squid tubes
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine (绍兴料酒), for the blanch

<!-- cup -->

- 6 cups water, for blanching

1. Pull the thin skin off the **squid tubes**. With the 8 in cleaver, slit each tube open and cut it into strips about 1/2 in wide and 2 in long. Rinse the strips.
2. Put the **water for blanching** in a deep heavy saucepan. Set the saucepan fully on a coil turned to high. Stay with it. When the water boils, add the **Shaoxing wine for the blanch** and the **squid tubes** together. Stir until the strips curl and turn opaque, about 15 seconds. Lift the strips out onto a plate. Pull the saucepan fully off and turn the coil off.
3. Empty the saucepan. Pat the **squid tubes** until the surfaces look dry.

## Prep

Do this before the oil is hot. Use whole dried erjingtiao marked 二荆条.

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the toss

<!-- cup -->

- 3 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

<!-- cup -->

- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp ginger, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 handful Chinese celery (香芹), cut into thin 2 in strips
- 1/4 onion, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)

1. Snip the **dried erjingtiao** in half and shake out the loose seeds. Slice the **garlic**. Slice the **ginger**. Cut the **Chinese celery** into thin strips about 2 in long. Slice the **onion** thin.
2. Put the **peanut oil for the toss** in one cup. Put the **dried erjingtiao** and the **Sichuan peppercorns** in another. Put the **garlic** and the **ginger** in another. Put the **Chinese celery** and the **onion** in another. Put the **kosher salt**, the **sugar**, and the **light soy sauce** in another.

## Fry

- 2 cups peanut oil, for frying

<!-- cup -->

- 3 tbsp potato starch

<!-- cup -->

- 10 oz squid tubes, blanched

1. Seat the wok. Press start. Turn the left dial to 350°F. Turn the right dial to 30 minutes. Add the **peanut oil for frying**. Stay with the thermometer. Keep the probe in the oil and off the steel. Heat until the oil reads 350°F. If the oil stalls short of 350°F, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, turn the left dial up another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 350°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When the oil returns to 350°F, press start.
2. Toss the **blanched squid tubes** with the **potato starch** until each strip has a thin coat. Shake off the extra starch.
3. Slide in half of the **blanched squid tubes**, a few strips at a time, in the bottom, so they stay separate and the oil still covers them. Leave them still until the coat sets, then stir until the strips have curled, about 30 seconds. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
4. Bring the oil back to 350°F. Fry the remaining half the same way. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
5. Turn the left dial to 375°F. Stay with the thermometer until the oil reads 375°F. If the oil stalls short of 375°F, turn the left dial up 10°F. If it is still short, turn the left dial up another 10°F. If the oil smokes or climbs past 375°F, press pause/clear once and leave the wok seated. When the oil returns to 375°F, press start.
6. Slide half of the **blanched squid tubes** back in, in the bottom, so the oil still covers them. Fry until the coat is pale gold and crisp, about 15 seconds. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
7. Bring the oil back to 375°F. Fry the remaining half the same way. Lift them out with a slotted spoon.
8. With the wok ladle, take the spare **peanut oil for frying** out into a bowl until a thin film is left on the bottom. Leave the wok seated.

## Toss

Do this as soon as the squid comes out.

These amounts were measured above.

**Prep**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil, for the toss

<!-- cup -->

- 3 dried erjingtiao (干二荆条), snipped in half, loose seeds shaken out
- 1/2 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (红花椒)

<!-- cup -->

- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp ginger, sliced

<!-- cup -->

- 1 handful Chinese celery (香芹), cut into thin 2 in strips
- 1/4 onion, sliced thin

<!-- cup -->

- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)

**Squid**

- 10 oz squid tubes, fried

1. Turn the left dial to 300°F. Add the **peanut oil for the toss**. When it shimmers and has stopped smoking, about 30 seconds, go on. If the oil smokes, press pause/clear once and stir until the smoke stops. Press start.
2. Add the **dried erjingtiao** and the **Sichuan peppercorns** together. Stir until the peppercorns smell citrusy and the chilies are one shade darker, still red, about 20 seconds. If they blacken, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
3. Add the **garlic** and the **ginger** together. Stir until they smell sweet, about 15 seconds. If they color, press pause/clear once and stir until they cool. Press start.
4. Add the **Chinese celery** and the **onion**. Keep them in the bottom. Toss until the celery is bright and still has bite, about 1 minute. If the bottom starts to catch, press pause/clear once and stir until it releases. Press start.
5. Add the **fried squid tubes**, the **kosher salt**, the **sugar**, and the **light soy sauce** together. Toss until the squid looks dry and the coating is still crisp, about 20 seconds. Press on/off.
6. Eat the squid, the celery, and the onion hot. The chilies and the peppercorns stay on the plate for their aroma.
