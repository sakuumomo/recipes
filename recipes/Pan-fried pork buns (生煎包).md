# Pan-fried pork buns (生煎包)

Serves 2. About 110 minutes.

The dough rises in a bowl. The sesame toasts in a small skillet. The buns fry, then steam, in a 10 in heavy skillet on the coil.

Mix the dough and leave it to rise. Toast the sesame and mix the filling while it rises. Shape the buns once the dough has doubled, then fry them after the short rest.

Measure each ingredient once. A later list is that same amount again.

## Ingredients

**Dough**

- 1 cup all-purpose flour, for the dough
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp instant yeast
- 5 tbsp lukewarm water

**Sesame**

- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

**Filling**

- 1 scallion, minced, for the filling
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 4 oz ground pork
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油), for the filling
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tbsp cold water, for the pork

**Buns**

- 1 scallion, minced, for the top
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour, for the counter

**Fry**

- 1 tbsp peanut oil
- 3/4 cup water, for the skillet

---

## Dough

- 1 cup all-purpose flour, for the dough
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp instant yeast
- 5 tbsp lukewarm water

1. Stir the **all-purpose flour for the dough**, the **sugar**, and the **instant yeast** together in a bowl.
2. Add the **lukewarm water**. It should feel barely warm on your wrist. Stir until no dry flour remains.
3. Knead until the dough is smooth and springs back when you press it, about 5 minutes.
4. Cover the bowl with a damp towel. Leave it until the dough has doubled and a finger pressed in leaves a dent, about 60 minutes. Go on to the sesame and the filling. If the dough has not doubled when those are done, wait.

## Sesame

- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻)

1. Toast the **white sesame seeds** in a small skillet over medium-low heat, shaking often, until they are golden and smell nutty, about 2 minutes. Slide the skillet off the coil and turn that coil off. If they start to smoke, slide the skillet off and turn that coil off.
2. Pour them onto a plate so they stop cooking.

## Filling

Use ground pork that shows some fat.

- 1 scallion, minced, for the filling
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 4 oz ground pork
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽)
- 1/4 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油), for the filling
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper
- 2 tbsp cold water, for the pork

1. Mince the **scallion for the filling** and the **ginger**.
2. Put the **ground pork** in a bowl. Add the **light soy sauce**, the **toasted sesame oil for the filling**, the **kosher salt**, and the **ground white pepper** together. Stir in one direction until the meat turns sticky and pale, about 1 minute.
3. Add the **cold water for the pork** a spoon at a time, still stirring in one direction, until the liquid disappears and the paste looks glossy.
4. Add the **scallion for the filling** and the **ginger** together. Stir until they are even through the meat.

## Buns

Once the dough has doubled, shape 8 small buns and close each top so the juice stays inside.

- 1 scallion, minced, for the top
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour, for the counter

**Dough**

- 1 cup all-purpose flour, for the dough, risen
- 1/2 tsp sugar, risen
- 1/2 tsp instant yeast, risen
- 5 tbsp lukewarm water, risen

**Filling**

- 1 scallion, minced, for the filling, mixed
- 1 tsp ginger, minced, mixed
- 4 oz ground pork, mixed
- 1 tsp light soy sauce (生抽), mixed
- 1/4 tsp toasted sesame oil (香油), for the filling, mixed
- 1/4 tsp kosher salt, Diamond Crystal, mixed
- 1/8 tsp ground white pepper, mixed
- 2 tbsp cold water, for the pork, mixed

1. Mince the **scallion for the top**.
2. Scatter the **all-purpose flour for the counter** on the counter.
3. Knead the risen dough on the counter until it shrinks back and feels smooth, about 1 minute.
4. Roll it into a log. Cut the log into 8 equal pieces. Cover the pieces you are not rolling.
5. Divide the mixed filling into 8 even portions.
6. Roll one piece into a 3 in round, thicker in the center than at the edge. If it sticks, dust it with the **all-purpose flour for the counter**.
7. Set one portion in the center of the round.
8. Pleat the edge up and over the filling, turning the round as you pinch, until the top is closed. Twist the top shut. There should be no hole.
9. Set the bun pleat side up. Cover it. Repeat with the remaining pieces.
10. Leave the covered buns until they look a little puffy, about 15 minutes.

## Fry

- 1 tbsp peanut oil
- 1 cup all-purpose flour, for the dough, shaped into 8 buns
- 3/4 cup water, for the skillet
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds (白芝麻), toasted
- 1 scallion, minced, for the top

1. Put the **peanut oil** in a cold 10 in heavy skillet.
2. Set the **all-purpose flour for the dough** in, shaped into 8 buns, pleat side up, with space between them.
3. Set the skillet on medium. Fry without moving the buns until the bottoms are pale gold, about 8 minutes. If a bottom smells sharp, pull the skillet fully off and turn the coil off. When the smell sweetens, turn the coil back to medium and set the skillet fully back on.
4. Hold the lid in front of the skillet. Pour in the **water for the skillet** and cover at once. The oil will spit.
5. Cook until the water is gone and the tops look set and puffed, about 8 minutes. If the bottoms smell sharp, pull the skillet fully off and turn the coil off. When the smell sweetens, turn the coil back to medium and set the skillet fully back on.
6. Take the lid off. Scatter the **white sesame seeds** and the **scallion for the top** over the buns. Cover until the scallion smells sweet, about 1 minute.
7. Take the lid off. Cook until the bottoms are deep golden and the skillet sounds dry, about 1 minute. If a bun sticks, leave it 30 seconds and lift again.
8. Lift the buns out, crisp side up. The center is hot and juicy. Eat them hot.
